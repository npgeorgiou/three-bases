ifelse AI

An AI that sees both hands and searches the real position, with no rules of thumb: every judgement is a weighted term. All its values (weights, how far and how wide it looks, switches) are tuned at once with CMA-ES against the opponents you tick, starting from the Benchmark's values. Each generation, several candidates around a centre (every value changed by a different amount) play the same set of games; the centre moves toward the better half and the method learns which values, and which combinations, pay off. It restarts by itself with more candidates when it stops improving. Draws count half.

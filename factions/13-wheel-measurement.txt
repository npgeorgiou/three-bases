==================================================================
13. THE WHEEL MEASUREMENT: how the radii are measured, and how to redo it (9 Nov 2026)
==================================================================
What it is: `library/cards_db/axes.py` measures every real faction of the library (10+ cards; 43 factions of 8 games on 9 Nov 2026; Monster Train is left out, NOT_MEASURED in axes.py: Kaptain, 9 Nov
2026, a different kind of game; Marvel Snap has no factions)
on the radii of factions/12 and writes `library/cards_db/axes.tsv` and `factions/wheel-axes.html` (page template
`tools/wheel_axes_template.html`; never edit the built page). The page: the circle of radii with factions to tick on and
off, the map of factions (the two directions in which factions differ most), the numbers table, the closest factions,
and a switch between all cards and cards in top decks.

How a value is made (all agreed with Kaptain, 9 Nov 2026):
- Each end of an axis is its own radius: one number per axis (one end minus the other) hid factions strong at both ends
  (Faeria Red has the most damage from its units' text and also strikes). Axes 4, 5 and 8 have a radius for their first end only: the
  other end (units on the board now, works alone, plays what it draws) is a low value on that radius.
- A card counts for a radius if it has one of the radius's concepts (SPOKES in axes.py; shown on hover in the page).
  "concept@game,game" counts only in those games, because a concept means something else elsewhere (heal is the
  player's life in Faeria and Slay the Spire; "position" outside Gwent, Runeterra and Marvel Snap is area hits and
  pushes).
- Damage (Kaptain, 10 Nov 2026: "fixed damage" counted the wrong things and spells had no radius). The old radius went
  by marks that differ per game, missed 158 of Gwent's 259 damage cards, shared half its cards with "kills them" and
  counted cards that destroy everything. Now three radii that share no mark: "damage from units' text" (a unit,
  building, item or weapon whose own text deals damage to an enemy), "damage from spells" (a spell that does), both
  read from the card's rows (DAMAGE_ON_ENEMY in axes.py; a unit's strike is no row), and "kills outright" (destroy,
  banish, removal, deathtouch: no damage).
- "Rewards playing spells": cards that react when a spell is played, count the spells played, or make spells
  stronger (the last only in Duelyst and Runeterra; in Slay the Spire that mark is on 131 cards and was not checked).
  A plain share of spells is no radius: inside a game the factions have nearly the same share (Duelyst 40-46%,
  Runeterra 34-44%, Gwent 10-18%).
- Growth (more strength, more defence on its own units) counts for "units' own strikes" and for "keeps them alive" in
  every game with units; in Gwent the one number grows, so its growth counts for both (the rule of axis 9).
- "One chosen" is read from the card's rows in every game: an effect on one chosen unit on the board (chosen* in
  axes.py). The library's mark for a chosen target exists almost only in Runeterra.
- "Breaks their plan" also counts an enemy unit that can't attack or can't move (CANT in axes.py). Kaptain, 9 Nov 2026:
  "can't block" does not count. "Can't strike" does not either: it is Inscryption's Waterborne, which protects the unit.
- "Many at once" also counts a card whose amount is the number of units on the board (countboard* in axes.py).
- Position in every game: a card's effect or condition on its OWN units beside, around or behind it (BESIDE in axes.py,
  read from the side written in the card's rows, so area hits and pushes on enemies stay out). Gwent, Runeterra and
  Marvel Snap also count the POSITION concepts. A game with under 10 such cards measures combos only.
- A faction's value = its share of cards that count, divided by its game's average share (0 = none, 1 = average), so
  games that mark more concepts per card don't stand out.
- Unit stats are numbers, not concepts: strength and defence per supply (per unit where a game's units mostly have no
  cost). unit_stats() reads each game's own fields: Stormbound's one number counts as both; Gwent's power is strength and
  power + armour is defence; Into the Breach has only defence (its damage is in weapons). A game paid in several kinds of
  supply (SUPPLIES: Inscryption's Blood, Energy, Bones, Mox) has each unit's cost divided by the average cost of its
  kind, so 4 Bones is not counted as 4 Blood.
- Width: a cheap unit and a big unit are a unit up to, or from, the cost that cuts nearest to a third of its game's
  units (when most units share one cost, cheap is under it and big is over it). Spells never count as big units.
- Lean = the strength value minus the defence value (axes.tsv, and the last column of the Numbers table).
- "Position and combos" = the average of the position value and the combos value (the share of the faction's card pairs
  that links.sqlite links).
- Smoothing (Kaptain: shapes looked like hedgehogs, then too flat): each faction counts as if it had SHRINK (5) more
  cards at its game's average (not for unit stats and combos), then values are raised to POWER (0.75): 4 times the
  average shows as 2.8.
- A game with under 10 cards for a radius gives its factions no value there (a dashed dot on the rim with its reason).
  With 5, two cards of a small game gave one of the highest values of the wheel (Inscryption Beast, breaks their plan).
  10 Nov 2026, with 24 radii: Duelyst 1 gap, Gwent 1, Faeria 4, Inscryption 10, Stormbound 11, Slay the Spire 11 (it
  has no units), Into the Breach 16. Read the small games' shapes with care. The page shows each gap with its reason.
- Two counts: all cards; or each card weighted by how many decks of decks.tsv play it (only factions with 10+ cards in
  decks). Deck lists hold each card once, without copies.
- 24 radii in 8 areas (Kaptain, 9 Nov 2026: broad radii hid how factions differ, e.g. Freljord sets enemy strength to 0
  and Noxus switches enemies off, both were "breaks their plan"; so five radii are split where most games have 10+
  cards per part, and neighbours form areas drawn as coloured arcs; the area names are Claude's). In circle order:
  Fighting: strength, grows strength, strikes more or first. Damage: damage from units' text, kills outright, many at
  once. Spells: damage from spells, rewards playing spells.
  Control: stops them (switch off, silence, can't attack, can't move), breaks their plan in other ways, one chosen.
  Cards and supply: more supply, cheaper or stronger hand, more cards, picks and copies, brings back.
  Many units and dying: many cheap units, spends them. Together: position and combos.
  Staying alive: heals them, grows defence, protects them, defence, a few big units.
  Not split, too few games with 10+ cards: "weakens enemy units" (3 games), "hurts its own units" (2 games).
- Radii that work together (Kaptain, 10 Nov 2026: the wheel must be a base for 20 factions with overlaps, so its radii
  must be things that can overlap and act on each other). The page's fourth view and `radius_links` in axes.py: for
  every two radii, how much more often than chance the library links a card of one with a card of the other in the
  same game (1 = chance; a number only when chance alone would give 20 linked pairs). On 10 Nov 2026 every pair of
  neighbours on the circle is at chance or above (0.93 to 4.5), so the circle order holds. Highest: damage from spells
  with rewards playing spells 4.5, brings back with a few big units 2.4, brings back with spends them 2.4, kills
  outright with spends them 2.2. Lowest: kills outright with stops them 0.56, damage from spells with stops them 0.58.
  "Rewards playing spells" is high with nearly everything, because such a card links to every spell. Strength,
  defence and "position and combos" are numbers, not cards, and are not in the table: they shape a faction but are
  not mechanics two factions can share.
  "Cards in top decks" (Kaptain, 10 Nov 2026: how often radii appear in the same real decks, linked or not; the
  earlier version counted library links inside decks and every value came out above 1): per deck, the share of its
  cards in each radius; for radii A and B, the average of (A share x B share) over a game's decks divided by (average
  A share x average B share), then the average over games (each game counts the same; a game counts only when 5 of its
  decks hold both). 1 = usual, 2 = decks heavy in one hold twice as much of the other; usual comes from the real decks,
  so a radius every deck plays does not look combined with everything. All 1,410 decks of 9 games (Marvel Snap
  included; Monster Train is not on the page). On 10 Nov 2026: middle value 0.97, 0.42 to 1.99; highest cheaper with
  picks 1.99, stops with supply 1.66; lowest spellpay with spends 0.42.
- Decks are read with `deck_analysis.parse_deck` (Kaptain, 10 Nov 2026: every deck and card must count). Splitting
  the card list on commas lost every card with a comma in its name ("Seifer, Blood Tyrant"): 294 of Faeria's 1177
  deck cards, 60 of Runeterra's.
- A planned shape: `python3 axes.py --near "heals=1.6,stops=0.3"` lists the 10 real factions closest to the values
  given, over the radii named (keys: the first word of each SPOKES line in axes.py).
- Open (Kaptain, 9 Nov 2026, undecided): a value is against the faction's own game, so across games it is loose
  ("spends them": Melting Remnant 63% of cards showed 2.4, Shadow Isles 44% showed 2.9). Claude proposed one value
  against all factions of all games; Kaptain thinks the answer may be a more accurate mapping of cards to axes.

Redo it (after a new game, changed library cards, changed concepts or links). From library/cards_db:
1. `python3 links.py` (rebuilds links.sqlite, not committed; about 2 minutes, Runeterra alone 9).
2. `python3 axes.py`. It prints "CHECK FAILED" if one of Kaptain's points breaks (CHECKS, GAPS and HAS in axes.py; add
   one for every point he raises, e.g. Faeria Red high on damage from units' text and on strikes).
3. Check what each concept catches: `python3 axes.py --cards <file>` writes, per radius, concept and game, how many cards
   it catches and 4 of them with their text. An agent reads the file (the main chat never reads card texts) and answers
   per concept: keep, drop, or count only in some games; plus concepts that are missing. Change SPOKES and rerun.
   Lesson (Kaptain found Faeria Red and Runeterra wrong): concepts chosen by their names, unchecked, were wrong in many
   games.
4. Check the zeros: list every faction value under 0.05; an agent reads all that faction's cards (name, text, concepts)
   and says per case whether 0 is real or the library missed a mark. A missed mark is a library error: write it in
   todo.md for a database chat, never patch it in axes.py.
5. A new game: add its stat fields to unit_stats(); check every radius's game lists (step 3); look at its factions on the
   circle with Kaptain.
6. Look at the page; send Kaptain only the page.
Kept apart / together: `python3 apart.py [top]` correlates concept shares over all factions (centred per game).

Not kept: numbers quoted by an earlier chat (39 factions, "six axes explain half") had no saved script; this script
gives Faeria Yellow 3.1 and Red 4.8 average cost as quoted, Runeterra Shurima 3.0 / Freljord 4.3 (quoted 2.9 / 4.1).
Dropped measures: early or late (right in only 63 of 149 Faeria deck pairs named aggro/midrange/control); sets the pace
or answers (wrong order in Runeterra).

The roster: map

How to read it

Each dot is a concept. Two dots sit close when real decks hold both more often than chance. With no faction ticked, a dot takes the colour of its group: concepts real decks play together (listed above); grey dots are in no group and sit beside the concepts they are played with most. Tick factions to light their concepts: big dots are the kernel, middle dots the other concepts, small dots the extras; lines, in the faction's colour, are strong card links between its concepts; its deck style is what its decks are like (many spells, few big units). Drag to move, wheel to zoom, hover a dot for its name.

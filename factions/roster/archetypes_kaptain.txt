Macro-archetypes

Aggro: cheap, efficient threats that win before the opponent stabilizes.
Control: answers everything, then wins late with a few finishers.
Midrange: resilient, high-value threats plus flexible removal.
Tempo: cheap threats protected by cheap disruption.
Aggro-Combo: early pressure with a sudden combo kill.
Prison / Stax: locks opponents out through asymmetric restrictions.
Draw-Go / Flash: holds up mana and acts only at instant speed.

Creature & combat

Tokens / Go-Wide: many small bodies plus anthem effects.
Tribal / Typal: synergies built around one creature type.
Voltron: piles buffs onto one unit.
+1/+1 Counters: grows and multiplies counters.
Extra Combats: multiple combat phases per turn.
Defenders / Toughness Matters: walls that deal damage equal to their toughness.
Toolbox: tutors the right creature for each situation.

Graveyard & sacrifice

Aristocrats: sacrifice outlets plus death triggers.
Reanimator: gets big creatures into the graveyard, then returns them cheaply.
Dredge / Self-Mill: fills the discard pile to fuel engines.
Delirium / Threshold: fills the graveyard to unlock bonuses.
Graveyard Recursion: replays cards from the graveyard.
Madness: discarding cards pays off.

Spells & engines

Spellslinger / Prowess: many cheap spells to trigger payoffs.
Storm: rewards many cards played in one turn.
Burn: direct damage to the face.
Blink / Flicker: re-triggers ETB abilities.
Cascade / Discover: free cards from a pool.
Clone / Copy: copies the best permanents and spells.
Cheat-into-Play: puts huge threats onto the battlefield without paying for them.
Eggs: loops cheap units that die for value.

Resources, land & life

Ramp: fast mana into big spells.
Stompy: Ramp into oversized creatures.
Lifegain / Drain: gains life and converts it into a win.
Life-as-Resource: pays life to power cheap threats.
Mill: empties the opponent’s library.
Discard / 8-Rack: empties hands and punishes having none.
Rosters: mass draw-seven effects plus draw and discard payoffs; swaps unwanted cards for new ones.

Defensive, multiplayer & alt-win

Pillowfort: deters or taxes attacks against you.
Turbo-Fog: repeated Fogs to stall combat.
Hatebears / Death & Taxes: disruptive small creatures plus mana denial.
Theft: steals opponents’ cards.
Aikido / Judo: turns the opponent’s own damage and spells against them.
Chaos: randomizes the game to disrupt plans.
Alternate Win: “you win the game” cards.

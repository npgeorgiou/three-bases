# Stop 1, part 4: M N O P
See `STOP1.md` for what the ratings mean.

## M: Enemy converter
- Strong in: breaks their plan 1.9, cheaper hand 1.3, more cards 1.4.
- Builds on: turn an enemy into another unit; take an enemy unit or card (NEW RULE). Extras: place my units in the enemy camp (NEW RULE); my unit strikes a chosen enemy; pick cards from my deck; round start effects.
- On the board: Place: turn the enemy in the facing front cell into a 1-cell unit with 0 strength and 1 supply -> a wall that held two or three dots now holds one and its lane loses income.
- On the board: Round end: turn every enemy in one row of this lane into a 0-strength unit. Why: the row still holds dots but cannot strike when the next round begins, so I take the first move.
- On the board: Place: take control of the enemy in the facing front cell; it stays in its cell as mine -> a two-dot swing from one action, his blocker is gone and my dot stands there. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: the enemy camp fills with harmless bodies: every unit of mine turns an enemy into a 1-cell 0-strength unit, so his camp holds dots with units that cannot strike
- Wins: I turn his units into harmless bodies or take them, so his camp works for me
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Place: take control of the enemy in the facing front cell; it stays in its cell as mine, a two-dot swing (NEW RULE: a stolen unit stays in the enemy front cell)
- Removes back-row units: Place: put this unit in an empty back-row cell of the enemy camp, from where it strikes his back row directly (NEW RULE: a unit is placed in the enemy camp)
- Between lanes: A unit I take from the enemy goes into my camp in the lane I choose (NEW RULE: a stolen unit moves to my camp)
- Holds: Round end: turn every enemy in one row of this lane into a 0-strength unit, so none of them can attack next round
- Feels good: Place: turn the enemy in the facing front cell into a 1-cell unit with 0 strength and 1 supply
- Never has: a unit worth more dead than alive

- A OK: M's Place turns or takes the enemy in the facing front cell; A's revive brings the unit back and its Place does it to the next enemy, so one dead unit clears two cells. The enemy can leave the unit alone.
- B OK: M takes an enemy unit and B then lets it die for supply, so his unit is gone and I gain its supply. The enemy can kill the stolen unit with his one action; steal is a new rule.
- C OK: M takes an enemy unit and C lets it die to hit another enemy, so his own unit is my bomb. The enemy can kill the stolen unit and steal is a new rule.
- E OK: M turns the enemy front blocker into a 1-cell 0-strength body; one small E damage spell then clears its dot. The enemy cannot strike with it but can fill another cell.
- F OK: M turns his front blocker into a 1-defence body, then my F strike kills it and the rest of the hit goes to the unit behind it.
- G OK: M makes an enemy strike my G unit; it survives, gains strength and hits back at the unit facing it, so the enemy loses a unit by its own strike.
- H OK: M's unit takes control of the enemy in the facing front cell; H takes it back and placing it again takes another front unit; the enemy answers between.
- I OK: M takes control of the enemy's 5+ strength unit in the facing front cell, which turns on my big-strength cards and takes his dot; the enemy can't undo it.
- J OK: The copy of a unit whose Place takes the facing enemy stands in the lane beside and takes the front unit there too; two dot swings from one deploy.
- K OK: My unit whose Place takes the facing front enemy goes back to hand with a K card, then is placed again to take the next one. The first steal already swung two dots, and the enemy has one action to refill.
- L OK: M's Place makes my L unit strike a chosen enemy out of reach; if it kills it grows or is readied by its own text, so one action kills and builds a bigger unit. The enemy can only answer afterwards.
- N OK: My unit whose Place takes a weak enemy goes back to hand by an N card and is placed again to take another. The first steal already took a dot.
- O OK: An O unit placed in the enemy camp puts small free units on the cells beside it, so he loses several cells and the tokens block his rebuilding. He must kill them one by one.
- P OK: M's Place makes my unit strike a chosen enemy; P's ready on kill lets it strike again, so one unit kills two enemies.
- Q OK: Q drags his best back unit into the front cell facing mine and an M Place takes it or changes it into a harmless body. He can move the pulled unit away between.
- R OK: M makes my R unit strike a chosen enemy; it kills, grows for good, and holds its dot. The enemy can only answer afterwards.
- S OK: A unit taken from his front cell fills a cell in my camp, so all my count units grow by one and his front is open; two cells swing. Needs the steal rule.
- T OK: I take his big unit with M's steal and sacrifice it with T's offering for its supply or a card; his strongest unit becomes my supply. He can strike the stolen unit between.

## N: Hidden shield wall
- Strong in: protects them 1.8, spends them 1.2, defence 1.5.
- Builds on: shields; my unit can't be targeted. Extras: my units grow from deaths; my unit guards the others; my unit goes back to hand and is placed again.
- On the board: Round end: give a shield to every unit of mine in the front row -> every dot I hold survives the first hit of the next round. Why: the enemy needs two attacks, each costing supply and an action, to clear one front cell.
- On the board: Hide: this unit can't be targeted until it attacks, then it shows until round end -> a unit that never attacks holds a dot the enemy cannot strike, he can only cancel it by standing opposite.
- On the board: This unit can't be the target of enemy spells or abilities (strikes still hit) -> kill, silence, change and steal do not work on it. Why: the units that hold my dots can be removed only by a strike.
- Faction idea: hidden walls: every front-row unit is hidden while it has not attacked and has strength only on Place or round end, so the base is held by units the enemy cannot target
- Wins: my dots cannot be removed: shielded, hidden units make him spend two actions for each cell
- Removes the unit facing my dot: My dot holder cannot be targeted until it attacks, so he can only cancel its dot by placing a unit opposite, which my hidden unit then strikes
- Removes back-row units: It does not remove them: its hidden units cannot be targeted, so his reach is wasted on my shielded front until he pushes into my dots
- Between lanes: Place: give a shield to my unit in the same cell of every lane, so one deploy shields the same cell in all three bases
- Holds: Round end: give a shield to every unit of mine in the front row, so every dot survives the first hit of next round
- Feels good: Place: this unit cannot be the target of enemy spells or abilities, so kill, silence, change and steal fail on it
- Never has: small fixed hits, often or many times

Combos with 16 factions (2 strong):
- L STRONG: The L guard in a front cell takes every enemy strike in reach, and N's round-end shield on the front row means each strike is wasted on a shielded wall. Neither half needs a named card and one enemy action cannot remove both.

- A OK: When my unit dies, N grows the others; A brings it back free and it can die again, so N grows twice for one card. The enemy chooses whether to kill it a second time.
- B OK: A shield lets a B unit survive a hit and the hurt effect makes it grow, so the same wall grows each round. The enemy can break the shield and kill it.
- C OK: C damages my own shielded unit so the shield takes the hit and its hurt effect acts without losing defence. The enemy can break the shield.
- F OK: N's guard takes the strikes; F's round-end heal restores it for the next round.
- G OK: An N guard that is hit and lives grows and hits back; the enemy must kill it in one strike, and the rest of N's units stay safe behind it.
- H OK: H takes back an N unit whose Place shields my unit in each lane, and placing it again shields them again after the enemy broke the shields.
- I OK: A hidden or shielded 5+ strength unit stays on the board, so the big-strength cards stay on; the enemy needs two strikes or a way around.
- J OK: A copy of a hidden unit holds a front cell in the lane beside, so two dots are held by units the enemy cannot target.
- M OK: My unit whose Place takes a weak enemy goes back to hand by an N card and is placed again to take another. The first steal already took a dot.
- P OK: A shielded unit that cancels dots in two lanes survives the first hit, so both dots stay next round.
- Q OK: A Q unit with a Place hit stands in my camp; an N card takes it back and I place it again to hit again.
- R OK: My unit kills the facing enemy and grows; the enemy kills it back; N's deathfeed units grow because a grown unit died, so each trade feeds both.
- S OK: Shielded front units survive the first hit, so my camp stays full and S's count units keep their size.
- T OK: T lets my own unit die for supply or a card, and the stack behind it grows for good; one sacrifice pays twice.

## O: Death payback
- Strong in: many cheap units 1.9, position and combos 1.5, spends them 1.2.
- Builds on: Death effects; extra small units. Extras: I damage my own units; my units react when one leaves; cards that count my units; units beside each other give each other more.
- On the board: Place: put small 1-cell units on two free cells next to this unit, they cost no supply -> one action fills a whole front row and holds all three dots. Why: one action and one cost buys three bodies.
- On the board: Death: the unit that struck this unit takes fixed damage equal to its strength, wherever it stands -> killing a dot holder costs the attacker his unit. Why: the enemy that clears my front cell loses the unit that cancels my dot.
- Faction idea: every unit is a 1-cell unit with defence 1 and a Death text: nine bodies fill the camp, every hit kills one, and each death hurts the enemy or fills a cell
- Wins: my small units die and each death hurts him or fills a cell, so killing them costs him
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Death: the unit that struck this unit takes fixed damage equal to its strength, wherever it stands
- Removes back-row units: Death: fixed damage to the nearest enemy in this unit's column, however deep, so his back row is hit when my front unit dies
- Between lanes: Death: fixed damage to the nearest enemy in the same column of the lane beside (NEW RULE: damage crosses lanes)
- Holds: Nine cheap small bodies fill the camp, every hit kills only one, and the front is never empty
- Feels good: Place: put small 1-cell units on two free cells next to this unit for no supply
- Never has: shields that cancel the next hit

## P: Twin-lane actor
- Strong in: a few big units 1.7, spends them 1.4, strikes more 1.3, strength 1.4.
- Builds on: my cards act in another lane; my unit acts again. Extras: my attacks do something extra; my units react when one leaves; I push enemy units; my unit guards the others.
- On the board: A unit in a front cell also cancels the enemy dot in the same column of the lane beside it -> one unit cancels two dots in two lanes. Example: my front-left unit in lane 1 also cancels the enemy's left dot in lane 2. Why: one front unit works on two bases. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Ready on kill: when my unit kills an enemy, it may attack again this round, paying its supply again -> one unit clears two cells. Why: I hold one dot and clear the next in one round with a single unit. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: A unit that did not move this round may attack twice -> a unit holding its front cell does two units' work and cancels two enemy dots. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: the three front-middle units are the whole army: one big unit per lane, each reaches into the lanes beside it and acts twice, so one unit equals two units' work
- Wins: one unit works in two lanes and attacks twice
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Ready on kill: when my unit kills an enemy it may attack again this round, paying its supply again (NEW RULE: a second attack in a round)
- Removes back-row units: Place: ready all my Ranged units so they may attack again, so a group of them hits the back rows twice in one round (NEW RULE: a second attack in a round)
- Between lanes: A unit's reach crosses to the same cell of the lane beside, so its strike works in two lanes (NEW RULE: reach crosses lanes)
- Holds: A unit that did not move this round may attack twice, so a unit holding its front cell does two units' work (NEW RULE: a second attack in a round)
- Feels good: Place: this unit's dot also cancels the enemy dot in the same column of the lane beside (NEW RULE: a unit's dot works in the lane beside)
- Never has: copying my own cards

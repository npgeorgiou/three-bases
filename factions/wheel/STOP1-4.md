# Stop 1, part 4: R N P C
See `STOP1.md` for what the ratings mean.

## R: riders
- Strong in: brings back 1.5, grows strength 1.6, grows defence 1.4.
- Builds on: my unit can't be targeted; attach a unit to a carrier (NEW RULE). Extras: my unit goes back to hand and is placed again; my dead units come back; cards that count my units; the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind.
- On the board: Place onto an ally in my lane (I take no cell): it gains my strength and defence; when it dies I drop into its cell, so the cell needs two kills (NEW RULE: a unit with no cell, on another unit).
- On the board: I can't be attacked or targeted until I attack or move: in a front cell I hold the dot, and the enemy can only cancel it by facing me (NEW RULE: can't be targeted).
- On the board: A rider adds its supply to the carrier's lane each round and moves with the carrier for free (NEW RULE: rider supply and free moves).
- Faction idea: every back-row unit rides its column: the ally directly in front has its keywords and half its strength (faeria: Majinata + CLAUDE).
- Wins: units ride on others: one front cell holds two units, and riders can't be hit.
- Removes: a carrier strikes with its riders' strength (NEW RULE).
- Holds: when the carrier dies its rider drops into the cell, so the cell needs two kills.
- Feels good: Place onto an ally: it gets my strength and defence.
- Never has: ping.

## N: guard and shield
- Strong in: protects them 1.8, spends them 1.2, defence 1.5.
- Builds on: shields; my unit guards the others. Extras: my units grow from deaths; my unit can't be targeted; my unit goes back to hand and is placed again.
- On the board: Round start: my front-row units gain a shield; dot holders survive the first hit every round.
- On the board: Enemies that reach me must strike me, not the nearest: in the front-middle cell I take the hits meant for both side holders (NEW RULE: changes the nearest-target rule).
- Faction idea: every unit Immobile, but every front unit starts each round shielded: a wall that never moves and never breaks on the first hit (Kaptain's all-Immobile idea + CLAUDE).
- Wins: a wall that never moves and never breaks on the first hit.
- Removes: a shielded guard strikes back at whatever hit it.
- Holds: round start: every front unit gains a shield.
- Feels good: Place: the ally in front of me gets a shield.
- Never has: movement.

## P: huge units
- Strong in: a few big units 1.7, spends them 1.4, strikes more or first 1.3, strength 1.4.
- Builds on: the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; units with strength 5+. Extras: strike twice or first; Death effects; my unit strikes a chosen enemy; I push enemy units.
- On the board: My strike damage beyond the target's defence hits the next enemy behind it in its column: the holder and its replacement.
- On the board: My front unit with 5+ strength holds its dot even with an enemy facing it, unless that enemy also has 5+ (NEW RULE: changes the dot rule).
- On the board: When my ally with 5+ strength dies, draw a unit with 5+ strength: losing a big holder brings the next.
- Faction idea: every unit is a line of 2, line of 3 or 2x2: few huge units, each holding two or three front cells (CLAUDE).
- Wins: few huge units each hold two or three front cells, and their hits go through to the unit behind.
- Removes: the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind.
- Holds: a front unit with 5+ strength keeps its dot even with an enemy facing it (NEW RULE).
- Feels good: One hit kills the holder and wounds the unit waiting behind it.
- Never has: cheaper cards.

## C: row hits
- Strong in: many at once 1.8, spends them 1.4, kills outright 1.2.
- Builds on: hits on a whole row or area; my units grow from deaths. Extras: my unit dies for supply, a card or strength; destroy outright; I damage my own units; my unit dies and takes an enemy with it.
- On the board: A line-of-3 unit in my front row: Place: 1 damage to every enemy in its three columns; one wide unit holds 3 dots and hits all 3 cancellers.
- On the board: Spell: 1 damage to every enemy in a row, +1 for each of my units that died this round; my losses make the next hit bigger.
- On the board: When my front unit dies, my unit directly behind it gains +1|+1 and steps into its cell at once; the dot is held again by a bigger unit (NEW RULE: a move without using the unit's move).
- Faction idea: my area hits also hit my own units in that row, and every death of mine feeds the others (duelyst: Glacial Fissure + CLAUDE).
- Wins: row hits clear his front, and every death of mine pushes a bigger unit forward.
- Removes: a row hit, bigger for each of my units that died this round.
- Holds: when my front unit dies the unit behind gains +1|+1 and steps into its cell.
- Feels good: Place a line of 3 in my front row: 1 damage to all three facing cells.
- Never has: one chosen.

Each: what it does, "I do X -> I gain from X". Cards = real cards the library has for it; biggest = the real faction
with the most. SMALL = under 30 in every real faction: still allowed as a faction's base. NEW = added now, CHANGED = fixed
now (agents read 8 real cards each; "right" = how many of 8 really are it). Dropped: "strike twice or first" (an attacked
unit doesn't hit back here; striking twice is now "acts again"), "lock an enemy for good" (the library has only
one-round stuns), "decide who acts first" (no library cards). Full sentences and concepts: `mechanics.tsv`.

## M: change and junk
- Strong in: breaks their plan 1.9, cheaper hand 1.3, more cards 1.4.
- Builds on: turn an enemy into another unit; useless cards and units for the enemy. Extras: create new cards; discard my cards for an effect; pick cards from my deck; round start effects.
- On the board: Place: the enemy facing me, if its strength is 2 or less, becomes yours and stays in its cell: his dot becomes yours.
- On the board: Place: a 0|1 Immobile Egg of the enemy fills the free front cell facing me; it holds and cancels no dot, so my dot stands (NEW RULE: a unit that holds and cancels no dot).
- On the board: Death: a 0|1 Immobile unit of the enemy fills a free cell of his camp in this lane; his big shapes have no room (NEW RULE: giving the enemy units).
- Faction idea: my units can be placed into free cells of the enemy camp: they hold nothing but block his front cells (duelyst: White Asp + CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: I turn his units and cells into mine or into useless ones.
- Removes: Place: the weak enemy facing me becomes yours, in its own cell.
- Holds: a junk Egg of his fills the cell facing my dot: it cancels nothing and blocks his real holders.
- Feels good: Place: I steal the enemy facing me, and his dot becomes yours.
- Never has: +strength.

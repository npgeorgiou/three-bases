## E: Spell taxman
- Strong in: damage from spells 1.9, cheaper hand 1.4, rewards playing spells 1.3.
- Builds on: damage spells; cheaper cards. Extras: spells make my cards stronger; units with strength 5+; act on the enemy's hand and costs.
- On the board: Spell: fixed damage to the enemy unit in one front cell I choose, my own strike not used -> the enemy dot is gone and mine stands. Why: one spell takes a dot back without a unit and without moving.
- On the board: Place: the enemy's next unit and spell cost +1 supply until the end of this round, in the lane of this unit. Why: he may not be able to refill a lane in time. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: the faction taxes the enemy: every spell I cast makes his next card cost 1 more this round, and the units are small because the cards that matter are spells
- Wins: spells from my hand remove his units while his next cards cost more, so he cannot refill
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Spell: fixed damage to the enemy in one front cell I choose, without using my strike, so his dot is gone and mine stands
- Removes back-row units: A damage spell chooses any one enemy in the lane, even in the back row
- Between lanes: A damage spell aims at an enemy in another lane and is paid from carry-over (NEW RULE: a spell aims at another lane)
- Holds: Every spell I cast makes his next card cost 1 more this round, so he cannot refill the cell I cleared (NEW RULE: my spell raises the enemy's costs)
- Feels good: Place: the enemy's next unit and spell cost +1 supply until round end in this lane (NEW RULE: my effect raises the enemy's costs)
- Never has: extra small units put on the grid

## F: Packed wall
- Strong in: heals them 1.8, grows strength 1.3, grows defence 1.5, defence 1.3.
- Builds on: units beside each other give each other more; heal my units. Extras: cards that count my units; the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; my unit grows when it is hit and survives; shields.
- On the board: Each of my units gains +1 defence for every friend directly behind it in its column (up to 2), so a full column of three has a front unit with +2. Why: a backed-up front unit is hard to kill and its dot stays mine.
- On the board: If I hold all 3 front cells of a lane, every front unit there gains +1 strength. Why: filling all three dots makes the whole front stronger, so it is hard to knock one dot loose.
- On the board: Round end: every friend around this unit gets 2 defence back. Why: a healer in the middle row refills the front units after the round, when the enemy cannot answer, so wounds do not carry the dot to the next round.
- Faction idea: marked cells: the faction marks cells of its camp, and every bonus reads the marked cells, so the front is wherever it marked, and a unit that ends the round on a marked cell is whole
- Wins: units stand close and heal, so my front is too thick to break
- Removes the unit facing my dot: If I hold all three front cells of a lane, every front unit there gains +1 strength and wins the strike against the unit facing it
- Removes back-row units: It mainly does not: its backed-up units win every front trade, and the back rows fall only when the front is gone
- Between lanes: Round end: restore a wounded friend in any lane, so a lane that was beaten down is fixed from the lane that was not
- Holds: Each unit gains +1 defence for every friend directly behind it in its column (up to 2)
- Feels good: Place: at every round end, every friend around this unit gets 2 defence back
- Never has: taking an enemy unit for myself

## H: Card recycler
- Strong in: brings back 1.7, damage from units' text 1.5, more cards 1.2.
- Builds on: spent cards come back from the discard pile; draw extra cards. Extras: my unit goes back to hand and is placed again; my dead units come back; I push enemy units; cheaper cards.
- On the board: Death: this unit's card goes back into my deck instead of the discard pile -> the unit that just died holding a dot comes round again in my draws. Why: the same units refill the same front cells every few rounds.
- On the board: Round end: one unit from my discard pile goes on top of my deck, so one of my 2 draws next round is the unit I choose. Why: the unit that just died holding a dot is back as soon as the next round starts.
- On the board: Place: draw a card; if a friend is beside this unit, draw two. Why: with more cards I always have a unit that fits an empty front cell and the supply left in that lane.
- Faction idea: the discard pile is a second hand: the faction fills its front cells with cheap bodies it brings back each round, and wins by cards fired from the back rows
- Wins: my dead units go back into my deck, so the same bodies refill the same cells round after round
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Round end: one unit from my discard pile goes on top of my deck, so first thing next round I place a unit whose Place hit clears the facing cell
- Removes back-row units: The top-of-deck unit's Place hit clears one front cell first, then the back rows step up and are hit the next round
- Between lanes: The unit on top of my deck costs 1 less when I place it in a lane other than the one it died in
- Holds: Death: this unit's card goes back into my deck, so the unit that died holding a dot comes round again
- Feels good: Place: draw a card; if a friend is beside this unit, draw two
- Never has: destroying an enemy outright, whatever its defence

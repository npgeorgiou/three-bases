Macro-archetypes

Aggro: cheap, efficient threats that win before the opponent stabilizes.
Control: answers everything, then wins late with a few finishers.
Midrange: resilient, high-value threats plus flexible removal.
Tempo: cheap threats protected by cheap disruption.
Combo: assembles specific cards to win instantly or go infinite.
Aggro-Combo: early pressure with a sudden combo kill.
Control-Combo: controls the early game, then wins with a combo.
Prison / Stax: locks opponents out through asymmetric restrictions.
Draw-Go / Flash: holds up mana and acts only at instant speed.

Creature & combat

Tokens / Go-Wide: many small bodies plus anthem effects.
Tribal / Typal: synergies built around one creature type.
Voltron: piles Auras and Equipment onto one creature.
Hexproof Auras (Bogles): Voltron on an untargetable creature.
+1/+1 Counters: grows and multiplies counters.
Infect / Toxic / Poison: wins with 10 poison counters.
Extra Combats: multiple combat phases per turn.
Defenders / Toughness Matters: walls that deal damage equal to their toughness.
Mutate: stacks creatures to combine their abilities.
Ninjutsu: swaps unblocked attackers for Ninjas.
Face-down (Morph / Disguise): cheap face-down creatures that flip for value.
Toolbox: tutors the right creature for each situation.

Graveyard & sacrifice

Aristocrats: sacrifice outlets plus death triggers.
Reanimator: gets big creatures into the graveyard, then returns them cheaply.
Dredge / Self-Mill: fills the graveyard to fuel free engines.
Delirium / Threshold: fills the graveyard to unlock bonuses.
Graveyard Recursion: replays cards from the graveyard.
Madness: discards cards to cast them cheaply.
Lands / Loam: recurs lands for value.

Spells & engines

Spellslinger / Prowess: many cheap spells to trigger payoffs.
Storm: chains spells into a lethal storm count.
Burn: direct damage to the face.
Artifacts / Affinity: cheap artifacts fuel cost reduction and engines.
Artifact Tokens: Treasure, Food and Clues as resources and payoffs.
Enchantress: draws cards off cheap enchantments.
Superfriends: multiple planeswalkers.
Blink / Flicker: re-triggers ETB abilities.
Cascade / Discover: free spells from exile.
Clone / Copy: copies the best permanents and spells.
Cheat-into-Play: puts huge threats onto the battlefield without paying for them.
Energy: banks and spends energy counters.
Vehicles: creatures crew Vehicles.
Legends / Historic: payoffs for legendary permanents, artifacts and Sagas.
Eggs: loops cheap sacrificial artifacts for a combo.

Resources, land & life

Ramp: fast mana into big spells.
Stompy: Ramp into oversized creatures.
Tron / Big Mana: special lands produce outsized mana for colorless bombs.
Landfall: extra land drops trigger payoffs.
Land Destruction / Ponza: denies lands to strand the opponent’s cards.
Wildfire: Ramp plus mass land destruction.
Lifegain / Drain: gains life and converts it into a win.
Life-as-Resource: pays life to power cheap threats.
Mill: empties the opponent’s library.
Discard / 8-Rack: empties hands and punishes having none.
Wheels: mass draw-seven effects plus draw and discard payoffs.
Miracles / Top-Deck: manipulates the library top for cheap miracle casts.
Cycling: cycles cards for draw and payoffs.
Domain: rewards having many basic land types.
Devotion: colored mana symbols on permanents fuel payoffs.
Snow: snow permanents unlock snow payoffs.

Defensive, multiplayer & alt-win

Pillowfort: deters or taxes attacks against you.
Turbo-Fog: repeated Fogs to stall combat.
Hatebears / Death & Taxes: disruptive small creatures plus mana denial.
Group Hug: gives resources to everyone, then pivots to a win.
Group Slug: symmetric punishment for everyone.
Goad: forces opponents to attack each other.
Theft: steals opponents’ cards.
Aikido / Judo: turns the opponent’s own damage and spells against them.
Monarch / Initiative: fights over card-advantage designations.
Chaos: randomizes the game to disrupt plans.
Extra Turns: chains extra-turn spells.
Alternate Win: “you win the game” cards.

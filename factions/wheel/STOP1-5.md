# Stop 1, part 5: S B K O
See `STOP1.md` for what the ratings mean.

## S: full camp
- Strong in: many at once 1.6, more supply 1.4, kills outright 1.4.
- Builds on: cards that count my units; my units react when allies are placed. Extras: extra supply; I damage my own units; I push enemy units.
- On the board: Round end: I gain +1 strength for each other unit of mine in this camp; a full camp grows its holders.
- On the board: I give +1 supply for each other unit of mine in my lane: a full lane pays for its big units.
- On the board: Whenever you place a unit in this lane, I deal 1 damage to the enemy facing the placed unit: each deploy fights for its dot.
- Faction idea: my units can be placed only beside my other units: the camp grows outward from one cell (faeria: Khalim's Skyguard + CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: the fuller my camp, the stronger and richer each unit, and every placement hits.
- Removes: each unit I place in this lane makes my unit deal 1 to the enemy facing it.
- Holds: round end: my units grow for each ally in the camp.
- Feels good: I'm placed, and the enemy facing me takes 1 from my allies.
- Never has: pick and copy.

Combos with 15 factions (1 strong):
- O STRONG: O's Place puts 1|1 tokens in each free front cell facing an enemy; each token placed sets off S's allyplay unit, which deals 1 to the enemy facing that token. One action cancels his dots and wounds every holder; my allyplay unit sits in the back row where he hardly reaches.
- A OK: S's allyplay unit stands in a back cell; A's token maker fills my front cells and each token counts as a placed unit, so S deals 1 damage to the enemy facing each token. He answers by killing the allyplay unit with a Ranged unit; tokens are only an extra of A.
- B OK: S damages my own B hurt units on my turn; they survive and act when I choose. Two named kinds of card must meet.
- C OK: S damages my own small units until they die; each death grows C's units, and a front death lets the unit behind step in bigger. The enemy can't stop it.
- D OK: D creates 1-supply units that cost 1 less; each one placed in S's lane deals 1 to the enemy facing it. One action per placement, and the enemy can kill the S unit.
- E OK: S's push moves his back-row Ranged unit into a front cell; my next spell deals 1 to every enemy in that front row. He can move it back with his own action; push is only an extra of S.
- F OK: S damages my F hurt unit on my turn, so its hurt effect acts; F's round-start heal makes it whole again for the next round. A loop each round; he can break it by killing the hurt unit.
- H OK: When I place a unit in the lane, H's recycle unit comes back into my middle row; if that counts as a placement, S's allyplay deals 1 to the enemy facing each. One deploy, two hits; he answers by killing the allyplay unit.
- I OK: S's card damages my own I hurt unit; it survives and its hurt effect acts on my turn, with no enemy hit needed. Both are extras, so the pair meets only now and then.
- J OK: A J unit placed in S's lane, with its copy beside it, counts as two placements: two 1-damage hits on facers. Only if the copy counts as placed.
- K OK: K's units go back to hand each round; each time they are placed, S's unit deals 1 to the enemy facing them, and K's Place kill acts too. The enemy must kill S's unit.
- M OK: S pushes the enemy out of the cell facing my unit, then M's Place puts a 0|1 Egg of his into that free cell; it cancels nothing, so my dot stands. He kills the Egg with one attack to get the cell back.
- N OK: S damages my own units for its gains; N's shield takes that damage, so the gain costs nothing. He can't stop it; selfhurt is only an extra of S.
- P OK: A full camp lifts S's front holder to 5+ at round end; P's rule then lets it hold its dot with an enemy facing. The enemy can kill my small units to shrink the count.
- T OK: S's card damages my own T lastwords unit in a front cell on my turn, it dies when I choose and its Death acts. The enemy can't stop it, but he can move away from the facing cell before.
- Weak or none: G L Q R.
- Against P: P's big multi-cell units fill the camp with few units, so S's cards count little.
- Against C: C's offerings, sacrifices and self-hitting area shrink the camp S counts.
- Against B: B kills my units for supply or cheaper big units, which shrinks S's count.
- Against T: T's plan lets my units die; S's cards count my units in the camp and shrink with every offering.

## B: offerings
- Strong in: spends them 1.7, more supply 1.5, a few big units 1.4.
- Builds on: my unit dies for supply, a card or strength; I move my own units. Extras: my unit dies and takes an enemy with it; extra supply; my unit acts when hit and it survives; my unit gains when it kills.
- On the board: Place: one of my units in this lane dies; I cost its supply less and go into its cells: a cheap holder becomes a big one in the same cell (NEW RULE: placed into the freed cells as part of the cost).
- On the board: Spell: one of my units dies; carry-over gains its supply (up to 5): supply stuck in a lost lane goes to the lane that matters.
- On the board: Spell: swap two of my units in one camp, neither uses its move: a fresh unit steps into the front cell, the hurt one goes back (NEW RULE: a swap).
- Faction idea: big units cost units, not supply: a 5+ unit is placed by letting my units in its lane die whose supply adds up to its cost (inscryption: blood cost + CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: my small units die on purpose to make room and supply for big ones in the same cell.
- Removes: my unit dies and the enemy facing it dies too.
- Holds: a fresh unit swaps into the front cell without using a move.
- Feels good: Place: my small holder dies and I take its cell for less.
- Never has: draw.

## K: destroy and return
- Strong in: kills outright 1.6, brings back 1.4, cheaper hand 1.2.
- Builds on: destroy outright; my unit goes back to hand and is placed again. Extras: my dead units come back; spent cards come back from the discard pile; cheaper cards; cards grow in hand.
- On the board: Place: mark the enemy front cell facing me; at round end the enemy unit in that cell dies; he must leave the cell or lose the unit (NEW RULE: a mark on a cell).
- On the board: Place: destroy the enemy facing me if its supply is lower than mine; defence doesn't matter, only cost.
- On the board: Round start: I go back to your hand; each round I'm placed where needed and my Place kill acts again.
- Faction idea: returning to hand is its only way to move: every round it re-places its units where the kills are (duelyst: Tusk Boar, Orbrider + CLAUDE).
- Wins: I kill outright and take my killers back to hand to kill again.
- Removes: Place: destroy the enemy facing me if it costs less than me.
- Holds: I mark the cell facing my dot; at round end whatever stands there dies (NEW RULE).
- Feels good: Round start: I'm back in your hand, ready to kill again.
- Never has: defence growth.

## O: small units together
- Strong in: many cheap units 1.9, position and combos 1.5, spends them 1.2.
- Builds on: extra small units; units beside each other give each other more. Extras: my unit dies for supply, a card or strength; I damage my own units; Death effects; my units react when one leaves.
- On the board: Place: a 1|1 in each of my free front cells that faces an enemy: every enemy dot in this lane is cancelled in one action.
- On the board: Place: a 0|2 in every free cell beside me: the camp fills at once.
- On the board: My unit directly behind a front unit gives it +1 defence: a full column is hard to clear.
- Faction idea: my front unit holds its dot only while a unit of mine stands behind it, but then one enemy can't cancel it (CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: one card floods every front cell facing an enemy with small units, and a unit behind each makes it hard to kill.
- Removes: damage for each ally beside me.
- Holds: a unit directly behind a front unit gives it +1 defence.
- Feels good: Place: a 1|1 in every free front cell facing an enemy, and all his dots there are cancelled.
- Never has: units of cost 6+.

## Q: Enemy mover
- Strong in: damage from units' text 1.8, strikes more 1.3.
- Builds on: I push enemy units; small hits. Extras: the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; my attacks do something extra; my unit strikes a chosen enemy; damage spells.
- On the board: Place: the enemy unit in the front cell facing my unit moves back one row, or aside -> his cancelling unit leaves and my dot counts. Why: I take a dot without killing, so my small unit holds it. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Place: an enemy from the middle or back row is moved to a free front cell, where my units reach it -> a protected unit stands where my melee hits it. Why: back units that held income or Ranged are dragged into my strikes. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: When I push an enemy into a cell already held by another enemy, both take small hits; in a full camp the push does nothing but damage -> a packed camp is punished. Why: a full enemy camp cannot rebuild its front row after I clear it. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: every unit has strength 0 and no strikes; units only hold dots and move enemies or deal fixed damage by their text, and they still hold bases because units with 0 strength hold dots
- Wins: I push his units out of the way, so my small units hold dots without a fight
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Place: the enemy in the facing front cell moves back one row or aside, so his cancelling unit leaves and my dot counts (NEW RULE: moving enemy units)
- Removes back-row units: Pull a back-row enemy into the front row and hit it with my front units (NEW RULE: moving enemy units)
- Between lanes: Place: pull the enemy in the front cell of the lane beside into the front cell of this lane (NEW RULE: moving enemy units across lanes)
- Holds: When I push an enemy into a cell held by another enemy, both take small hits, so a packed camp cannot rebuild its front (NEW RULE: moving enemy units)
- Feels good: Place: an enemy from the middle or back row is moved to a free front cell where my units reach it
- Never has: my cards growing while they wait in hand

## R: Hand grower
- Strong in: brings back 1.5, grows strength 1.6, grows defence 1.4.
- Builds on: my unit gains when it kills; cards grow in hand. Extras: cards that count my units; my units grow from deaths; units with strength 5+; my unit dies for supply, a card or strength.
- On the board: Each round start, a card kept in my hand gains +1 strength and +1 defence; played, it enters the board that big -> I hold back a card to get a big unit for the same supply. Example: 2|2 in round 1 and 5|5 in round 4. Why: a card kept in hand takes a base from a bigger enemy unit later. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: At round start a card in my hand gains +1 strength for each unit of mine that died last round -> losses feed the hand while the board fights. Why: losing the front row turns into stronger units coming from the hand. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: When my unit kills an enemy, it gains +1 strength for good. Why: the dot-holding unit that clears the cell facing my dot gets harder to remove each round.
- Faction idea: holds dots from the back: back-row units have Pike reach and count as holding the dot of their column, so the front row is empty or small
- Wins: cards kept in my hand grow, so late big units arrive while my losses feed them
- Removes the unit facing my dot: When my unit kills an enemy it gains +1 strength for good, so the dot holder that clears the facing cell grows harder to remove
- Removes back-row units: Big grown units in my middle row have Pike reach and hit over the front row, picking off the enemy by strength
- Between lanes: The growth of a card in my hand counts the deaths of my units in all three lanes, and I place the card in the lane that needs it
- Holds: Each round start a card kept in my hand gains +1 strength and +1 defence (NEW RULE: a card in the hand grows)
- Feels good: Place: this unit enters as big as the rounds it waited in my hand (NEW RULE: a card in the hand grows)
- Never has: turning an enemy into another unit

## S: Camp filler
- Strong in: many at once 1.6, more supply 1.4, kills outright 1.4.
- Builds on: cards that count my units; extra supply. Extras: destroy outright; hits on a whole row or area; I damage my own units; small hits.
- On the board: A unit gets +1 strength and +1 defence for each filled cell of my camp (9 cells) -> a full camp holds all three dots strongly. Why: it rewards filling cells with cheap small units, and a camp wiped out loses everything at once.
- On the board: My carry-over starts each round at 5 (max 6 for me) and Place: +1 carry-over -> big units deploy in round 1. Why: I put a big unit in a front cell in the first turns and take a base early. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: no printed numbers: no unit has its own strength or defence, every number comes from how full the camp is, so the camp as a whole is the unit
- Wins: I fill my camp with cheap bodies, and every number comes from how full the camp is
- Removes the unit facing my dot: A unit gets +1 strength and +1 defence for each filled cell of my camp, so my front unit of a full camp beats the unit facing it
- Removes back-row units: Round end: when my camp in a lane is full, the enemy unit with the most supply in that lane takes 4 fixed damage, wherever it stands
- Between lanes: A unit counts my units in all three lanes, so one lane stacked with small units makes the other lanes strong (NEW RULE: a count reads all three lanes)
- Holds: A full camp holds all three dots strongly, and cheap units refill it each round from the big carry-over
- Feels good: Place: +1 carry-over, so big units deploy in round 1 (NEW RULE: carry-over start and maximum change)
- Never has: taking my unit back to hand to place it again

## T: Place striker
- Strong in: kills outright 1.5, spends them 1.3.
- Builds on: my unit strikes a chosen enemy; attack the turn it is placed. Extras: my unit dies for supply, a card or strength; Death effects; take an enemy unit or card (NEW RULE); my unit gains when it kills.
- On the board: Place: this unit strikes any enemy in the lane, even out of reach -> the blocker that cancels my dot dies by my unit's strength and no attack is spent. Why: I take a dot on the same action that places the unit. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Place: two chosen enemies strike each other (strength against defence) -> one action removes two units. Why: I remove his best unit by his own strength, so my small units can take the dot. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Place: this unit also attacks once now, paying its supply -> a unit placed in the front row strikes right away without a move. Why: a unit placed at the end of a round has already struck before the enemy can answer. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: one-shot strikers: every unit strikes only when it is placed and never attacks later, so the faction attacks by placing, not by attacking
- Wins: units strike the moment they are placed, so I clear the blocker and take the dot in one action
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Place: this unit strikes any enemy in the lane, even out of reach, so the blocker dies by my unit's strength and no attack is spent (NEW RULE: choose a target outside reach)
- Removes back-row units: The same Place strike chooses an enemy in his back row, so a unit placed in my front row hits his back row
- Between lanes: A unit that kills with its Place strike may move to the same cell of the next lane at once (NEW RULE: a free move to another lane)
- Holds: Place: this unit also attacks once now, in the same action, so the blocker is gone before he can answer (NEW RULE: a unit attacks the turn it is placed)
- Feels good: Place: two chosen enemies strike each other, strength against defence, so one action removes two units (NEW RULE: an enemy is made to strike)
- Never has: a unit that makes enemy attacks go where I choose

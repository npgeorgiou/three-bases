## A: death and return
WALK revive | front cells: the unit comes back into the front cell it died in -> the dot is held again at once (Duelyst: Auroraboros, Sarlac the Eternal) | round start: it leaves a 0-strength Egg in its cell that becomes the unit again at round start -> the cell, and so the dot, stays filled over round end (Duelyst: Rebirth) | lanes: units that died this round come back in any lane, any free cell -> a lost front cell is retaken late in the round (Duelyst: Nether Summoning) | hand: it goes back to the hand on death -> it can be deployed again where a dot is open (Inscryption: Unkillable) | supply: the return costs nothing, so the lane's supply goes to another deploy (CLAUDE)
WALK lastwords | front cells: Death: a small unit takes this cell -> the dot stays mine (Duelyst: Fenrir Warmaster) | facing: Death: damage to the facing enemy -> the unit that cancelled my dot dies with mine (Inscryption: Detonator, Gem Detonator) | rows: a stronger Death when it dies in my front row (Stormbound: Embers of Chaos) | supply: Death: +supply in this lane this round -> a replacement can be deployed into the cell (Faeria: Deathwish Ghoul)
VERSION A | revive | front cells | I die in a cell -> I come back once in that same cell. "Death: return to this cell, once." | Duelyst: Auroraboros / Sarlac the Eternal | -> (like Revenant) | killing my front unit does not free the dot the first time.
VERSION A | lastwords | front cells | I die -> a small unit of mine takes my cell. "Death: put a 1/1 in this cell." | Duelyst: Fenrir Warmaster | -> | the dot stays held after the holder dies.
VERSION A | revive | round start | I die -> an Egg holds my cell, and at round start the Egg becomes me again. "Death: leave a 0/1 Egg; round start: the Egg becomes this unit." | Duelyst: Rebirth | -> | the cell is filled at round end and at round start, the two moments that count.
VERSION A | revive | lanes | my units died this round -> I bring two back in any lane. "Spell: two of my units that died this round come back in free cells of any lane." | Duelyst: Nether Summoning | -> | retakes front cells in the lane that matters just before round end.
VERSION A | lastwords | rows / facing | I die in my front row -> the facing enemy takes damage. "Death, if in my front row: 3 damage to the facing enemy." | Inscryption: Detonator; Stormbound: Embers of Chaos | -> | the unit that cancels my dot dies with my holder, so my next deploy holds it.
IDEA A | my dead units' tombstones hold their front cell's dot until round end | CLAUDE (NEW RULE)

## T: duels
WALK fight | front cells: Place: strike the facing enemy front unit -> deploy and attack in one action, the cancel is gone this turn (Duelyst: Lavaslasher) | reach: strike a chosen enemy in reach, not the nearest -> a back-row supply unit can be picked by a Ranged unit (Faeria: Hunt Down; NEW RULE, strikes go to the nearest) | camp: an enemy strikes another enemy beside it -> two of his front holders hurt each other (Stormbound: Unhealthy Hysteria, Hair-Raising Cats; NEW RULE) | camp: before my unit attacks, enemies beside the target strike it too (Stormbound: Laurus, King in Exile; NEW RULE)
WALK onkill | one action: on kill it can attack again this round (Duelyst: Sleet Dasher) | cells: on kill a 0-strength Wall of mine takes the killed unit's cell in his camp -> he can't redeploy into that front cell (Duelyst: White Asp, Rage Reactor) | rows: on kill, strike the next enemy behind in the column (Into the Breach: EM Railgun) | supply: on kill +1 carry-over (Duelyst: Mana Deathgrip; Into the Breach: Grid Charger)
VERSION T | fight | front cells | I deploy -> I strike the facing enemy front unit at once. "Place: strike the facing enemy." | Duelyst: Lavaslasher | -> | removes the cancel and holds the dot in the same action.
VERSION T | fight | camp (enemies strike) | I pick an enemy unit -> it strikes an enemy unit beside it. "Spell: an enemy unit strikes the enemy beside it." | Stormbound: Unhealthy Hysteria | NEW RULE (an enemy strikes its own side) | his two front holders hurt each other, without me spending a unit's attack.
VERSION T | onkill | one action / attacks | my unit kills -> it can attack again this round. "When this kills, it may attack again this round." | Duelyst: Sleet Dasher | -> | one front unit clears two cancelling enemies in a round.
VERSION T | onkill | cells (enemy camp) | my unit kills -> a 0/1 Wall of mine takes that enemy cell. "When this kills, put a 0/1 Wall of mine in that cell." | Duelyst: White Asp / Rage Reactor | -> (units of mine in his camp exist) | he can't put a new unit in that front cell, so my dot stays uncancelled.
IDEA T | every unit's Place strikes the facing enemy: the faction fights only across the base, one duel per front cell | Duelyst: Lavaslasher + CLAUDE

## I: big units early

WALK cheaper | bases: costs 1 less for each base I hold (Faeria: Primeval Colossus) | one action: Place: deploy a cheap card from hand too (Gwent: Emhyr var Emreis; NEW RULE, two deploys in one turn) | strength: costs less if I have a unit with 5+ defence (Faeria: Ancient Boar) | deaths: costs 1 less per unit of mine that died (Faeria: Mother of All Yaks) | enemy costs: his deploys in this lane cost 1 more (Duelyst: Corporeal Haunt)

VERSION I | supply | cells | I mark a cell -> the unit standing on it gives +1 supply. "Place: mark a free front cell; a unit of mine there gives +1 supply." | Duelyst: Mana Spring / Celebrant | NEW RULE (marked cells) | makes the front cell worth holding twice.
VERSION I | cheaper | lanes / bases | I hold bases -> my big unit costs less. "Costs 1 less for each base I hold." | Faeria: Primeval Colossus | -> | the side holding a base gets the big unit to hold it to round end.
VERSION I | cheaper | one action per turn | I deploy -> a cheap unit from hand comes too. "Place: deploy a unit of supply 2 or less from hand into this lane free." | Gwent: Emhyr var Emreis | NEW RULE (two deploys in one turn) | two front cells filled in one action, before he can answer.
VERSION I | hand (enemy costs) | supply per lane | my unit is in this lane -> his deploys here cost 1 more. "His deploys in this lane cost 1 more." | Duelyst: Corporeal Haunt | -> | he can't afford to refill a lost front cell.
IDEA I | the back row as a mine: my units in the back row give double supply but can't attack | CLAUDE (NEW RULE)

## L: weaken and silence
WALK silence | cells: Place: units in the cells around it, across the base too, lose their text for good -> the facing holder's Armoured or Riposte is gone (Duelyst: Lightbender, Ephemeral Shroud) | rows: Death: enemy units in the facing front row lose their text (Duelyst: Ironclad) | moves: Place: facing front enemies can't move or attack this round (Stormbound: Frosthexers, freeze) | Immobile: silence takes away moves like Outrider (CLAUDE)
WALK weaken | facing: while this is in my front row, the facing enemy has less strength (Inscryption: Stinkbug, Stinky) | strength 0: lose strength, then destroy those at 0 (Faeria: Emperor Kaios) | lanes: enemies in this lane -2 strength this round (Duelyst: Horrific Visage) | set: an enemy's strength becomes 1 (Stormbound: Confinement) | guard: when an enemy is placed facing an empty front cell, this moves there (Inscryption: Guardian)
VERSION L | weaken | facing | my unit is in my front row -> the facing enemy is weaker. "While in my front row: the facing enemy has -2 strength." | Inscryption: Stinkbug (Stinky) | -> | my holder survives the unit that faces it.
VERSION L | silence | cells around | I deploy -> units around me lose their text for good. "Place: every enemy around this loses its text." | Duelyst: Lightbender | -> | his front-row protections stop, so my strikes take the cell.
VERSION L | weaken | round end | round ends -> enemies in this lane lose strength, those at 0 die. "Round end: enemies in this lane -1 strength for good; destroy those with 0." | Faeria: Emperor Kaios | -> | happens when he can't answer, right at the moment bases are counted.
VERSION L | silence (stop) | moves and attacks | I deploy -> the facing front enemies can't move or attack this round. "Place: enemies in the facing front row can't move or attack this round." | Stormbound: Frosthexers | -> | he can't strike my holders or retake the cell before round end.
VERSION L | guard | front cells | he places a unit facing my empty front cell -> my unit moves there. "When an enemy is placed facing an empty front cell of mine, this moves there." | Inscryption: Guardian | -> (a reaction move) | his new unit never gets an uncancelled dot.
IDEA L | every enemy facing one of my front units has no text while it faces it | Inscryption: Stinky + CLAUDE

## M: change and junk

WALK junk | facing front cell: Place: an Egg of his in the facing front cell that holds no dot -> he can't put a holder there (Inscryption: Brood Parasite; NEW RULE) | free cells: Death: a 0/1 Immobile unit of his in a free cell of his camp -> no room for his 2x2 or line of 3 (Stormbound: Dubious Hags; Inscryption: Cuckoo) | hand: fill his hand to 10 -> he draws nothing at round start (Faeria: Rapala, the Dopefish) | deck: put a junk card in his deck (Stormbound: Malicious Finch)

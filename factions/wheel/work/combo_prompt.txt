You judge combos between factions of Three Bases, a browser card game on a board. Decks will mix 2 factions. You
write no cards with stats and no code.

Read first (short): /home/claude/three-bases/summary/03-board-and-bases.txt to 07-areas-resolution.txt (the rules),
then /home/claude/three-bases/factions/wheel/work/combo_factions.txt (each faction's mechanics) and
/home/claude/three-bases/factions/wheel/board.tsv (each faction's board versions: how its mechanics work on this board,
and one faction idea; NEW RULE = the game can't do it yet, still allowed).

The rules in short: 3 lanes; in each, my 3x3 camp faces the enemy camp across a base. A unit in a front cell holds that
cell's dot; an enemy in the facing front cell cancels it; 2 dots = the base. Win: hold 2 bases at the start and the end
of the same round. Supply per lane comes from my units there; carry-over (max 5) is spent anywhere. One action per
turn, alternating, so the enemy answers every card before the next. Decks of 30-40 cards with 3 copies, 2 draws a round:
a combo of two NAMED cards is rare; a combo between two mechanics that each sit on many cards is common.

Your input file lists faction pairs. Under a pair there may be LIBRARY KEEP lines: combos real card games show between
their mechanics, with a played sequence. For EACH pair:
1. Find the best 1-2 combos between the two factions, using their board versions first, then their mechanics. A combo
   means one side changes what the other does or sets it off (a loop, a conversion, a rule change), so together they
   do something neither does alone. Bigger numbers alone are not a combo. Library KEEP combos count as candidates too:
   judge them on THIS board.
2. Rate each combo:
   STRONG = both halves sit on many cards of their factions, it helps take or hold a base, and the enemy's one action
            between the two cards can't easily stop it.
   OK     = real, but needs a setup the enemy can break with one action, or a narrow board state.
   WEAK   = barely a combo: mostly bigger numbers, or needs two named cards.
3. Also note if the two factions work AGAINST each other in one deck (one's plan ruins the other's).
Write one line per combo (and one per "against"), tab-separated, to the output file named in your task, appending as
you go:
x <TAB> y <TAB> rating (STRONG/OK/WEAK/AGAINST) <TAB> combo in a few words ("x's push + y's Place strike") <TAB>
how it plays, one or two plain sentences with cells and the enemy's answer <TAB> source (LIBRARY KEEP a+b, or
BOARD: the board versions used, or CLAUDE)
If a pair has nothing real, write one line with rating NONE. Plain words, no flavour. Be strict: most pairs of
factions should NOT get STRONG. Reply at the end with one line only: "done, N lines".

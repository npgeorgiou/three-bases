# Board versions, input board_in_2 (E, D, J, H, Q). Sources from library/cards_db (card texts read).

## E: damage spells
THINGS spelldmg: dots/front cells: damage the enemy in the front cell that faces my unit -> his cancel goes, my dot counts. Rows: hit the whole enemy front row of one lane -> all 3 of his dot holders at once. Columns: hit one enemy column of a lane, front to back -> the holder and the one that would step in. Lanes: hit one unit in each lane -> weak holders in every base. Round end: a spell can't be cast then (no answers rule), so damage spells are the turn before he passes. Reach: a spell needs no reach, so it hits back rows that melee can't. Immobile: a spell kills a blocker that can't be moved away. Kept: front cells, rows, columns.
THINGS spellpay: supply per lane: a spell paid from a lane makes my units of that lane gain. Front cells: my front-row unit hits the enemy facing it each time I cast a spell. Carry-over: spells cast count up so later spells cost less. One action per turn: each spell is one turn, so the gain must be worth a turn (+strength now, then attack). Kept: front cells, supply per lane, carry-over.

## D: created cards
THINGS draw: dots/front cells: draw when this front-row unit faces no enemy at round end -> holding pays cards. Round start: draw if I held this unit's base at round start. Death: a dot holder that dies draws, so a replacement comes. Hand: hand max 10, so draw must be spent. Rows: a back-row unit draws, safe from melee. Kept: front cells, round end, Death.
THINGS create: hand: a Death or spell adds a small unit card to my hand -> a cheap holder for the next turn. Enemy camp: create a useless unit in his cell (junk). Lanes: a created card that can be deployed only in the lane it was made in. Carry-over: created cards cost little so carry-over pays them anywhere. Shapes: created units are 1 cell, to fit any free front cell. Kept: hand, enemy camp, Death.
VERSION D-A | draw | dots and front cells | My front-row unit with no enemy facing it at round end draws -> holding an open dot gives cards. "Round end: if no enemy faces this unit, draw 1." | inscryption: Looter (draws for damage dealt straight through) | -> | rewards keeping the dot uncancelled to the end of the round.
VERSION D-B | create | Death | My dot holder adds a small unit card to my hand when it dies -> I refill the front cell next turn. "Death: add a 1-cell, 1-supply Guard card to my hand." | faeria: Cartographer, Hold the Line! | -> | the dot is lost for one turn only.
VERSION D-C | create | one action per turn | Each spell I cast adds a small unit card to my hand -> spell turns leave me more holders to place. "Whenever I cast a spell, add a 1-supply 1-cell unit card to my hand." | duelyst: Circulus | -> | more cheap units than he has cells to answer.
VERSION D-D | junk | enemy front cells | I put a useless unit in an empty enemy front cell -> he can't deploy a holder there and it cancels nothing. "Place: put an Egg (0 strength, holds and cancels no dot) in the empty enemy front cell facing this unit." | inscryption: Brood Parasite (Egg on the opposing space) | NEW RULE: a unit that holds and cancels no dot; a unit placed in the enemy camp | locks one of his 3 dots out for as long as the Egg lives.
VERSION D-E | cheaper | carry-over | Created cards cost 1 less -> two deploys in one lane's supply. "Cards I create cost 1 less supply." | faeria: Khalim, Sky Prodigy (Follower costs 0), Bloomsprite | -> | fills two front cells in one round in a poor lane.
IDEA D | created units may be deployed in any lane, paid only from carry-over, even a lane where I have no units yet | CLAUDE

## J: pick and copy
THINGS pick: dots/front cells: a unit that dies in a front cell brings the next unit from my deck into its cell -> the dot stays held. Enemy front row: pick as many units as he has holders. Round start: pick instead of drawing. Shapes: pick a unit of the shape that fits the free cells (a 1-cell for one gap, a line of 3 for an empty front row). Discard pile: pick from it (revive). Kept: front cells, round start, shapes.
THINGS copy: free cells: a copy goes into the free cell beside the unit -> one card fills two front cells. Lanes: a copy goes into the same cell of another lane. Hand: a copy of a placed unit goes to my hand for later. Supply per lane: a copy gives supply too, so a copied unit doubles a lane's income. Kept: free cells, lanes, supply per lane.
VERSION J-A | pick | dots and front cells | My dot holder that dies brings a unit of my deck into its cell -> the dot is never empty. "Death: deploy the cheapest unit of my deck into this unit's cell, free." | duelyst: Reaper of the Nine Moons, Windcliffe Alarmist, Kaido Expert | -> | he must kill two holders to open the cell.
VERSION J-B | pick | enemy front row | I take as many units from my deck as he has front-row units in the lane -> an answer for each of his holders. "Place: for each enemy in the facing front row, draw a unit from my deck." | faeria: Curious Biomancer | -> | catches up in a lane where he holds the base.
VERSION J-C | copy | free cells | My unit makes a copy of itself in the free cell beside it -> one card holds two dots. "Place in the front row: put a copy of this unit in a free front cell beside it." | duelyst: Orb Weaver, Mirror Meld | -> | takes a base (2 dots) with one action.
VERSION J-D | copy | lanes | My unit makes a copy in the same cell of another lane -> a dot in two bases from one card. "Place: put a copy of this unit in the same cell of another lane, if free." | CLAUDE (from duelyst: Orb Weaver) | NEW RULE: an effect that places a unit in another lane | holding 2 bases needs presence in 2 lanes.
VERSION J-E | revive | front cells | My dead unit leaves an Egg in its cell that becomes the unit again at round start -> the cell is never free for him. "Death: leave a 0|1 Egg; round start: the Egg becomes this unit again." | duelyst: Rebirth | NEW RULE: does the Egg hold the dot (proposed: yes) | the base stays held from start to end of the round.
IDEA J | no draws at round start: instead I pick 2 cards from my deck | CLAUDE (NEW RULE; from inscryption: Boon of the Magpie's Eye)

## H: discard and return
THINGS discard: front cells: a discarded unit jumps from the discard pile into a free front cell -> a holder with no deploy cost. Strikes/fixed damage: each discard hurts the enemy facing my front-row unit. Hand: discard as the cost of a Place, so the hand pays instead of supply. Supply per lane: discarding instead of paying lets a poor lane act. Kept: front cells, fixed damage, supply per lane.
THINGS recycle: rows: a dead unit comes back from the discard pile into a row of the lane where I deploy -> the lane refills. Spells: a damage spell played again from the discard pile hits the next blocker. Round start: a unit returns at round start into a free cell. Hand: a card goes back to the hand to be paid again. Kept: rows, spells, hand.
VERSION H-A | discard | dots and front cells | My unit, when I discard it, deploys itself from the discard pile -> a free holder in an empty front cell. "When discarded: deploy this into a free front cell of a lane where I have a unit." | gwent: Tuirseach Skirmisher | NEW RULE: an effect that deploys from the discard pile when discarded | takes a dot without spending a turn's supply.
VERSION H-B | discard | fixed damage | Each card I discard makes my front-row unit hit the enemy facing it -> discards clear dot blockers. "Whenever I discard a card, this unit deals 1 damage to the enemy facing it." | gwent: Coral (aimed, not random) | -> | turns spare cards into an open dot.
VERSION H-C | discard | hand | My Place draws then discards -> I set up H-A and H-B and keep the best card. "Place: draw 1, then discard 1." | gwent: Heymaey Skald, Birna Bran | -> | the engine that starts every discard version in one action.
VERSION H-D | recycle | rows | My dead unit returns when I deploy in its lane -> the lane's middle row refills behind the holder. IDEA H | my discard pile is a second hand: its units can be deployed for 1 more supply | CLAUDE (NEW RULE)

## Q: push and ping
THINGS push: dots/front cells: push the enemy facing my unit out of his front row -> my dot counts though he lives. Rows: push him deeper so his melee no longer reaches my front row. Columns: push a whole column one row back. Lanes: push him into another lane where he is weak. Camp full: a push into a filled cell fails or hurts (bump). Moving/turning: a pushed unit has not used its move, so he can step back: the push must come late in the round or with a cost on stepping back. Immobile: a pushed Immobile unit stays where it lands. Kept: front cells, rows, full camp, round order.
THINGS ping: front cells: 1 damage to whatever steps into the cell facing my unit. Strikes vs fixed damage: ping is fixed damage, so it ignores strength and hits units that win strikes. Bump: damage when a pushed unit hits another. Rows: 1 damage to each enemy in his front row. Kept: front cells, bump, rows.

"Place: push the enemy facing this unit back as far as free cells allow." | stormbound: Ozone Purifiers, Eloth the Ignited; duelyst: Repulsor Beast | NEW RULE: moving an enemy unit inside his camp (does it use his move? proposed: no) | opens a dot without killing, best as the last action before he passes.
VERSION Q-B | push | camp full | A push into a filled cell hurts both units -> pushing works best on a crowded enemy camp. IDEA Q | everything the faction does aims only at the enemy front row: it holds bases by keeping his three front cells empty, not by having big units | CLAUDE

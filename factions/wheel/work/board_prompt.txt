You help design factions for Three Bases, a browser card game on a board. The factions were placed on a "wheel" of
general mechanics taken from other card games. Your job: turn each faction's mechanics into THIS game's own board
things, from scratch. You write no cards with stats, no code. You only read the library and write one file.

THIS GAME (read summary/03-board-and-bases.txt ... summary/07-areas-resolution.txt in /home/claude/three-bases first;
they are short):
- 3 lanes. In each lane my camp (3x3 cells: front, middle, back row; 3 columns) faces the enemy camp across a base.
- Dots: each base has 3, one per front cell. My unit in a front cell holds the dot; an enemy in the facing front cell
  cancels it. 2 dots = the base. Win: hold 2 bases at the start AND the end of the same round.
- Supply per lane comes only from my units in that lane; spent only there. Carry-over (start 4, max 5) is spent anywhere,
  filled at round end from leftover supply. A unit's supply is its income AND its cost to deploy, move and attack.
- One action per turn (deploy, move, attack, spell), alternating. Round ends when both pass in a row. Round start:
  supply comes in, 2 draws. Round-end effects happen when the enemy can't answer.
- Each unit moves once and attacks once per round; moves go anywhere free in my own camp; turning counts as a move.
  Shapes: 1 cell, line of 2, line of 3, 2x2. Melee reaches the cells around it, across the base; Pike from the middle
  row; Ranged the whole lane. Strikes use strength; fixed damage is not a strike. No hits between lanes; changing lane
  only by special moves (Outrider). Immobile units can't move.
- An attacked unit does not hit back (only Riposte does), so "strike first" means nothing. Units are placed only in
  my own camp (placing in the enemy camp is a NEW RULE).
- No player health, no answers on the enemy's turn, no abilities you press, no counters ("at 3 counters"), no unit
  types, no randomness. Place, Death, round-end and position effects are wanted.

THE OWN THINGS (go through every one for each mechanic): dots and front cells; rows (front/middle/back); columns;
lanes (3, and moving or acting between them); the 3x3 camp (full, empty, free cells); shapes and size; supply per
lane; carry-over; round start and round end; one action per turn; moving and turning; reach (melee, Pike, Ranged);
strikes and fixed damage; the hand and the discard pile; Immobile.

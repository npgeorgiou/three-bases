# Step 3: the ways to play that decks lean on, by NMF (made by deck_groups_nmf.py)

3027 decks of 10 games, 136 concepts. 17 groups (set by hand). A deck fits a group that is 30% or more of its mix. The archetype: the group's 10 heaviest and base concepts (more than half of its decks have them), without the five unit measures and without the concepts more than half of the groups hold. Design markers: what its decks are like, x2 = twice as often as their games' decks. The 10 heaviest come with the share of its decks that lean on each. A faction fits its best 2 groups at 2 times its game's average share of their concepts ("x2").

Deck card names the library does not know: duelyst 29, faeria 54, into_the_breach 2, marvel_snap 2, monster_train 37, runeterra 11, sts1 121.

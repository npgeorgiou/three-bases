# Factions from halves of the deck archetypes (an experiment; made by kernel_factions.py)

5 factions; each pair of factions shares one deck archetype, one half in each. Kernels: the concept of each half.
Engines have a strong card link with another of the faction's concepts; bricks have none.

## Faction A (281 decks lean on its kernels)
- Halves: Wheels: draw and discard many (Discard my own cards); Clone / Copy: copy a card, a unit or its stats (Repeat or double triggers); Eggs: gain from all the dying and leaving (Counts deaths); Toolbox: fetch it again (Bring back dead units (revive))
- Engines: Discard my own cards (kernel); Repeat or double triggers (kernel); Counts deaths (kernel); Bring back dead units (revive) (kernel); Draw cards; Having a card in play or hand; When a card is discarded; Sacrifice my own units; Cheat death (survive lethal); When another unit dies
- Bricks: none

## Faction B (105 decks lean on its kernels)
- Halves: Wheels: gain from draws and discards (Grows again and again); Control: cards keep coming from the removal (Grows with what happened this game); Tempo: cheap cards that send back, move or stop enemy units (Move enemy units (push, pull)); +1/+1 Counters: cards that care about big stats (Stats matter (highest, lowest))
- Engines: Grows again and again (kernel); Grows with what happened this game (kernel); Move enemy units (push, pull) (kernel); Stats matter (highest, lowest) (kernel); Order of my plays matters (combo)
- Bricks: Enough spells played

## Faction C (229 decks lean on its kernels)
- Halves: Clone / Copy: the unit worth copying (Draw cards); Control: answers to every enemy unit (Deal damage); Ramp: big spells (Damage to many at once); Storm: gain from how many (Counts / thresholds)
- Engines: Draw cards (kernel); Deal damage (kernel); Damage to many at once (kernel); Counts / thresholds (kernel); Discard my own cards; When a card is discarded
- Bricks: none

## Faction D (269 decks lean on its kernels)
- Halves: Eggs: cheap units that die and give something (Sacrifice my own units); Tempo: cheap units that act at once (Move my units (reposition)); Ramp: more supply in a lane, or cards that cost less (Summon units / tokens); Voltron: keep that unit alive (Take less damage (armour))
- Engines: Sacrifice my own units (kernel); Move my units (reposition) (kernel); Summon units / tokens (kernel); Take less damage (armour) (kernel); Counts deaths
- Bricks: none

## Faction E (192 decks lean on its kernels)
- Halves: Toolbox: a different answer on each unit (Destroy outright); +1/+1 Counters: units that grow and keep it (Defence gain (+health)); Storm: play many cards (Put into play for free); Voltron: bonuses piled on one unit (Attack gain (+attack))
- Engines: Destroy outright (kernel); Defence gain (+health) (kernel); Put into play for free (kernel); Attack gain (+attack) (kernel); Board wipe
- Bricks: none

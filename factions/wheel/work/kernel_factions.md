# Ten whole factions from what they do (an experiment; made by kernel_factions.py)

Kernel: the effects that do what the faction does. Engines: concepts real cards link to 2+ kernel concepts. Bricks: what its decks hold 1.5x+ as often as their games, with no card link to the kernel. About 12 concepts. Partners: its 5 strongest pairs (card links between their concepts, plus 3 per kernel concept the other's decks hold 1.5x+). Bricks taken by more than 3 factions are dropped from all: Silence (abilities removed).

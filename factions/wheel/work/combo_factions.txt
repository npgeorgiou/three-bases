## A: signature revive,lastwords; extras recycle,tokens,attach,leave
   revive: my dead units come back from the discard pile -> their Place acts again
   lastwords: I die -> my Death effect acts (a unit worth more dead than alive)
   recycle: my spent cards go from the discard pile back to deck or hand -> I play them again
   tokens: my Place or Death effect puts extra small units on the grid -> they fill cells and front cells cheaply (never round-end seeding: Rootkin)
   attach: I attach a unit or item to another unit -> the carrier gets its stats and text
   leave: my unit leaves the grid (dies, returns, moves) -> my other cards react
## T: signature fight,onkill; extras offering,deathfeed,lastwords,spelldmg
   fight: I make my unit strike a chosen enemy, or make enemies strike -> removal by my unit's strength
   onkill: my unit kills an enemy -> it gains or acts again
   offering: I let my own unit die -> I gain supply, carry-over, a card or strength (the library's usual sacrifice)
   deathfeed: my units die -> my other units or cards grow from each death
   lastwords: I die -> my Death effect acts (a unit worth more dead than alive)
   spelldmg: my spells deal damage -> removal from the hand, no unit needed
## I: signature supply,cheaper; extras bigstrength,hand,hurt,onkill
   supply: I gain extra supply or carry-over -> big units come early
   cheaper: my cards get cheaper or free -> two cards in one turn's supply
   bigstrength: my units have strength 5 or more -> cards that need big strength fire
   hand: I act on the enemy's hand and costs -> his next cards cost more or are gone
   hurt: my unit is hit and survives -> it acts
   onkill: my unit kills an enemy -> it gains or acts again

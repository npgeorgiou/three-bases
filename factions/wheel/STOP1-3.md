# Stop 1, part 3: H Q G F
See `STOP1.md` for what the ratings mean.

## H: discard and return
- Strong in: brings back 1.7, damage from units' text 1.5, more cards 1.2.
- Builds on: discard my cards for an effect; spent cards come back from the discard pile. Extras: draw extra cards; useless cards and units for the enemy; small fixed damage, often; hits on a whole row or area.
- On the board: When discarded: I am placed into a free front cell of a lane where you have a unit; a free holder (NEW RULE: placed from the discard pile when discarded).
- On the board: Whenever you discard a card, I deal 1 damage to the enemy facing me: spare cards open a dot.
- On the board: When you place a unit in this lane, I come back from the discard pile into a free middle-row cell: a replacement behind the holder.
- Faction idea: my discard pile is a second hand: its units can be placed for 1 more supply (CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: my discards are not lost: they hit, and they walk into the front cells by themselves.
- Removes: each card discarded: my front unit deals 1 to the enemy facing it.
- Holds: a dead unit comes back from the discard pile into the middle row behind the holder when I place a unit in the lane.
- Feels good: I'm discarded and jump from your hand into a free front cell.
- Never has: guard.

## Q: push and ping
- Strong in: damage from units' text 1.8, strikes more or first 1.3, one chosen 1.5.
- Builds on: small fixed damage, often; I push enemy units. Extras: strike twice or first; my attacks do something extra; my unit strikes a chosen enemy; damage spells.
- On the board: Place: push the enemy facing me back as far as free cells allow; my dot counts and he must spend a move to return (NEW RULE: moving an enemy inside his camp (not using his move)).
- On the board: A pushed enemy that can't move because a unit is behind it: both take 1 damage; pushes punish a crowded camp (NEW RULE: bump damage).
- On the board: Whenever an enemy is placed or moves into the cell facing me, I deal 1 damage to it: 1-defence holders can't take that cell.
- Faction idea: everything aims at the enemy front row: it holds bases by keeping his three front cells empty, not with big units (CLAUDE).
- Wins: I keep his three front cells empty: pushed back, or too hurt to step in.
- Removes: push the enemy facing me back as far as it goes.
- Holds: an enemy placed or moving into the cell facing me takes 1 damage at once.
- Feels good: Place: his holder slides to the back row and my dot counts.
- Never has: return to hand.

## G: first strike
- Strong in: strikes more or first 1.8, grows strength 1.5, strength 1.4.
- Builds on: strike twice or first; my attacks do something extra. Extras: my cards act in another lane; my unit strikes a chosen enemy; units with strength 5+; I push enemy units.
- On the board: My attack strikes every enemy in my reach; from the front-middle cell it hits all three facing front cells.
- On the board: I may move twice and attack twice per round, paying each time: strike with Pike from the middle row, then step into the freed front cell.
- On the board: When I attack from the front row, +1 carry-over: the attack at one base pays for holding another.
- Faction idea: every unit attacks only from the front row, but strikes twice there (CLAUDE).
- Wins: my front units hit everything in reach and act twice, so every trade for a front cell is mine.
- Removes: one attack hits all three facing front cells.
- Holds: attacks from the front row pay +1 carry-over, so one base pays for holding another.
- Feels good: One attack from the front-middle cell hits all three enemy holders.
- Never has: damage spells.

## F: heal and grow
- Strong in: heals them 1.8, grows strength 1.3, grows defence 1.5, defence 1.3.
- Builds on: heal my units; round start effects. Extras: the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; units beside each other give each other more; my units grow from deaths; my unit acts when hit and it survives.
- On the board: Round start: the ally directly in front of me regains all its defence; a back-row healer keeps its column's holder whole.
- On the board: Round start: if I hold a dot, I gain +1|+1; the longer I hold it, the harder it is to take.
- On the board: Round end, before carry-over fills: each leftover supply in this lane restores 1 defence to my units there (NEW RULE: leftover supply spent at round end).
- Faction idea: a back-row garden: units that never leave the back row and heal or grow the unit in front of them every round start (stormbound: Emerald Towers, Upgrade Point).
- Wins: my back row keeps my front row whole, and holders grow every round they hold.
- Removes: a holder healed every round grows and outlasts what faces it.
- Holds: round start: the unit in front of my healer regains all its defence.
- Feels good: Round start: I held my dot, so I gain +1|+1.
- Never has: offering.

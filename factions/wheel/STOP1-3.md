# Stop 1, part 3: I J K L
See `STOP1.md` for what the ratings mean.

## I: Big striker
- Strong in: more supply 1.6, cheaper hand 1.4, a few big units 1.8.
- Builds on: the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; units with strength 5+. Extras: act on the enemy's hand and costs; my unit acts again; my unit gains when it kills; my unit strikes a chosen enemy.
- On the board: A strike that kills with strength to spare sends the rest of the hit into the unit behind the dead one in its column. Example: strength 6 kills a defence 2 unit and the other 4 hits the unit behind it. Why: killing the front enemy also hurts the middle and back unit, so the next unit to fill the front cell is already weak. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: A card works only while I have a unit with strength 5 or more in the lane. Example: Round end: while I have a 5+ strength unit here, 2 fixed damage to the enemy facing each of my front units. Why: one big unit in a lane clears the cells facing my dots at round end.
- On the board: A big unit costs 1 less for each friend of strength 5 or more in its lane. Why: lane supply is what limits big units, so a second big unit reaches the front the same round.
- Faction idea: tall: every unit is a vertical line of 2 or 3 cells with strength 5 or more; three units fill the three columns and no unit stands behind them for the rest of a hit to reach
- Wins: one huge unit kills, and the rest of its hit wrecks the units behind
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Round end: while I have a unit of strength 5 or more in the lane, 2 fixed damage to the enemy facing each of my front units
- Removes back-row units: A strike that kills with strength to spare sends the rest of the hit into the unit behind the dead one, down the column (NEW RULE: the rest of a hit passes on)
- Between lanes: A unit of strength 5 or more in any lane turns on my big-unit cards in all three lanes
- Holds: A big unit costs 1 less for each friend of strength 5 or more in its lane, so a second big unit reaches the front the same round
- Feels good: Place: this unit costs 1 less for each friend of strength 5 or more in its lane
- Never has: units that cannot be targeted or hit

- Q OK: Q pulls an enemy from the back into a front cell and my big unit kills it and sends the rest of the hit onward; the enemy answers once between the two cards.
- S OK: S's carry-over start of 5 and extra supply pay for I's costly big units in round 1; the enemy can't stop it.
- T OK: T's unit strikes when placed; I's extra-act card readies it and it strikes again; the enemy answers between.

## J: Copy picker
- Strong in: picks and copies 1.9, brings back 1.4, more cards 1.2.
- Builds on: copy my cards; pick cards from my deck. Extras: my unit goes back to hand and is placed again; my dead units come back; spent cards come back from the discard pile; create new cards.
- On the board: Place: a copy of this unit with 1 less strength appears in the same cell of my camp in the lane beside -> one deploy holds a front cell in two lanes for the supply of one. Why: two dots in two bases from one action. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Place: shuffle 2 copies of this unit into my deck. Why: the unit that holds a front cell well comes back in my next draws, so I can fill the cell again.
- On the board: Place: look through my deck and take into my hand the unit I name, for example the one that costs exactly what the lane has left. Why: the right unit for the supply left and the free front cell comes when I need it.
- Faction idea: few cards, many copies: the deck holds 3 strong units in many copies, and the pick finds the one a lane needs
- Wins: I copy my best unit and pick the card I need, so one strong card is everywhere
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Place: a copy of this unit appears in the same cell of the lane beside with the same Place hit, so one deploy strikes two blockers (NEW RULE: an effect places a unit in another lane)
- Removes back-row units: It mainly does not: each copy fires the same Place hit, so deploys hit the facing cells twice and wear the enemy stack down from the front
- Between lanes: Place: take from my deck the unit that costs exactly the leftover supply of another lane
- Holds: Place: shuffle 2 copies of this unit into my deck, so the unit that held a cell comes back in my draws
- Feels good: Place: look through my deck and take into my hand the unit I name
- Never has: a unit that attacks the turn it is placed

## K: Replay killer
- Strong in: kills outright 1.6, brings back 1.4, cheaper hand 1.2.
- Builds on: my unit goes back to hand and is placed again; destroy outright. Extras: my dead units come back; spent cards come back from the discard pile; cheaper cards; cards grow in hand.
- On the board: Place: destroy one enemy in the same row as this unit whose supply is at most this unit's. A second unit's Place takes the first back to my hand, and I place it again for 0 supply this round and it kills again -> two actions give a unit two kills. Why: a won front cell becomes a held dot without waiting for next round.
- On the board: Round end: take this unit back to my hand -> next round I place it in another lane and its Place acts there. Why: one unit's Place strikes the lane that needs it each round, and the dot it held still counted. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: A strike by this unit destroys the unit struck whatever its defence -> a 1-strength unit in a front cell holds its dot and kills the big unit that tries to cancel it.
- Faction idea: every unit has a strong Place and is cheap to place again: the faction does not hold dots by standing but by placing last each round into the front cells it just cleared
- Wins: one cheap unit is placed again and again, and each placing kills an enemy
- Removes the unit facing my dot: A strike by this unit destroys the unit struck whatever its defence, so a 1-strength unit holds its dot and kills the big unit that cancels it
- Removes back-row units: The same Place destroys one enemy in this unit's own row, so a unit placed in my back row kills an enemy in his back row
- Between lanes: A card returned to my hand has no lane, so I place it in any lane next time, paid with carry-over from cheap replays
- Holds: Round end: take this unit back to my hand and place it in another lane next round; the dot it held was already counted (NEW RULE: the return happens after the base is counted)
- Feels good: Place: destroy one enemy in the same row as this unit whose supply is at most this unit's
- Never has: hitting every enemy in a row or area

## L: Wall silencer
- Strong in: stops them 2.2, more supply 1.2, a few big units 1.3, defence 1.2.
- Builds on: take an enemy's text away; my unit guards the others. Extras: extra supply; the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; my unit acts again; my unit gains when it kills.
- On the board: Place: take away the text of the enemy in the facing front cell, its keywords and abilities stop for good. Why: it still cancels my dot but loses what made it hard to remove, so the next strike clears the cell.
- On the board: Death: take the text from every enemy in this lane. Why: a unit worth more dead than alive, the enemy's whole lane loses its keywords just when it moves in.
- On the board: Guard: while this unit stands in a front cell, enemy strikes in reach must hit it, Ranged strikes too when it is in a row in front of others -> the guard takes the hits while my other front cells keep their dots. Why: one wall unit shelters the whole lane. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: every unit is big and Immobile and the whole front row is a guard line: three walls take the hits, silence comes from the back, no unit ever moves
- Wins: big guards take every hit while silence takes away the enemy's tricks
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Place: take away the text of the enemy in the facing front cell for good, so it still cancels my dot but the next strike clears the cell
- Removes back-row units: It does not remove them: silence takes the reach from enemy Ranged units, and my guards take every strike that could reach my back rows
- Between lanes: A guard in a front cell also guards the same cell in the lane beside, so one wall shelters two lanes (NEW RULE: guard reaches another lane)
- Holds: Guard: while this unit stands in a front cell, enemy strikes in reach must hit it, so my other front cells keep their dots (NEW RULE: guard)
- Feels good: Place: this guard steps into a front cell and enemy strikes in reach must hit it
- Never has: bringing my dead units back

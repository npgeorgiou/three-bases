## A: death and return
- Strong in: many cheap units 1.4, spends them 1.9, brings back 1.6.
- Builds on: my dead units come back; Death effects. Extras: spent cards come back from the discard pile; extra small units; attach a unit to a carrier (NEW RULE); my units react when one leaves.
- On the board: Death in a front cell: I come back once into that same cell, so killing my holder doesn't free the dot the first time.
- On the board: Death: an Egg (0|1) holds my cell; at round start the Egg becomes me again, so the cell is held at both moments that count (NEW RULE: the Egg holds the dot).
- On the board: Death in my front row: 3 damage to the enemy facing me; the unit cancelling my dot dies with my holder.
- Faction idea: my dead units' tombstones hold their front cell's dot until round end (CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: my front units die and come straight back into their cells, and every death hits back.
- Removes: Death in my front row: 3 damage to the enemy facing me.
- Holds: a holder that dies comes back once into its own cell, so killing it once frees nothing.
- Feels good: Death: an Egg holds my cell, and at round start I hatch back into it.
- Never has: shields.

## T: duels
- Strong in: one chosen 1.6, kills outright 1.5, spends them 1.3.
- Builds on: my unit strikes a chosen enemy; my unit gains when it kills. Extras: my unit dies for supply, a card or strength; my units grow from deaths; Death effects; damage spells.
- On the board: Place: I strike the enemy facing me; the cancel is gone and the dot held in one action.
- On the board: When I kill, I may attack again this round: one front unit clears two cancelling enemies.
- On the board: When I kill, a 0|1 Wall of mine takes the dead enemy's cell, so he can't refill the cell facing my dot.
- Faction idea: every unit's Place strikes the facing enemy: one duel per front cell (duelyst: Lavaslasher + CLAUDE).
- Wins: every front cell is a duel I start: I'm placed, I strike the unit facing me, I take its cell.
- Removes: Place: I strike the enemy facing me; a kill lets me attack again.
- Holds: a kill leaves a Wall of mine in the enemy's cell, so he can't refill the cell facing my dot.
- Feels good: Place: I strike the facing enemy; if it dies, I attack the next one.
- Never has: area hits.

## I: big units early
- Strong in: more supply 1.6, cheaper hand 1.4, a few big units 1.8.
- Builds on: extra supply; cheaper cards. Extras: units with strength 5+; act on the enemy's hand and costs; my unit acts when hit and it survives; my unit gains when it kills.
- On the board: Round start: if I hold my dot uncancelled, +1 supply in this lane; the dot pays for the big unit that keeps it.
- On the board: I cost 1 less for each base you hold: the side holding bases lands the big unit that keeps them.
- On the board: While I'm in this lane, the enemy's deploys here cost 1 more: he can't refill a lost front cell.
- Faction idea: the back row is a mine: my back-row units give double supply but can't attack (CLAUDE, NEW RULE).
- Wins: held dots pay supply, and the supply lands big units a round before the enemy can answer.
- Removes: damage equal to the supply left in the lane.
- Holds: while my unit is in the lane, his deploys there cost 1 more, so he can't refill the cell facing my dot.
- Feels good: Round start: my uncancelled dot gives +1 supply in this lane.
- Never has: tokens.

## L: weaken and silence
- Strong in: stops them 2.2, more supply 1.2, a few big units 1.3, defence 1.2.
- Builds on: take an enemy's text away; take an enemy's strength away. Extras: extra supply; my unit guards the others; the rest of a hit goes into the unit behind; turn an enemy into another unit.
- On the board: While I'm in my front row, the enemy facing me has -2 strength: my holder survives the unit facing it.
- On the board: Place: every enemy around me loses its text for good; his front-row protections stop.
- On the board: When an enemy is placed facing my empty front cell, I move into that cell at once; his new dot is cancelled (NEW RULE: a reaction move).
- Faction idea: an enemy facing one of my front units has no text while it faces it (inscryption: Stinky + CLAUDE).
- Wins: enemies facing my front units shrink and lose their text, so my holders never lose the trade.
- Removes: the enemy facing my front unit loses strength and text for good, and stops mattering.
- Holds: while I'm in my front row the enemy facing me has -2 strength; an enemy placed facing my empty front cell finds my guard stepping in.
- Feels good: Place: every enemy around me loses its text.
- Never has: kill outright.

# Stop 1, part 1: A B C D
See `STOP1.md` for what the ratings mean.

## A: Returning dead
- Strong in: many cheap units 1.4, spends them 1.9, brings back 1.6.
- Builds on: my dead units come back; my units react when one leaves. Extras: spent cards come back from the discard pile; extra small units; attach a unit to a carrier (NEW RULE); units beside each other give each other more.
- On the board: Death: leave a small egg in the cell where this unit died -> at my round end the egg turns back into this unit and its Place acts again, so a dot I lost in that cell is mine again when the base is counted. Why: the enemy cannot answer after the round end. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Place: bring back a unit of mine that died this round from my discard pile into a free cell of any lane at no supply cost -> its Place acts again and its supply is income in that lane. Why: a lost base can be refilled in the lane that needs it.
- On the board: Leave: when my unit in a front cell dies or leaves, the unit behind it in its column steps free into that cell and gains +1 strength -> the dot is never empty for long. Why: the enemy front unit cannot hold its dot unopposed. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: every column is a queue: when the front unit dies the middle steps up, then the back steps up; the faction puts its Place-heavy units in the back row first and its front is never empty
- Wins: units die and come back, so every cell I lose is refilled and its Place acts again
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Death: an egg stays in the cell and at my round end turns back into the unit, so the dot I lost is mine again when the base is counted (NEW RULE: an egg turns into a card's unit)
- Removes back-row units: A unit that comes back fires its Place again, hitting the enemy facing it and the cell behind it
- Between lanes: A returning unit may appear in a free cell of another lane that I choose (NEW RULE: a returning unit changes lane)
- Holds: When my front unit dies or leaves, the unit behind it steps into the cell and gains +1 strength (NEW RULE: a free move made by an effect)
- Feels good: Place: bring back a unit of mine that died this round into a free cell of any lane for no supply, and its Place acts again
- Never has: damage from a spell in my hand, with no unit needed

## B: Feeding stack
- Strong in: spends them 1.7, more supply 1.5, a few big units 1.4.
- Builds on: my units grow from deaths; my unit dies for supply, a card or strength. Extras: extra supply; my unit dies and takes an enemy with it; my unit guards the others; my unit grows when it is hit and survives.
- On the board: Death: when a unit of mine dies, every unit of mine behind it in the same column gains +1 strength and +1 defence for good -> the stack behind a front unit grows as the front is lost. Why: the unit that steps into the empty front cell is stronger than the one that fell.
- On the board: Place: destroy a unit of mine in this lane -> gain supply in this lane equal to its supply, spent only there this round. Why: a weak unit becomes a big unit in the lane at once.
- On the board: A big unit costs units instead of supply: I destroy 1 or 2 of my units in its lane (a strong one counts as 2) and the big unit is placed over the cells they free -> big units come in the first round from a lane of small units. Why: one unit with many cells holds the base. (NEW RULE)
- Faction idea: every front unit has Death: this lane gets supply equal to this unit's supply this round, so the faction wants its front units to die and refills at once
- Wins: units die on purpose, and every loss makes the units behind stronger and pays for bigger ones
- Removes the unit facing my dot: The unit that steps into the empty front cell has grown from the loss and kills the enemy that took the cell
- Removes back-row units: It does not reach them directly: each stronger unit that steps up kills the one in front of it, cell by cell, until the back rows are reached
- Between lanes: Place: destroy a unit of mine in another lane to pay for this unit here (NEW RULE: a cost paid with a unit of another lane)
- Holds: Every front unit has Death: this lane gets supply equal to its supply this round, so I refill the cell at once
- Feels good: Place: destroy a unit of mine in this lane and gain its supply in this lane for this round
- Never has: taking an enemy's text away for good

## C: Row bomber
- Strong in: many at once 1.8, spends them 1.4, kills outright 1.2.
- Builds on: my unit dies and takes an enemy with it; hits on a whole row or area. Extras: my units grow from deaths; my unit dies for supply, a card or strength; I damage my own units; Death effects.
- On the board: Place or spell: fixed damage to every enemy in one row of the enemy camp in this lane, I choose front, middle or back -> a whole row is hit by one card. Why: a front-row hit clears all three dots of a base, a back-row hit removes the units that give the enemy his income.
- On the board: Spell: choose a cell of the enemy camp; every enemy in that cell and the 8 around it takes fixed damage, diagonals too -> one card takes the dots of two or three front cells. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: Round end: this unit dies and every enemy around it takes fixed damage, including the facing front cells across the base -> I give up my dot to take his dots, and the base is counted right after when he cannot answer.
- Faction idea: delayed hit: Place: choose a cell of the enemy camp; at my next round end every enemy in it takes 3 fixed damage, so the enemy must move out or lose the unit when he cannot answer
- Wins: one card hits a whole row, so I lose one unit and he loses many
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Round end: this unit dies and every enemy around it takes fixed damage, including the facing front cells, and the base is counted right after
- Removes back-row units: Place or spell: fixed damage to every enemy in the back row of this lane
- Between lanes: One higher-cost area card hits the same row in all three lanes (NEW RULE: damage crosses lanes)
- Holds: Place: choose an enemy cell; at my next round end every enemy in it takes 3 fixed damage, so he must move out or lose the unit (NEW RULE: a hit that waits for round end)
- Feels good: Place or spell: fixed damage to every enemy in one row of the enemy camp in this lane, I choose front, middle or back
- Never has: giving defence back to wounded units

## D: Spell chain
- Strong in: rewards playing spells 2.0, more cards 1.5, more supply 1.3.
- Builds on: spells make my cards stronger; create new cards. Extras: extra supply; draw extra cards; useless cards for the enemy; cheaper cards.
- On the board: When I cast a spell, my units in one row of my camp gain +1 strength until round end, and it adds up for each spell. Why: the front row is strongest exactly in the round I need the dots. (NEW RULE)
- On the board: When I cast a spell in a lane, that lane gets +1 supply until round end -> a spell pays for the next spell or unit there. Why: a lane with spells grows its supply without more units.
- On the board: Round end: for each lane where no enemy unit stands in the cells facing my units, I put a new card that was not in my deck into my hand -> an empty enemy front gives me a card. Why: holding dots through an empty enemy front gives more cards next round.
- Faction idea: spells do not use my one action: I cast a spell and still act, but each spell costs supply -> many spells in one turn
- Wins: every spell I cast makes my units and lanes stronger, so a turn of many spells wins the base
- Removes the unit facing my dot: Each spell I cast gives my units in one row of my camp +1 strength until round end, so my front unit beats the one holding the base (NEW RULE: a unit reacts to my spell)
- Removes back-row units: The card made by an empty enemy front is a free unit with Ranged reach and +1 strength for each spell this round, so it hits his back row the turn it is placed
- Between lanes: Each spell I cast in one lane gives my units in the same row of the other two lanes +1 strength until round end
- Holds: Round end: for each lane where no enemy faces my units I get a new card that was not in my deck, so a held lane feeds me
- Feels good: Place: a new 0-supply Ranged unit that was not in my deck goes into my hand
- Never has: moving enemy units out of their cells

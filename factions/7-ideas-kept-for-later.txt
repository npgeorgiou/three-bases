7. IDEAS KEPT FOR LATER (moved from summary/, 12 Oct 2026)
==================================================================
- Spells: buff strength / defence / both; bind two units (they share future buffs, effects and damage); give 2 units +1|+1; all units in the last 2 rows charge; give a unit Charge; pay a unit's supply, it deals damage to an enemy equal to its defence.
- Units: +1|+1 each round (maybe a keyword); Place: draw a card; buffs other units in the lane by 1 each time they take damage; a chain of 3 units, each placeable only if the previous one is on the board.
- Lurker (for a future faction): While this card is in your hand, when one of your units dies, it is placed for free in that unit's cells if its shape fits there (once per round per card).
- Ideas library: Bleed; Death's Door (first lethal damage leaves it alive; for a future faction); Flank (+2 strength while an ally stands directly beside it; from an earlier card list); a unit with a lot of defence while in the front row that loses almost all of it elsewhere.
- Spells cheaper for each of your units that died this round (10 Oct 2026 idea, not built): a new kind of cost effect, kept for later.
- Weakening ideas from the library (10 Oct 2026): Bleeding (damage each round for a set number of rounds), fading Poison, contagion range (enemies near plague units take more damage), Inscryption's Stinky (the facing enemy has 1 less strength while it faces the unit), Weak (deals 1 less damage with its strikes), Hearthstone's Plagues shuffled into the enemy deck.
- Future ideas from Kaptain (October 2026):
  - A future faction whose units are all Immobile.
  - Mark (keyword idea): a marked unit takes double damage from strikes; Monomachia could then also pick 0-strength units whose strikes do something.
  - Items: cards attached to units. If the unit is killed by a spell or a ranged unit, the item returns to its owner's hand; if killed by a melee unit, the item goes to that unit. Faction items give properties the faction is missing, so they are dangerous when the enemy takes them: they play for and against the factions' strengths and weaknesses.
  - The Horde needs more end-game units (its end game is weak); Tagma and the Horde will get more cards, inspired by the inspiration library.
- Future suggestions (October 2026; the Horde gets more units soon):
  - More Push units for the Horde (Kaptain wants them).
  - Horde synergy with "Whenever an ally takes damage" cards: the Horde takes a lot of damage and self-damage, so units that profit each time an ally takes damage would turn that into strength.
  - A precise, fine-tuning faction (future): its X effects let the player choose exactly how much supply to spend, as Thousand Cuts did before October 2026 (pick a unit, choose X up to your available supply, deal 1 damage X times), and its cards favour exact amounts and small adjustments over the Horde's all-in spending.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C7. TREASURY — save, then buy big (ramp + kicker)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: weak early, banks supply, then plays expensive cards or the paid-up version of cheap ones.
How it wins: survives the first rounds with cheap defenders that grow while it saves, then spends the bank on 7-10 supply units or Surplus effects that swing two lanes.
Mechanics:
- Vault (2): your carry-over cap is 2 higher while I'm in your back row. [resource_banking, round_carryover]
- Hoard (text; moved from the dropped Tempo idea): round end: I gain +1|+1 (lasting) for every 2 supply left unspent in my lane. Saving makes its holders grow. [resource_banking, permanent_growth]
- Surplus N: when you play the card, you may pay N more; then its Surplus effect happens too. Each card is a cheap play now or a strong one later. [kicker]
- Interest (text): round end: +1 carry-over if you have 3 or more. [round_carryover, resource_gain]
- Big buys: units of 7-10 supply with plain strong stats. [finisher]
Self-sufficiency: holding (Hoard units), removal (Surplus effects, big buys), supply (its core), cards (Buy Intelligence: draw 2; Surplus draws).
Weak to: early aggression, theft.
Examples:
- Vault Keeper     2 - 0|3  — Vault.
- Miser            2 - 1|3  — Hoard.
- Hired Blade      2 - 2|3  — Surplus 2: Place: I deal 2 damage to the enemy facing me.
- Moneylender      3 - 1|4  — Interest.
- Gilded Colossus  8 - 8|8.
- Windfall (1) — +2 carry-over.
Spells ~30%.

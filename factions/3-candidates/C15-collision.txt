------------------------------------------------------------------
C15. COLLISION — the lane is a battering ground (forced movement)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: pushes enemies out of the front row, where they lose their dots, and into each other, where they take damage.
How it wins: pushes enemy holders back off the front row; collisions wear down the units behind them.
Mechanics:
- Shove (text): pushes one cell in any direction (sideways, back, forward), not only back like Push. [forced_movement]
- Collision (NEW RULE): a unit pushed into a cell held by another unit doesn't move; both take 1 damage. Units only; the camp's edge stops a push without damage. [collision_damage]
- Knock-on (text): when my push collides, the unit hit is pushed one cell further in the same direction if it can move; then the damage. [collision_damage, forced_movement]
- Rams (text): Place: I move straight forward as far as I can and push the first enemy in my way (a Charge that pushes instead of striking). [on_play, forced_movement]
- Anchored (1): I can't be pushed and take no collision damage. Its own holders stay put. [immunity]
Self-sufficiency: holding (Anchored), removal (pushing off the dots, collisions), supply (Scavenge: when a collision kills, +1 supply in its lane), cards (spells that draw when a push moves 2 or more units).
Weak to: Heavy titans, Lock, full enemy back rows that stop pushes (but those collide).
Examples:
- Shover         1 - 1|1  — My strikes push the target one cell in the direction I choose.
- Anchor Guard   2 - 1|3  — Anchored.
- Battering Ram  3 - 2|3  — Push. My collisions deal 2 damage instead of 1.
- Tremor (2) — Pick a lane: every enemy there is pushed one row back.
- Head-on (1) — Push an enemy into the unit behind it.
Spells ~35%.

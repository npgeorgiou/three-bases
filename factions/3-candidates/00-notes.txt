REVIEW NOTES (11 Oct 2026): Part 3 is a list of RAW IDEAS, not a source: check every claim against summary/ section 11 and the library. Every mechanic names library concept ids in brackets (concepts table; use concept_tree for parents and children), so it can be looked up. They are Claude's mapping of its own wording: lookup handles, to be verified against real cards. They are STARTING POINTS, NOT LIMITS: widen each one through concept_tree (parents and children), cooccur and concept_relations, and add, replace or drop mechanics freely where the library shows something better. Thin entries are expected to grow. This version predates Part 5 and is to be remade in the format of Part 6. Open fixes for the remake are in todo.md.

How this list was built: in the library, a faction is never one mechanic. Each one combines two or three archetypes whose mechanics feed each other (Shadow Isles: sacrifice + graveyard + lifesteal; Duelyst's Magmar: growth + sustain; Lyonar: sustain + fortress; Gwent's Monsters: sacrifice + damage over time + swarm). So every candidate below has one overarching idea, carried by 3-5 mechanics that work together, and covers on its own the four things every Three Bases faction needs: units that hold dots, a way to remove enemies, supply, and cards.
Across the list, every faction gets a reason to use its second and back rows (healers, growers, casters, dot holders from row 2, screened strikers), because in play units pile into the front row.
Terms: defence is one number. Damage lowers it, and restoring brings it back up to what the unit had. A lasting defence loss ("loses 2 defence", as Creeping Rot's "-2 strength") lowers it for good, and restoring can't undo it.
Keyword values and example stats are suggestions; stats follow the supply budget (summary/ section 9) minus the suggested values. NEW RULE = the current rules can't do it yet. E1-E3 are the existing factions, written as if not designed yet.

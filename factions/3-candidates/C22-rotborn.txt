C22. ROTBORN — remains that feed growth (planned; part 2)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: every death leaves remains; remains hold dots, feed growth and sprout new units; the enemy starves.
How it wins: trades units into remains that keep holding the dots, grows through its camps, and lowers enemy supply with Blight.
Mechanics:
- Remains: dying units leave a Fungal Corpse (0|0, Immobile, destroyed by any damage, holds dots). [death_trigger, summon]
- Sprout (text): units placed onto remains (as Corpse Gnawer is placed onto Shades), gaining stats. [placement_rules, synergy_payoff]
- Spreading (text): masses that spread into free cells (the existing spreading rules). [summon, board_clog]
- Blight: lowers the supply of the enemy's lane. [disruption, economy]
- Feeding (text): units that eat remains for lasting growth. [sacrifice, permanent_growth]
Self-sufficiency: holding (remains), removal (masses, Blight), supply (Mycelium: +1 supply for each remains in my lane), cards (Decompose: draw a card when remains are eaten). [BREAKS DRAW RULE: fires on every eaten remains; fix in remake]
Weak to: Exile, area damage (remains die to any damage).
Examples:
- Spore Rat  1 - 2|1  — Death: leaves a Fungal Corpse.
- Sprouter   2 - 1|2  — Can be placed only onto remains, which it consumes; I gain +2|+2.
- Blighting Spores (1) — Blight an enemy unit.
Spells ~30%.

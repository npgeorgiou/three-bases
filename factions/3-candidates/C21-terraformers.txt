------------------------------------------------------------------
C21. TERRAFORMERS — shapes the cells (terrain; new idea)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: changes the cells themselves: ground that helps it in its camps, hazards in the enemy's.
How it wins: covers its camps with terrain that strengthens its units and the enemy's facing cells with hazards, so the enemy can't hold dots there.
Mechanics:
- Terrain (NEW RULE): a cell marked with a terrain that stays until replaced. Mire: units here can't move. Shrine: units here have +1 defence. Burning Ground: round end, the unit here takes 1 damage. Fertile Ground: a unit here gives +1 supply. [terrain]
- Hazards (spells; moved from Collision): put harmful terrain on enemy cells. [terrain, damage_over_time]
- Ground-lovers (text): units that gain on terrain ("+2 strength while I stand on Burning Ground; I take no damage from it"). [terrain, stat_condition]
- Shapers (text): Place: the cell I'm on, or the cell facing me, becomes a terrain. [terrain, on_play]
Self-sufficiency: holding (Shrines), removal (Burning Ground, hazards), supply (Fertile Ground in the back rows), cards (Survey: draw a card for each terrain in a lane, up to 2). [DRAW RULE: fine as a spell, not as a repeating trigger; check]
Weak to: units moved off its ground, Edicts.
Examples:
- Mire Witch    2 - 1|2  — Place: the cell facing me becomes Mire.
- Shrinekeeper  2 - 1|3  — Place: my cell becomes a Shrine.
- Firewalker    3 - 3|2  — +2 strength while I stand on Burning Ground; I take no damage from it.
- Scorched Earth (2) — The 3 cells of an enemy front row become Burning Ground.
Spells ~40%.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C11. MEASURERS — exact numbers (precision + control)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: picks exact amounts and rewrites stats; rewarded for exactness and balance.
How it wins: neutralises the enemy's biggest units by setting or swapping their stats, kills with exact amounts, holds with balanced units.
Mechanics:
- Choose X: X spells and units, X picked up to your available supply. [kicker, variable_stats]
- Set: set a unit's strength or defence to N (this round cheaply, lasting for more). [variable_stats, debuff]
- Swap: swap a unit's strength and defence. Your 6|1 attacker becomes a 1|6 holder; the enemy's 1|6 wall becomes a 6|1 target. [stat_swap]
- Exact (2): if my strike kills with nothing left over, draw a card. [excess_damage, draw]
- Balance (text): a bonus while my strength equals my defence. [stat_condition]
Self-sufficiency: holding (Swap, Balance), removal (Set, exact strikes), supply (Exact Change: an action that leaves exactly 0 supply in its lane gives +1 carry-over), cards (Exact).
Weak to: many targets, copies.
Examples:
- Gauger        2 - 2|1  — Exact.
- Scale-Keeper  3 - 2|2  — While my strength equals my defence, I have +1|+1 and Armoured.
- Measured Cut (X) — Deals X damage to a unit.
- Invert (1) — Swap a unit's strength and defence this round.
- Equalise (2) — Set an enemy unit's strength to 1 this round.
Spells ~50%.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C12. MONOLITHS — never moves, owns its columns (every unit Immobile; Kaptain's idea)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: every unit is Immobile and sturdier for it; its units reach down whole columns and stop the enemy moving.
How it wins: places each monolith once, in the right cell; they strike the whole facing enemy column and lock the lane, so the enemy can't fix its line.
Mechanics:
- Immobile on every unit (its -2 gives each unit +2 stats). [drawback, movement]
- Column Reach (2): my strikes reach every cell of my column in the enemy camp. A back-row monolith attacks the enemy's back rows. [ranged, lanes]
- Lock (text): enemies in my lane can't move. [disable, zone_of_control]
- Foundations (text): +1|+1 for each adjacent Immobile ally. Monoliths are built in blocks. [adjacency, link_formation]
- Placement as movement: Place effects that let the next unit you play this round be placed in any lane or any cell, the faction's only way to choose position late. [placement_rules, cross_lane]
Self-sufficiency: holding (sturdy), removal (Column Reach), supply (Quarry: Supply +1), cards (Inscription: Place: draw a card if I'm adjacent to two allies).
Weak to: units displaced to other lanes, pushes.
Examples:
- Standing Stone  1 - 1|4.
- Keystone        3 - 3|4  — Foundations.
- Obelisk         3 - 2|5  — Column Reach.
- Warden Stone    4 - 2|6  — Lock.
- Seal the Lane (1) — Pick a lane: enemies there can't move this round.
Spells ~30%.

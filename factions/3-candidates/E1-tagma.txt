------------------------------------------------------------------
E1. TAGMA — the Byzantine army (all-round)
------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: VERY MUCH up for BIG redesign. The current cards (summary/ section 11) are a starting point only; any of them can be changed or dropped. Do not describe Tagma from its current cards: they lean defensive only because of how earlier card design went. Tagma is NOT a defence faction (Kaptain, 11 Oct 2026).
Identity (Kaptain, 11 Oct 2026): an all-round faction, good at everything, flashy at nothing, with the character of a thousand years of East Roman (Byzantine) military, social and spiritual history:
- Adaptable: it changed its army to fit each enemy; mixed troops working together.
- Mercenaries: it hired foreign units (Varangians and others), strong but bought, with their own terms.
- Diplomacy: it won wars without battles: bribes, alliances, turning enemies against each other.
- Logistics: themes, supply trains, fortified roads; armies that arrived fed and on time.
- Strategy: master strategists who wrote manuals (Strategikon, Taktika); plans, stratagems, the right answer prepared in advance.
- Faith: deep spirituality and religiosity; priests, icons and relics with the army. "En touto nika" (in this sign, conquer).
Mechanic directions (Claude's suggestions; the remake picks 3-5 as core, the rest become splash or flavour; library concept ids in brackets):
- Adaptability / combined arms: rewards for mixing different unit roles; cards that choose between modes. [variety_payoff, modal_choice, synergy_payoff, adjacency]
- Mercenaries: strong units bought with supply, carrying terms (upkeep, desertion, a price). [upkeep, drawback, resource_conversion]
- Diplomacy: winning over or pacifying enemy units; bargains. [steal, cant_attack, disable, enemy_benefit]
- Logistics: supply, cheap repositioning, carry-over planning. [resource_gain, round_carryover, movement, rows]
- Strategy: set up now, pay off later; finding the prepared answer (a candidate for the deck-search signature, pending the allocation table). [delayed, tutor, reactions, combo_sequence]
- Faith: blessings, restoring, protection, faith that grows while you hold the lane's base. [heal, protection, aura, buff, territory_trigger]

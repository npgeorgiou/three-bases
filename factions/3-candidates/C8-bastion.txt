------------------------------------------------------------------
C8. BASTION — the unbreakable line (fortress + punishment)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: you can't break through it, and trying hurts.
How it wins: holds dots with walls and screened units, kills attackers with defence-based strikes; slow, wins by holding two bases through rounds.
Mechanics:
- Palisades: tokens 0|3, Immobile, supply 0, made in front-row cells; they hold dots. [summon, blocking]
- Screen (2): I can't be struck while an ally stands directly in front of me. Strikers and supply sit safe in rows 2-3 behind the walls. [protection, rows]
- Hold (2): enemies placed or moved into the cells facing me can't attack this round. [on_enemy_enter, cant_attack]
- Brace (1): my strikes use my defence instead of my strength. Its high-defence units are its attackers. [stat_swap]
- Unbroken (text): round end: if I took no damage this round, I gain +1 defence (lasting). [permanent_growth, turn_end]
Self-sufficiency: holding, removal (Brace), supply (Garrison: Supply +1 for each Palisade in my lane, up to 2), cards (Fortify: draw a card and make a Palisade).
Weak to: lasting defence loss, units displaced to other lanes, control.
Examples:
- Wallwright     2 - 1|2  — Place: a Palisade in a free cell of your front row.
- Gate Sentinel  3 - 2|3  — Hold.
- Tower Shield   3 - 1|5  — Brace.
- Raise the Walls (2) — Pick a lane: a Palisade in each free cell of your front row there.
Spells ~20%.

==================================================================
4. KAPTAIN'S IDEAS (11 Oct 2026, written while reading Runeterra's Noxus and Faeria's red cards; not all for the Horde)
==================================================================
- unit: Deal double damage to large units (more than 1 cell)
- Keyword: only units with 3 or more strength can hit me
- Units that give keywords to lane/row/adjacent, according to what is best for the placement of the units that the faction mechanics dictate.
- Spells that give keywords, or only give them for one round if they are too strong. Also can give them permanently, but they are more expensive. These faction spells give faction-relevant keywords.
- Units whose actions, placement, etch, give bonuses to other units of the same type.
- spell: Kill a unit that is (X,Y,Z) and create a copy of same spell in hand.
- spells that give a variety of str|def bonuses to a variety of unit numbers.
- units that give bonuses when a unit survives damage
- units that do stuff when they survive damage
- Units that drag opponent to first cell if possible, and strike it.
- Units that summon the lowest cost unit in the opponents hand and strike each other, if placed on first lane, maybe gaining a bonus on kill
- Horde needs some cards that also give def, with a sacrifice of course, but dont be afraid to add cards, generally speaking, for each faction, that give something maybe too strong and without an obvious drawback, if it means that it alleviates for some faction weeknesses.
- Units that when summoned they create spells
- spells that create copies of cards in hand. Actually I have not seen any of your factions playing with the ideas of creating copies of cards, own or enemies, of being focused on drawing cards, of searching in the deck to pick cards, and nifty things like that.
- I also like the idea of a faction enhancing cards.
- Do you have the idea of freeze/stun in the game? 
- units that do something when you cast spells, this can be something to be added in most factions.
- Also, dont be afraid to use concepts/keywords that mostly belong to one faction, also in another. Just make sure to use them VERY sparingly, and only if it somehow helps the faction mechanic. For example a HIT HIT HIT faction, could on summon of a unit, also resurrect the strongest dead unit (see, HIT HIT -> STRONGEST).
- units that do something when they are the target of spells
- Units that when they die they create another unit in hand, this can go on like a BABUSKA a few times
- Units that on round end do a strike-each-other with enemies
- Units that on summon deal a good amount of damage (3?) to friend and enemy
- An xpensive ranged unit that also damaged adjacent units
- More hit and push units for horde, to make their vulnerable front line units harder to reach, as the enemy would need to spent supply to move AND hit.
- spell cards that are cast on own units and activate the THEN part of a card or keyword that has the structure IF -> THEN
- Units that on hit or attach gain permanently 1/2 in own produced supply
- cards that can reduce the cost of other cards
- units that on placement can throw units to other cell in own lane
- spells that deal damage of various amount, and inv arioso costs per faction, according to faction profile, and in various split configurations. Of course not all factions should ahve pure damage spells, but this holds for everything, not every faction can have everything
- Some way for Horde to gain def as well, some small ways.
- Also Horde gaining things from enemy deaths
- An expensive but weak unit that gains +1|+1, in hand or in play, every time you summon another unit that fulfills a faction-specific role
- Units that deal damage ON PLACEMENT
- Damage that travels in reduced ways from unit to unit, if the unit is killed. Maybe only to edge-connected units
- Damage that travels in reduced ways from unit to unit, like a chain lightning spell
- Units that are mirroring back the damage they get
- Unit that gives you supply when you summon it. Can be both weak, mid, or strong.
- Units that on placement gain if the enemy has this or that, or more that this of X or Y, again playing with faction signature.
- Units that on placement draw some card that works well with them, or the conditions this unit is expected to operate in
- spells that on cast, create other spells in hand or deck
- spell to give a creature ranged, permanently or for this round
- units that on hit also damage all of their own adjacent enemies for some amount

Ideas for a bigger change of rules (under discussion):
- R1. Cards (strong big-ticket units, or cheap ones) that gain something while you hold a base; at most 3 such cards per deck, any mix; what they give builds on the faction's mechanics.
- R2. Owning a dot also gives +1 supply.
- Planned: each base offers a different bonus to the side that holds it.

==================================================================
11. THE SEVEN FACTIONS OF 4 NOV 2026 (Claude's design, built through the chain; in the game for testing)
==================================================================
Asked by Kaptain: a clockwork, a death (not the Exanimati), an aggro, a defensive, a spell, an all-Immobile and a World War I
artillery faction. Each one has a stage 1 entry in factions/designs/<faction>.txt (name, icon, theme with 10+
characteristics, one-line identity, 5 mechanics with library leads, how they feed each other, 3 keywords, groups,
champions, strong/weak, self-sufficiency, premade decks) and its card lines in factions/designs/<faction>-cards.txt.
Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-<faction>.js. The old drafts in factions/designs/ (brokers, glyphwrights, hunters,
ironfront, lodestones) were not used. All older factions (Tagma, Horde, Exanimati and the five of 3 Nov 2026) were removed from the game on 4 Nov 2026.

Checks run on every faction: tools/network_check.py on the entry, the cards and both premade decks (0 problems each;
champions have more partners than the average card and gather different cards), tools/fxtest.js (the new keywords),
tools/simtest.js (100 random games, no errors), tools/smoke.js, tools/cardstats.js (Benchmark report, below).

HOROLOGERS (c, clockface icon) — 17th-18th century clockmakers' guilds and automata.
  Machines act by themselves at round end; winding makes them act again during the round; broken machines become Spare
  Parts that wind the next one. Keywords: Geared, Wound, Salvage. Mechanics: striking the hour, winding, gearing,
  salvage, the workings (back rows). Champions: Master Horologer, The Grand Orrery, Mistress of Salvage, The Great Clock
  Tower, Brass Colossus. Decks: The Gear Train, The Scrap Yard. 108 cards.
SUNFEEDERS (d, sun-skull icon) — the Mexica world: flower wars, temple steps, the Day of the Dead.
  Every death feeds them: cuts wound, Obsidian finishes the wounded, Offerings give their own, the Toll grows with the
  deaths of the round. Keywords: Toll, Obsidian, Offering. Champions: Jaguar Knight, High Priestess of the Steps, Keeper
  of the Skull Rack, Lady of the Marigold Path, The Sun-Eater. Decks: The Cutting Rain, The Temple Steps. 103 cards.
IMPI (a, hide-shield icon) — the Zulu regiments.
  Fast cheap warriors attack in waves from round 1; the horns hit from the side columns, kills bring cattle (supply) and
  more attacks, the reserves run in to fill each fallen warrior's cell. Keywords: Onrush, Flanker, Reserve. Champions:
  Induna of the Wave, Horn Captain, Old Bull of the Loins, Praise Singer, Spear of the King. Decks: The Wave, Horns and
  Loins. 104 cards.
HOSPICE KNIGHTS (b, eight-pointed cross icon) — the hospital orders in a besieged island fortress.
  Few tough knights outlast the enemy: blows they survive harden them or are struck back, the hospital restores them,
  sorties hit arrivals, and relief comes while they hold the base. Keywords: Harden, Retaliate N, Vigil N. Champions:
  Castellan of the Bastion, Knight of the Sortie, Infirmarian Mother, The Grand Master, Relief Galley. Decks: The Bastion,
  The Thorn Hedge. 101 cards.
STAR PRIESTS (s, eight-pointed star icon) — the astrologer-priests of Babylon.
  Spells are the army: units react to spells, Omens are fulfilled when a spell targets them, lesser tablets put greater
  ones into the deck, back-row priests give the spells power. Keywords: Omen, Augur N (Tablet is a name word). Champions:
  High Astrologer, Bull-Man of the Gate, Chief Scribe, King's Seer, The Star of the Seven. Decks: Fire from the Ziggurat,
  The Omen Guards. 101 cards (41% spells).
ROOTKIN (o, oak icon) — a living sacred forest; every unit is Immobile (Kaptain's idea).
  Nothing moves: trees grow at each round end, seed Saplings, feed their neighbours when they fall and strike up through
  their roots from any row. Keywords: Perennial N, Seeds N, Roots N. Champions: Grove Druid, Mother of Saplings, Deep
  Root, The Eldest Oak, Wandering Seed-Wind. Decks: The Thicket, The Old Grove. 101 cards.
THE LONG GUNS (l, shell icon) — the artillery of the Western Front.
  Heavy guns in the back rows bombard the enemy's rear at round end, observers mark targets (Kaptain's Mark idea:
  strikes on a Marked unit deal double damage), dug-in infantry holds the front, and the infantry goes over the top when
  enemies die. Keywords: Marked, Barrage N, Entrenched. Champions: Chief Observer, Trench Major, Quartermaster General,
  The Great Howitzer, The Railway Gun. Decks: The Grand Battery, Spot and Strike. 101 cards.

NEW RULES these factions brought: built, written in summary/08-rulings.txt and summary/10-keywords.txt.
REWORK (Kaptain's points of 4 Nov 2026, START_DESIGN "Rosters"): Place effects on 40-50% of each faction's units and Death
effects on 10-15%; cost 1-4 cards already combine; 6+ units use what the lane has set up (each entry's THE CURVE section
names them); removal / disruption / protection levels are in each entry. Scenarios of the seven factions:
three-bases-scenarios.html (src/scenarios/scenarios.html), two boards per faction.
COSTING NOTE (Benchmark, 4 Nov 2026): after the runs most units sit 1 defence above the supply-to-stats budget of summary/
section 9 (all units of six factions got +1 defence; the Rootkin trees with 3+ defence lost 1). Kaptain decides whether the
budget itself should change or the factions go back to it with other fixes.

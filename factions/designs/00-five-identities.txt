==================================================================
THE FIVE NEW FACTIONS, FROM ZERO (3 Nov 2026; Claude's proposal, stage 0: identities only, for Kaptain to check)
==================================================================
They replace the five in the game once each is designed (START_DESIGN 2b stages). Each line: who they are, the traits
their mechanics come from, how they win, strong at / weak at, and how they contrast with the others.

1. SIEGEWRIGHTS — a guild of engineers and gunners (Kaptain's Projectiles idea).
   Traits: patient, engineered, explosive, thrifty, rooted.
   Win: build a battery in the back rows, hold the front with powder kegs, then fire shot into the enemy camp; shot that
   lands in a free cell stays there and takes the enemy's dot.
   Strong: back-row damage, holding the front cheaply, units in the enemy camp. Weak: own movement, healing, big melee
   bodies, a fast start. Contrast: the slowest start, the strongest set-up turns; the Hunters punish it early.
   Full stage 1: factions/designs/siegewrights.txt.

2. LODESTONES — a stone people with the pull of the earth (Kaptain's all-Immobile idea, joined with forced movement).
   Traits: unmoving, heavy, attracting, crushing, ancient.
   Win: their monuments never move; they move the ENEMY: pull back-row enemies forward to face the monuments, crush
   what cannot move further, tax what stands in front of them.
   Strong: forced movement (signature), round-end damage to what faces them. Weak: own movement and lane changes,
   card draw, spells that buff. Contrast: they move the enemy while the Siegewrights move nothing.

3. GLYPHWRIGHTS — spellcasters who write magic into the ground.
   Traits: scholarly, positional, lingering, transforming, growing.
   Win: spells are cast THROUGH their units (where the caster stands decides what the spell hits), leave glyphs in the
   cells they touch, and grow into stronger spells in the deck; their apprentices become spells when they die.
   Strong: back-row reach, card creation (signature). Weak: holding dots with bodies, defence gains, big units.
   Contrast: units and spells turn into each other; every other faction keeps them apart.

4. BROKERS — smugglers and dealers who live off the enemy.
   Traits: greedy, sly, well-informed, bargaining, opportunist.
   Win: tax the enemy's actions for supply, take copies of the enemy's cards and play them, slip worthless goods into
   the enemy deck.
   Strong: interaction (the highest), stealing and seeing cards (signature). Weak: raw damage, big units, defence.
   Contrast: the only faction whose plan runs on the enemy's cards and actions.

5. HUNTERS — a hunting people who chase the weak.
   Traits: fast, hungry, pack, tracking, trophy-taking.
   Win: mark prey, kill it, and grow the lane's supply from trophies while the enemy's lane loses its income; back-row
   chargers strike from far behind (Kaptain's Charge-from-the-back idea).
   Strong: early pressure, removal of weak units, supply from kills (signature). Weak: defence, late game, spells,
   holding after a lost fight. Contrast: the fastest; "the enemy with the least defence" belongs only here.

Shared hooks for two-faction decks later (each faction answers them its own way, Strixhaven-style): a unit dies, a unit
enters the enemy camp, an enemy is moved, a spell is cast.

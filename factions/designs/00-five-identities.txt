==================================================================
THE FIVE NEW FACTIONS, FROM ZERO (3 Nov 2026; Claude's proposal, stage 1 for Kaptain to check)
==================================================================
Each has its own file in factions/designs/ with its theme (12 traits), 5 mechanics (each a give-and-take), the web the
network check reads, ways to play, strong/only a little/weak, and library leads. They replace the five in the game once
each is designed.

1. IRONFRONT (World War I) — ironfront.txt. Hold, then release · Attrition · The front wears, the rear recovers · Fire
   from the rear (the few Projectiles, two kinds) · No man's land. Slow, holds the front, deep-row damage.
2. LODESTONES (Neolithic megaliths; all Immobile) — lodestones.txt. The world comes to me (they move the enemy) · Crush
   (and Topple) · Alignment (columns across lanes) · The turning year · The ancestors' ground. Control, late game.
3. GLYPHWRIGHTS (Daoist talisman masters) — glyphwrights.txt. Yin and yang · Talismans (spells stuck on units, burned
   later) · Paper and ink (units and spells turn into each other) · Qi flows (along touching units) · The master's
   lesson (spells that improve themselves in the deck). Spells, card creation.
4. BROKERS (1920s Prohibition gangs) — brokers.txt. Debt · Protection (the enemy's actions pay you) · Informants (see and
   steal the enemy's cards) · Laundering · The crew. The most interaction.
5. WILD HUNT (folklore's Wild Hunt) — hunters.txt. The quarry (Mark) · Blood trail (wound, then finish) · Trophies
   (kills grow the lane's supply) · The horn (many hunters at once, back-row riders) · Hunger. The fastest.

CONTRAST: Ironfront waits, the Hunt never waits; the Lodestones move the enemy, Ironfront barely moves; the Glyphwrights
fight with spells, the Hunt with strikes; the Brokers live off the enemy's actions, the Lodestones deny him actions.
SIGNATURES: forced movement (Lodestones), card creation (Glyphwrights), stealing and seeing cards (Brokers), supply from
the enemy (Brokers, Hunt), Mark (Hunt), Projectiles (Ironfront, a few cards; usable by others).
SHARED HOOKS for two-faction decks later: a unit dies, a unit is moved, an enemy is Bloodied, a spell is cast, an enemy
acts. Each faction answers them its own way.
NEW RULES these need (for Kaptain): Mark; Yin/Yang kinds and "your last card"; spells attached to units; cards given
to the enemy; marked (sacred) cells; Projectile and its value.

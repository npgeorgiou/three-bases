NAME AND ICON
The Hospice Knights. Icon: an eight-pointed cross (new glyph "eightcross"). Colour: white on deep red.

THEME: the military orders of hospital knights, at the great siege of an island fortress in the 16th century.
1. They are monks and knights at once: vows, prayers, the hours of the day.
2. The order began as a hospital: the care of the wounded is its first duty.
3. They hold star-shaped forts with bastions and curtain walls, built to outlast any siege.
4. A siege is won by enduring: the attacker spends himself on the walls until relief arrives.
5. Cisterns, granaries and stores: a fortress lives on what it saved.
6. Sorties: when the enemy comes close, the knights ride out of the gate and strike.
7. Relics and saints protect the faithful; prayer hardens the spirit.
8. Iron discipline: a knight holds his post until relieved.
9. Each blow taken on the shield is answered: the knights strike back at whoever hits them.
10. They are few: every knight is precious, expensive and hard to replace.
11. They fear starvation and betrayal; they lack numbers, speed and far-reaching weapons.
12. Relief comes by sea: as long as the fortress holds, reinforcements arrive.

IDENTITY (one line)
Few, tough knights who hold the dots by outlasting the enemy: every blow they survive hardens them or is struck back, the
hospital restores them, and as long as they hold a base, relief keeps arriving.

MECHANICS (each "I do X -> I benefit from Y")
1. Enduring: my units survive damage -> they harden (gain defence) or grow. The keyword Harden: when I survive damage, I gain
   +1 defence. Cards also react when an ally in their lane survives damage. Library: on_damaged (Monster Train Hellhorned
   34%, Stygian Guard; Demacia 17%; Inscryption Technology).
2. Striking back: I am hit -> the attacker pays. The keyword Retaliate N: when I survive damage from an enemy unit, I deal
   N damage to it (works against Ranged attacks and ability damage too, unlike Riposte). Library: counterattack (Monster
   Train Awoken spikes), Riposte in this game.
3. The hospital: I restore shields -> healed units gain (strength, defence, cards). Shields come back at round end (the
   keyword Vigil N) and from spells; cards react to it ("whenever my shields are restored"). Library: regeneration
   (Freljord, Lyonar), heal triggers (Duelyst Lyonar).
4. The sortie: enemies arrive in my lane -> my knights strike or deal damage to them as they are placed. Library:
   on_enemy_enter (Inscryption Technology sentries, 7%).
5. Relief: I hold this lane's base -> at round end and on Place I get more (a Novice token, shields, carry-over). Lane
   control is this faction's own kind of card: many cards check "as long as you hold this lane's base". Claude's idea,
   built on the agreed lane-control conditions.

HOW THE MECHANICS FEED EACH OTHER
- Enduring needs units that survive: the hospital restores them, so they survive the next blow too.
- Striking back makes every enemy attack cost the enemy shields; the sortie hits enemies as they arrive, so they arrive
  weakened and die to the striking back.
- Relief needs the base held; the hardened, healed front holds it; relief tokens give more units that endure.
MECHANIC Enduring | makes: hardened-front, base-held | needs: survives-damage, shields-restored
MECHANIC Striking back | makes: enemy-damaged, base-held | needs: survives-damage
MECHANIC The hospital | makes: shields-restored, survives-damage | needs: damaged-ally
MECHANIC The sortie | makes: enemy-damaged, damaged-ally | needs: enemy-arrives
MECHANIC Relief | makes: survives-damage, enemy-arrives | needs: base-held, hardened-front

KEYWORDS OF ITS OWN
- Harden (value 1; measured: at 2 the faction lost almost every Benchmark game): when I survive damage, I gain +1 defence.
- Retaliate N (value N): when I survive damage from an enemy unit, I deal N damage to it.
- Vigil N (value N): round end: restores N of my shields.
Shared keywords used: Armoured, Bodyguard, Riposte (a few), Immobile (bastions).

GROUPS
1. The bastion: Harden and Armoured knights in the front, Bodyguards, round-end Vigil. Champion: Castellan of the Bastion
   (Harden; allies in its row have +1 defence; links to relief).
2. The thorn hedge: Retaliate knights and sortie units that make every enemy action cost. Champion: Knight of the Sortie
   (Retaliate 2; whenever an enemy is placed in its lane, deals 1 damage to it; links to the bastion).
3. The hospital: healers in rows 2-3, units that gain when their shields are restored. Champion: Infirmarian Mother
   (round end: restores 2 shields to each damaged ally; links to enduring).
Two more champions: The Grand Master (as long as you hold this lane's base, allies here have +1|+1; links relief and the
bastion) and Relief Galley (Place and round end, if you hold this lane's base: a Novice in your front row; between
lanes: also in the lane with the fewest of your units).

STRONG / ONLY A LITTLE / WEAK
- Strong: holding dots and bases, defence, punishing attacks, healing.
- Only a little: removing enemies on its own turn (few spells deal damage; Sortie Charge, Judgement of the Order), draw.
- Weak: fast starts (expensive units), the enemy's back rows (one Ranged unit, no back-row damage), swarms of cheap units.
- No card gives it Ranged armies or damage to the enemy back rows.

INTERACTION WITH THE ENEMY: medium. It reacts to the enemy's attacks and placements, rarely acts first.

SELF-SUFFICIENCY
- Holds dots: the whole faction.
- Removes: Retaliate, Riposte, sortie damage, Judgement of the Order (spell: an ally deals damage equal to its defence
  to the enemy facing it), Lance Charge.
- Supply: Granary (Supply +1), Cistern Keeper, relief carry-over (Victualler: round end, if you hold this lane's base,
  +1 carry-over).
- Draw: Book of Hours (spell: draw 2), Almoner (Place: draw 1 if an ally in its lane is damaged).
- Lane control: the relief mechanic (Relief Galley, Grand Master, Sea Watch: if you don't hold this lane's base, round
  end: restore all shields of allies in its row).
- Between lanes: Relief Galley (round end, as long as you hold this lane's base: a Novice in another lane), Signal Bell
  (spell, Everywhere: restores 1 shield to each of your units in every lane).
- Hooks for two-faction decks: Harden and the hospital work on any unit (Tagma's Shield wall units, the Rootkin's trees);
  Retaliate punishes anyone.

ROWS 2-3: the hospital (healers that restore the front), chaplains (allies in front gain), the one Ranged crossbow knight.
FREQUENCY: Harden, Retaliate and heal triggers fire on every hit (stats and damage only, so unbounded is fine); draw only
on spells, Place, once per round.

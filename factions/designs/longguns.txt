THE LONG GUNS (the World War I artillery faction) — stage 1 entry, 4 Nov 2026 (Claude's design). Cards: longguns-cards.txt
(made from the game: node tools/design_cards.js l). In the game: src/game/js/cards/new-longguns.js. Network check:

NAME AND ICON
The Long Guns. Icon: an artillery shell standing upright (new glyph "shell"). Colour: field grey with brass.

THEME: the artillery of the Western Front, 1914-1918.
1. The guns stand miles behind the line and fire at targets they never see.
2. Forward observers in the front trenches spot targets and telephone the range back: a spotted target is hit true.
3. The barrage falls on the enemy's rear: batteries, supply dumps, reserves (counter-battery fire).
4. The creeping barrage walks forward in front of the infantry, row by row.
5. Shells must be hauled from depots by rail and horse: no shells, no barrage.
6. The infantry live in trenches: dug in, supported from the trench behind, they survive the shelling.
7. Going over the top: after the barrage, the infantry leave the trench and attack.
8. Gas shells: a cloud that lingers and wears the enemy down.
9. Attrition: the battle is won by grinding the enemy down, round after round.
10. Railway guns move between sectors along the lines.
11. Mud and barbed wire: nothing moves fast; the line hardly moves at all.
12. They fear being spotted and shelled in turn; they lack speed, and their infantry is ordinary.

IDENTITY (one line)
Heavy guns in the back rows bombard the enemy's rear at every round end while dug-in infantry holds the front; observers
mark targets so every strike on them hits twice as hard, and the infantry goes over the top once the barrage has
broken the enemy.

MECHANICS (each "I do X -> I benefit from Y")
1. Spotting: my observers mark enemies -> marked enemies take double damage from strikes (Kaptain's Mark idea: Marked
   lasts until the unit leaves the board). Marks come from observers' Place effects, from flares (spells) and from
   attacks of spotter planes. Library: mark_target (Monster Train Stygian Guard, Freljord Frostbite-style marks).
2. The barrage: my guns in rows 2-3 hit the enemy's rear -> the keyword Barrage N: round end, deals N damage to the enemy
   furthest from the base in my lane (back row first). Guns that are Ranged attack marked targets for double. Library:
   Into the Breach artillery (Ranged squad), turn_end damage.
3. Trenches: my infantry stand in the front row with an ally behind them -> they are Armoured (the keyword Entrenched:
   while in my front row with an ally directly behind me, I am Armoured). The second trench line gives the back rows a
   second job. Claude's idea (Armoured in this game; positional bonuses from the library: Demacia, Faeria).
4. Shells: depots make Shell cards -> Shells are cheap spells (deal 2 damage to an enemy; 4 to a marked one), and guns
   grow with the Shells you hold. Library: generate_cards (Piltover & Zaun), items_consumables.
5. Over the top: enemies in my lane die or are Marked -> my front infantry gain and charge (when an enemy in my lane dies,
   front units with "Over the top" gain +1 strength this round; Charge units placed in row 2-3 run in). Library:
   other_death_trigger (Shadow Isles), Charge in this game.

HOW THE MECHANICS FEED EACH OTHER
- Spotting doubles the strikes of Ranged guns and infantry; Shells deal more to marked units.
- The barrage kills the enemy's back rows, and every enemy death sends the infantry over the top.
- Trenches need an ally behind: the guns in row 2 are that ally; so the guns protect the trench and the trench protects
  the dots while the guns fire.
- Shells come from depots and observers; guns and infantry use them.

MECHANIC Trenches | makes: dug-in-front, observer-placed | needs: gun-behind
MECHANIC Shells | makes: shell-in-hand, enemy-dies | needs: enemy-marked
MECHANIC Over the top | makes: enemy-dies, observer-placed | needs: enemy-dies, dug-in-front

KEYWORDS OF ITS OWN
- Marked (a state, like Bloodied, given by cards): strikes on me deal double damage. Kaptain's idea.
- Barrage N (value N + 1; it reaches the back row from any row): round end, deals N damage to the enemy furthest from the
  base in my lane.
- Entrenched (value 1): while I am in my front row with an ally directly behind me, I am Armoured.
Shared keywords used: Ranged (field guns), Charge (assault troops), Immobile (heavy guns and railway guns), Pike (bayonets).

GROUPS
1. The grand battery: Barrage guns in rows 2-3, depots making Shells, counter-battery. Champion: The Great Howitzer (2x2,
   Immobile, Barrage 3; when a Shell is cast in its lane, deals 1 damage to the deepest enemy; links to spotting).
2. Spot and strike: observers, flares, Ranged field guns that strike marked enemies for double. Champion: Chief Observer
   (Place: marks the enemy facing it and the deepest enemy; links to the battery).
3. The trench line: Entrenched infantry in front of guns, over-the-top units that gain when enemies die. Champion:
   Trench Major (other allies in its row have +1 strength; when an enemy in its lane dies, allies in its row gain +1
   strength this round; links to the battery, because the guns kill).
Two more champions: The Railway Gun (Immobile, Barrage 2; between lanes: round end, also deals 1 damage to the deepest
enemy of each other lane) and Quartermaster General (Place: two Shells into your hand; round end: if you hold this lane's
base, +1 carry-over).

STRONG / ONLY A LITTLE / WEAK
- Strong: damage to the enemy's back rows, round-end attrition, holding the front with Entrenched infantry.
- Only a little: drawing (Field Telephone), healing (Aid Post), between lanes (railway guns).
- Weak: speed (expensive guns, slow starts), moving, the enemy's Armoured units (the barrage is many small hits),
  swarms in the front row (barrage hits one unit at a time).
- No card gives it Outrider, Lightfooted or mass healing.

INTERACTION WITH THE ENEMY: medium. Marks and shells are aimed; the barrage picks its own target.

SELF-SUFFICIENCY
- Holds dots: Entrenched infantry with a gun behind; sandbag lines.
- Removes: the barrage, Shells, Ranged guns on marked targets.
- Supply: Munitions Depot (Supply +1; round end: a Shell into your hand, once per round), Light Railway (Supply +1).
- Draw: Field Telephone (Place: draw 1 if there is a Marked enemy in its lane), Orders from the Staff (spell: draw 2).
- Lane control: Sector Command (round end: if you hold this lane's base, deals 1 damage to the deepest enemy here),
  Desperate Defence (spell: if you don't hold this lane's base, allies here gain +2
  defence; else +1).
- Between lanes: The Railway Gun, Rail Transfer (spell: an ally rides to another lane), Long-range Shell (spell: deals 3
  damage to an enemy in ANY lane; extremely rare kind).
- Hooks for two-faction decks: Marked doubles every faction's strikes; Entrenched works with any ally behind; Shells
  are spells (the Star Priests' spell triggers).

ROWS 2-3: the guns (Barrage, Ranged field guns) and the second trench line that keeps the front Entrenched.
FREQUENCY: Barrage once per round end per gun; Shell making once per round per unit; draws on Place or spells.

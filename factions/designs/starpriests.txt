NAME AND ICON
The Star Priests. Icon: an eight-pointed star in a circle (new glyph "astar"). Colour: lapis blue with gold.

THEME: the astrologer-priests of Babylon (the 7th-6th century BC temples and ziggurats).
1. They read omens: "if the moon is red, then the king wins" — every sign has a meaning.
2. Incantations are written on clay tablets and recited in a fixed series, one tablet after another.
3. Scribes copy the tablets; a library of tablets is the temple's treasure.
4. The ziggurat is an observatory: priests watch the sky from its high terraces.
5. The king consults the priests before every battle; the army follows the omen.
6. Exorcists drive out demons with spells and amulets.
7. The constellations: seven sacred stars, a night of many signs is a night of power.
8. Charms and amulets are laid on people to protect them; a protected man is changed.
9. Lesser rites lead to greater ones: a first tablet opens the way to the next.
10. They fear eclipses (an omen of a king's death) and broken tablets.
11. They lack soldiers: temple guards are few and weak; the priests win with words.
12. Sacred numbers (7, 60) and exact calendars order every rite.

IDENTITY (one line)
Priests whose spells are their army: every spell cast makes their temple units act, spells laid on their own units fulfil
those units' omens, and lesser tablets put greater ones into the deck; they win by burning the enemy's holders while
their guards hold the dots.

MECHANICS (each "I do X -> I benefit from Y")
1. Incantation: I cast spells -> my units react (gain stats, deal damage once per round, make tablets). Library: on_spell
   (Monster Train Stygian Guard 22%, Piltover & Zaun 14%, Duelyst Songhai, Slay the Spire Defect).
2. Omens: I target my own unit with a spell -> its omen is fulfilled (the keyword Omen: "Omen: ..." happens when one of
   your spells targets me). Cheap spells that target allies exist to fulfil omens. Library: on_targeted (Targon 15%,
   Shurima 14%) and Kaptain's idea "spells cast on own units activate the THEN part of a card".
3. Tablets: my spells and units make other spells -> lesser spells put greater spells into my hand or deck (Lesser
   Tablet of Fire -> Greater Tablet of Fire), scribes copy my last spell. Library: generate_cards (Runeterra, Monster
   Train Umbra, Slay the Spire), Kaptain's idea "spells that create more powerful spells in the deck".
4. The night of signs: I cast many spells in one round -> cards that count the spells cast this round grow. Library:
   spells_played_condition (Runeterra Ionia, Slay the Spire).
5. The ziggurat: my back-row priests give my spells power -> the keyword Augur N: your spells deal N more damage (in any
   lane, for each Augur unit). This is the back rows' job. Library: spell_power (Slay the Spire Watcher/Defect,
   Monster Train Stygian Guard).

HOW THE MECHANICS FEED EACH OTHER
- Tablets make spells, which set off Incantation and the night of signs, and target allies for Omens.
- Omen units are fulfilled by any ally-targeting spell; many such spells are cheap tablets.
- The ziggurat makes damage spells kill, which keeps the weak guards alive and the base held.
MECHANIC Incantation | makes: spell-reaction | needs: spell-cast
MECHANIC Omens | makes: omen-fulfilled, base-held | needs: ally-targeted, enemy-holder-dies
MECHANIC Tablets | makes: spell-cast, ally-targeted, tablet-made | needs: spell-reaction, omen-fulfilled
MECHANIC The night of signs | makes: big-spell, enemy-holder-dies, spell-reaction | needs: spell-cast, tablet-made
MECHANIC The ziggurat | makes: big-spell, enemy-holder-dies | needs: spell-cast

KEYWORDS OF ITS OWN
- Omen (value: its effect): "Omen: X" means X happens whenever one of your spells targets me.
- Augur N (value 2 per point): your spells deal N more damage.
- Tablet: a card name word only (no rule). Lesser tablets put their Greater tablet into the deck (Kaptain's idea).
Shared keywords used: Ranged (two star-gazers), Armoured (temple guards), Fading (conjured lions).

GROUPS
1. Burn from the ziggurat: Augur priests in rows 2-3, damage spells, Greater tablets. Champion: High Astrologer (Augur 1;
   round end: if you cast 2 or more spells this round, deal 2 damage to the enemy facing it; links to the night of signs).
2. The omen guards: front units with Omens, fulfilled by cheap protective charms. Champion: Bull-Man of the Gate (Omen:
   gains +1|+1; links to tablets, since the charms are tablets).
3. The library: scribes, copies of the last spell, lesser tablets that grow into greater ones. Champion: Chief Scribe
   (whenever you cast a spell, the first time each round, a copy of it into your hand; links to burn).
Two more champions: King's Seer (Place: draw 2; links the library and the omen guards) and The Star of the Seven
(Incantation: each time you cast a spell, deals 1 damage to the enemy facing it; Omen: draw a card, once per round;
links incantation and omens).

STRONG / ONLY A LITTLE / WEAK
- Strong: damage spells, card making, the enemy's back rows (spells reach every row), round-by-round card advantage.
- Only a little: holding the dots (omen guards, temple guards), supply (Temple Granary).
- Weak: unit strength and melee (its units are weak), big defence without spells, between lanes, mass strength buffs.
- No card gives it strong melee units without a spell behind them.

INTERACTION WITH THE ENEMY: medium. Damage spells, a few spells that make an enemy unable to act this round (exorcism).

SELF-SUFFICIENCY
- Holds dots: omen guards, Bull-Man, Armoured temple guards, conjured Lions (Fading).
- Removes: damage spells grown by Augur and greater tablets.
- Supply: Temple Granary (Supply +1), Tithe of the Temple (spell: +1 carry-over and +1 supply in this lane this round).
- Draw: King's Seer (Place: draw 2), Reading the Liver (spell: draw 2), Night Watch (round end: draw 1 if you cast 3 or
  more spells this round; once per round).
- Lane control: Temple Hill (round end: if you hold this lane's base, a Lesser Tablet into your hand, once per round),
  Omen of Ruin (spell: deals 2 damage; 4 if you don't hold this lane's base).
- Between lanes: Message by the Stars (spell: deals 2 damage to an enemy in ANY lane; paid in one lane, cast in another:
  extremely rare), Augur works in every lane.
- Hooks for two-faction decks: Omen is fulfilled by any spell of yours, so any faction's ally spells work; Augur boosts
  any damage spell.

ROWS 2-3: the ziggurat priests (Augur, spell reactions) and the scribes.
FREQUENCY: spell reactions that deal damage or make cards: once per round per unit; stat gains unbounded; draws on spells,
Place, once per round.

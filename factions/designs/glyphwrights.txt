THEME: WHAT THEY ARE LIKE
- Talismans: magic written in ink on paper strips and stuck on people, doors, corpses; it works as long as it stays.
- Burning: a talisman is burned to release its power all at once.
- Yin and yang: every force has its opposite; balance between them is strength; too much of one is weakness.
- Feng shui: where a thing stands and which way it faces decides its luck.
- Qi flows: energy runs along lines and through connected things, like water in channels.
- Paper effigies: paper soldiers, servants and horses brought to life (and burned as offerings to the dead).
- Calligraphy: the brush and the written word; a scholar copies and improves the old texts.
- Alchemy: elixirs that lengthen life; slow, patient growth.
- Exorcism: driving out spirits, sealing them with a talisman.
- The I Ching: everything changes into its opposite in turn.
- Masters and disciples: a master teaches, the disciple carries the lesson on.
- Mountains and hermitages: they keep to the back, far from the fighting.

MECHANICS
1. YIN AND YANG. Every one of their cards is Yin (protects, restores, binds: works on your units) or Yang (strikes,
   burns, drives out: works on the enemy). Give: you can't just play your best card; the order matters. Take: a card
   played after one of the other kind gets its "balanced" bonus; two of the same kind in a row are weaker (or cost 1
   more). The decision every turn: which kind comes next. Answer: kill the unit a Yin card was meant for, so the chain
   breaks. NEW RULE (a card's kind, and "your last card"). [library: Slay the Spire Watcher stances (in and out of
   Calm/Wrath); Runeterra Ionia "if you've played 2+ spells"; Claude's: alternation as the condition]
2. TALISMANS. Give: a spell's effect now. Take: a talisman spell is stuck on a unit (yours or the enemy's) and stays on
   it: it works while it stays (a seal on an enemy, a ward on an ally), and other cards BURN talismans for a big effect
   at once. Kaptain's attachment idea ("items"). Answer: kill the unit that carries it (the talisman is lost, or goes
   back to its owner's hand, item rule). NEW RULE (attached spells). [library: Runeterra equipment (Bandle City, Noxus:
   The Papertree, Furious Wielder); Duelyst Vetruvian attachments]
3. PAPER AND INK. Give: their units are paper (low defence). Take: units and spells turn into each other: some spells
   make paper soldiers; a paper unit that dies puts a spell into your hand (Kaptain's babushka idea); burning a talisman
   can raise a paper soldier in its cell. Answer: kill them with something that doesn't let them die "properly" (no
   such answer for most factions: they are weak to plain numbers instead). [library: Faeria and Duelyst "summon on
   spell", generate_cards in Songhai; Runeterra spells that create units]
4. QI FLOWS. Give: their units must touch edge to edge (packed, easy to hit together). Take: what you cast on one unit
   flows along edge-connected units: a Yin ward spreads to every touching ally, reduced by 1 each step; a Yang strike on
   an enemy flows through touching enemies (Kaptain's chain lightning, damage that travels to edge-connected units).
   Answer: break the line (kill the middle unit), keep their own units apart. [library: Monster Train/Runeterra chain
   damage (Prism Bolt-like ricochet), Duelyst Magmar adjacency; Claude's: reduced flow on allies]
5. THE MASTER'S LESSON. Give: a weak first copy of a spell. Take: cast, it puts a better version into the deck
   (the disciple improves the text) — Kaptain's "spells that create more powerful spells in the deck". Their draw card
   sits here ("study": draw one of your improved spells). Answer: win before the improved spells come. [library:
   Runeterra champion spells that shuffle stronger versions; Faeria Hammer of Destruction, back into the deck]

How they feed: talismans and lesson spells are the Yin/Yang cards (1); the balanced bonus makes the flow reach further
(4); paper soldiers carry talismans (2) and connect the flow (4); paper dies and returns as spells (3) that are cast
(5) and improve. A card in two: a Yin talisman that wards a paper soldier and flows to its neighbours (2 + 4); a Yang
spell that, cast balanced, also raises a paper soldier (1 + 3).

WAYS TO PLAY: Balance (perfect alternation, small cards) · Seals (talismans on enemy units that stop them; burn them
later) · Paper army (cheap paper soldiers, spells from their deaths) · Flow (a packed line where one cast hits all).

STRONG AT: spells (40-50% of the cards), card creation (signature), reaching the enemy back rows with spells,
interaction (seals). ONLY A LITTLE: holding dots with bodies (paper soldiers do it badly), supply (Supply +1 disciples).
WEAK AT: big units, defence, front-row melee, lane changes (one or two spells that cast in another lane, very rare).
LANE CONTROL: "as long as you hold this lane's base, my talismans can't be removed"; "if you don't, burn me for +2".
FEEL: a calligrapher's game: the right stroke after the right stroke.

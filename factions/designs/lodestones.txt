THEME: WHAT THEY ARE LIKE
- Stones that never move once raised; they will stand for thousands of years.
- Raised by many hands: whole villages hauling one stone on ropes and rollers.
- Hauling: they know how to drag great weights where they want them.
- Alignments: rows of standing stones in long lines (Carnac); a stone in line with another means something.
- Sun and stars: built to the solstice; the stones are a calendar; the longer the year turns, the more they mean.
- Ancestors: the dead are laid in stone tombs; the stones hold their spirits.
- Circles: henges enclose sacred ground; what is inside belongs to them.
- Patience: nothing hurried; they win by outlasting.
- Gatherings: at the solstice the tribes meet at the stones; feasts.
- Markers: a menhir claims the land around it.
- Weight: a fallen stone crushes what is under it.
- Few and big: a few great works, not many small ones.

MECHANICS
1. THE WORLD COMES TO ME. Give: no unit of theirs ever moves (all Immobile). Take: they move the enemy: drag enemy
   units forward toward the base, pull them into the column of a stone, swap two enemies. The decision: where to put
   each enemy so the other mechanics hit it. Answer: units that can't be moved, empty rows behind. [library: forced
   movement chains: forced_movement -> removal -> on_kill (Duelyst, Into the Breach, Runeterra, Stormbound);
   forced_movement -> adjacency -> zone_control]
2. CRUSH. Give: the enemy keeps its units packed. Take: an enemy dragged into another unit, or into the base edge where
   it can't go further, takes damage, and so does what it hit (both enemies, the base side does not move). Packed enemy
   camps hurt themselves. Topple: a stone can be thrown down on purpose onto the enemies facing it, dealing damage equal
   to its defence (give the stone, take the blow; grown stones fall harder). Answer: spread out (which leaves dots open). [library: Into the Breach bump damage, Force Amp
   "+1 damage from Bumps"]
3. ALIGNMENT. Give: their stones must stand in set columns (the enemy knows where). Take: stones in the same column in
   two or three lanes share their gains and their round-end damage between lanes (their between-lanes job), and
   enemies standing in a stone's column are hit by it at round end. Joins (1): drag enemies into the aligned columns.
   Answer: keep out of those columns (dragging puts them back). [library: Duelyst Vetruvian rows/obelysks; Claude's:
   alignment across lanes]
4. THE TURNING YEAR. Give: weak first rounds (few, slow, big stones). Take: every stone gains a little at each round end
   it stands through (lasting +0|+1 or +1|+0; no counters), and some cards ask "from round 4 on". The game gets worse
   for the enemy each round. Answer: win early, kill stones before they grow. [library: Inscryption Giant, "+1 Power
   every turn"; Stormbound/Duelyst turn-start growth]
5. THE ANCESTORS' GROUND. Give: their dead (they have few units, each death hurts). Take: when a stone falls, its
   ground stays sacred: the cell keeps a mark where enemies placed or dragged into it take damage, and the next stone
   raised there is cheaper. Answer: kill them where it costs them least. NEW RULE (marked cells). [library: Duelyst
   Abyssian shadow creep (cells that damage); Claude's: the cell that makes the next stone cheaper]

How they feed: dragging (1) packs enemies (2) and puts them in a stone's column (3); crushed and aligned enemies die,
which keeps the stones standing longer (4); grown stones (4) topple harder (2); a stone that falls (5) leaves ground that makes the next one cheaper and punishes enemies dragged onto it (1). A card in two: a
stone that drags an enemy on Place (1) and hits its column at round end (3); a tomb that grows each round (4) and
leaves sacred ground (5).

WAYS TO PLAY: Hauling (drag and crush; most interaction) · The long line (aligned stones across all lanes) · Henge
(few huge stones that grow, outlast) · Barrow ground (stones that fall and come back cheaper on sacred cells).

STRONG AT: control of where enemies stand, round-end damage, the late game, acting between lanes (alignment).
ONLY A LITTLE: spells (~20%: hauling and the solstice), supply (plain Supply +1 workers). WEAK AT: own movement and
lane changes, card draw (one card: the oracle stone), a fast start, many small units, reach without a stone in line.
LANE CONTROL: "as long as you hold this lane's base, the enemies I drag take 1 damage"; "if you don't, I drag twice".
FEEL: the enemy army slowly pulled onto the stones.

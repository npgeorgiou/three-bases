NAME AND ICON
The Sunfeeders. Icon: a skull inside a sun's rays (new glyph "sunskull"). Colour: obsidian black with marigold orange.

THEME: the Mexica (Aztec) world of the 15th century: the flower wars, the temple pyramids, the Day of the Dead.
1. The sun needs blood to rise: every death, theirs or the enemy's, feeds it.
2. Flower wars were fought to wound and capture, not only to kill: wounded enemies were finished on the altar.
3. Obsidian blades cut deep but break: many small cuts rather than one great blow.
4. Priests on the temple steps offer one life to gain a greater thing (rain, victory, the sun's favour).
5. Jaguar and eagle warriors rose in rank by the captives and kills they took.
6. Skull racks displayed the dead: the more skulls, the greater the city's power.
7. Marigold paths and offerings guide the dead home; the dead are remembered, not feared.
8. The calendar counts the days of offering: a day of many deaths is a great day.
9. Tribute flowed from conquered towns: maize, cacao, cloth (supply).
10. Pyramids rise in steps: the priests stand high above the battle (back rows).
11. They fear the sun dying at the end of an age; they lack metal armour and horses.
12. Death is a passage, not an end: a warrior's death is honoured and makes the next one stronger.

IDENTITY (one line)
Every death on the board feeds the Sunfeeders: they wound enemies with small cuts and finish the wounded, offer their own
units for greater power, and grow with each life taken this round.
(Not the Exanimati: no returns, no Shades, no Plague, no discard-pile play. The Sunfeeders profit from deaths happening,
the Exanimati from coming back.)

MECHANICS (each "I do X -> I benefit from Y")
1. Obsidian cuts: I deal 1 damage to many units -> enemies everywhere are Bloodied (lower than their card's defence) and
   small units die. Spells and Place effects spread 1-damage cuts (Rain of Obsidian, Knife Dancer). Library: ping and
   aoe_damage (Noxus, Shadow Isles).
2. Finishing the wounded: enemies are Bloodied -> my cards kill them outright. The keyword Obsidian kills a Bloodied enemy
   my strike leaves alive; spells kill a Bloodied enemy (Heart Taken). Library: Noxus "kill a damaged unit" (Noxian
   Guillotine), execute effects.
3. Offering: I let my own unit die -> a bigger effect now. The keyword Offering on a unit means: Place, the ally directly
   behind me dies; if one did, the card's offering effect happens (damage, supply, cards, growth). The player chooses the
   victim by where he places the unit. Library: sacrifice (Shadow Isles 24% of its cards; Monster Train Umbra and Melting
   Remnant).
4. The toll: units died this round -> my cards grow with the count. The keyword Toll: I have +1 strength for each unit
   (any side) that died this round. Spells and round-end effects count the deaths too. Library: death_count (Shadow Isles
   20% of its cards).
5. Harvest: a unit dies in my lane -> my units there gain (strength, shields, supply, a card once per round). Library:
   other_death_trigger (Shadow Isles, Noxus).

HOW THE MECHANICS FEED EACH OTHER
- Obsidian cuts make Bloodied enemies for Finishing the wounded, and kill small units for the Toll and the Harvest.
- Finishing the wounded makes deaths for the Toll and the Harvest.
- Offering makes deaths of my own for the Toll and the Harvest; cheap victims (Captive, Marigold Child) are made by the
  Harvest and by tokens.
- The Toll and the Harvest make my units big enough to strike and finish the wounded.
MECHANIC Obsidian cuts | makes: enemy-bloodied, unit-dies | needs: strong-striker
MECHANIC Finishing the wounded | makes: enemy-dies, unit-dies | needs: enemy-bloodied, strong-striker
MECHANIC Offering | makes: ally-dies, unit-dies | needs: victim-behind
MECHANIC The toll | makes: strong-striker | needs: unit-dies
MECHANIC Harvest | makes: victim-behind, strong-striker | needs: ally-dies, enemy-dies

KEYWORDS OF ITS OWN
- Toll (value 1 per point): I have +1 strength for each unit that died this round, on either side.
- Obsidian (value 1): when my strike leaves an enemy Bloodied, it dies.
- Offering (value 0; the effect is costed per card): Place: the ally directly behind me dies. "If offered" effects need it.
Shared keywords used: Ranged (eagle archers), Sacrifice (none; Offering replaces it here), Charge (jaguar runners, few).

GROUPS
1. The cutting rain: cheap cuts and Obsidian strikers that finish the wounded. Champion: Jaguar Knight (Obsidian, Toll;
   links to the toll group).
2. The temple steps: Offering units placed in front of cheap victims; priests in rows 2-3 that gain from each offering.
   Champion: High Priestess of the Steps (Offering: deals 3 damage to the enemy facing her and draws a card; links to
   the harvest).
3. The great day: Toll units, spells that grow with deaths this round, a big round-end finisher. Champion: The Sun-Eater
   (Toll 2; round end: deals damage equal to the deaths this round to the enemy facing it; links to the cutting rain).
Two more champions: Keeper of the Skull Rack (Harvest: whenever a unit dies in its lane, gains +1|+1; holds a front cell;
links the temple steps and the great day) and Lady of the Marigold Path (round end: if 2 or more units died this round,
draw a card, once per round; links the toll and the offering group).

STRONG / ONLY A LITTLE / WEAK
- Strong: removing enemies (cuts and kills), growing during a round of many deaths, the turn when everything dies.
- Only a little: supply (Tribute Bearer, offerings that give supply), drawing (Codex of Days, Marigold Path, offerings).
- Weak: holding the dots over many rounds (its units are soft and it kills its own), defence gains, healing, moving.
- No card restores shields to many allies or gives Armoured.

INTERACTION WITH THE ENEMY: high. Cuts, kills and harvests of enemy deaths.

SELF-SUFFICIENCY
- Holds dots: Temple Guard and Keeper of the Skull Rack grow from deaths in their lane; victims stand behind, not in front.
- Removes: cuts, Obsidian strikes, Heart Taken.
- Supply: Tribute Bearer (Supply +1), Maize Offering (offering: +2 supply in this lane this round), Rain Priest.
- Draw: Codex of Days (spell: draw 1, draw 2 if 3 or more units died this round), High Priestess (offering), Lady of the
  Marigold Path (round end, once per round).
- Lane control: Sun Altar (round end: if you hold this lane's base, deals 1 damage to each Bloodied enemy here), Flower
  War Captain (has +2 strength while you don't hold this lane's base), Eagle Herald (one effect for each case).
- Between lanes: Messenger of the Dead (Death: deals 1 damage to each Bloodied enemy in every lane), Day of the Dead (spell, Everywhere, very rare kind: each unit of yours gains +1 strength for each death this round).
- Hooks for two-faction decks: Toll and Harvest count any unit's death, so the Horde's Reckless units and the Exanimati's
  deaths feed them; Bloodied enemies made by any faction's damage are finished by Obsidian.

ROWS 2-3: the temple priests (offering payoffs, round-end cuts) and eagle archers (Ranged, finish wounded back-row enemies).
FREQUENCY: draws on spells, Place (offering) or once per round; Harvest gains of stats are unbounded (only stats).

REMOVAL, DISRUPTION, PROTECTION (4 Nov 2026 rework)
- Removal: many (cuts, Obsidian, Heart Taken, Executioner, kills of Bloodied enemies). Disruption: very few.
  Protection: very few (Bodyguards of the altar only).
THE CURVE
- 1-4: cheap victims (Willing Victim, Marigold Child) placed behind Offering units (Bloodletter, Sun Dancer, Altar Priest);
  Knife Dancer and Jaguar Scout cut on Place, Obsidian units finish.
- 4-6: Executioner and Twin Jaguars kill the wounded on Place; Keeper of the Skull Rack and the Shieldbearer grow with the
  round's deaths; Altar Guardian and High Priestess offer for big effects.
- 6+: The Sun-Eater deals 1 to each enemy per death this round; Jaguar Lord kills every Bloodied enemy; High Sacrificer
  offers to kill the strongest enemy; Temple Pyramid turns the whole lane into damage; Obsidian Colossus grows with the
  discard pile; Lord of the Skull Rack grows the lane with the round's deaths.

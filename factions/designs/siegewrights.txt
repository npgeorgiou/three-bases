WHO THEY ARE
A guild of engineers and gunners. They never march to a fight: they bring the wall and the shot to it. Every keg, every
gun, every cart stands where the plan says. Nothing is wasted: a spent shell is scrap, scrap is a new shell. They are
slow to set up and terrible once set up. What the player should feel: building the battery, then the volley; what the
enemy should feel: a lit fuse sitting in his own camp.
Traits: patient, engineered, explosive, thrifty, rooted.
How they win: kegs hold the front row; cannons in rows 2-3 fire shot into the enemy camp. Shot that hits a unit damages
it; shot that lands in a FREE cell stays there as the Siegewrights' unit and takes that dot (summary/08: units you
control in the enemy camp count for your dots; the enemy cannot place in a cell it fills, so only killing it frees it).

THE FIVE MECHANICS (from the traits)
1. AMMUNITION (engineered, explosive). Projectile: a keyword. A Projectile is a 1-cell unit, Immobile, strength 0,
   defence only, with an Impact text (what it does when it lands). Kaptain's rule: it is cheap, because the player pays
   twice (place it, then fire it). Placed in your front row it is also a wall that holds a dot until fired or killed.
   The decision: keep it as a wall or fire it, and where to place it (beside which cannon, in the front row or not).
   Answer: kill it (it is weak) — which sets off Powder (4).
2. BATTERIES (patient, rooted). Cannons stand in rows 2-3 (melee cannot reach them). A cannon fires an adjacent
   Projectile instead of attacking, paying what its attack costs (the Ogre Thrower's throw, built): it lands on any cell
   of the enemy camp in its lane. Kinds of cannon change the shot (a mortar that lands only in the enemy rows 2-3, a
   ballista whose shot passes the first unit and lands behind it). Timing: firing is an action during the round, so the
   enemy answers with his next action. Answer: Ranged units and spells on the back rows; or keep the front row full so
   there is no free cell to land in.
3. BREACH (explosive; the reverse of rooted). Your units in the enemy camp: lodged shot. A lodged unit in the enemy
   front row holds that dot for you until it is killed. Lodged shot has Fuse texts: "Round end: if I'm in the enemy camp,
   I deal 2 damage to the enemies beside me, then I die" — round end is when the enemy can no longer answer, so the
   enemy must spend an action and supply to kill it before passing (and killing it may set off Powder). Cards at home
   gain while you have units in the enemy camp of their lane.
4. POWDER (explosive). Projectiles have Death texts: when one dies, it damages the units beside it, both sides. So a
   keg in your front row that the enemy strikes hurts the striker's neighbours and the striker (the two front rows touch);
   kegs side by side set each other off (Kaptain's "damage that travels to edge-connected units"). The decision for the
   enemy: attack the kegs and lose units, or leave them and lose the dots. Answer: Ranged and spells from afar, or
   Incorporeal/Armoured units against the blast.
5. SALVAGE (thrifty). When Projectiles die, their scrap comes back: cards that return Projectile cards from the discard
   pile to the hand, a foundry that makes a Shot token beside a cannon at round start, and the faction's draw ("Round
   end: if 2 or more of your Projectiles died this round, draw a card", once per round per card).

WEB (the lines the network check reads; tags are things in play)
MECHANIC Ammunition | makes: projectile-ready, front-wall | needs: cannon, projectile-back
MECHANIC Batteries | makes: cannon, impact, lodged, projectile-dies | needs: projectile-ready, enemy-cell-freed
MECHANIC Breach | makes: enemy-dot-taken, enemy-action-spent, projectile-dies, enemy-damaged | needs: lodged
MECHANIC Powder | makes: enemy-damaged, enemy-killed, enemy-cell-freed, projectile-dies | needs: projectile-dies, front-wall, impact
MECHANIC Salvage | makes: projectile-back, card-drawn | needs: projectile-dies
Loops: fire -> lands on a unit (Impact, dies: Powder, Salvage) or in a free cell (Breach: the enemy pays to kill it:
Powder, Salvage) -> Salvage brings the shot back -> fire again. The wall side: a keg in the front row -> the enemy
strikes it -> Powder -> Salvage. Every Projectile card belongs to two mechanics at least (Ammunition + Powder or Breach).

WAYS TO PLAY (one faction, several decks)
- Bombardment: many cannons, the Salvage loop keeps them fed; kill the enemy's front holders, which also cuts his supply.
- Breach: Powder clears enemy front cells, then shot lands in them and takes the dots; win with the enemy's own cells.
- Minefield: front-row kegs and few cannons; the enemy loses units attacking the walls; win by holding at round end.
- Chain: kegs packed side by side (at home, or a cluster lodged in the enemy camp) going off one after another.

STRONG AT / ONLY A LITTLE / WEAK AT
- Strong: back-row damage into the enemy camp, cheap front walls, taking dots from inside the enemy camp.
- Only a little: moving its own units (a cart or two that move shot beside a cannon on Place), restoring shields of walls,
  supply (plain Supply +1 crews), spells (powder spells, rapid fire, salvage; ~25% of the cards).
- Weak at: movement and lane changes, healing big units, big strong melee bodies, a fast first round (two actions, a
  cannon and a shot, before the first volley, and the enemy sees them coming), protecting what it lodged.

ROWS, LANES, CONTROL, SELF-SUFFICIENCY, INTERACTION
- Rows: front row kegs and crews; rows 2-3 cannons and foundries (a cannon in the middle row centre has 8 cells beside it,
  in the back row only 5: where to stand is the puzzle).
- Between lanes: the Trebuchet (fires into ANY lane; one or two cards only), a powder cart that brings shot to another
  lane's cannon (Outrider on one card only).
- Lane control: "as long as you hold this lane's base, my Projectiles cost 1 less" (the garrison); "if you don't hold
  this lane's base, my Impact deals +1" (the desperate bombardment); some cards with both.
- Self-sufficiency: holds dots (kegs, lodged shot), removes (Impact, Powder), supply (Supply +1 crews, the foundry's free
  Shot tokens), draw (Salvage, once per round).
- Interaction: high. Lodged shot forces the enemy to act; kegs punish his attacks; he chooses which loss to take.

LIBRARY (commands run: factions/compare self_destruct, both death_trigger aoe_damage, compare recycle, both summon
zone_control, text search for throws and bombs)
- Into the Breach: the Blobber throws a blob that becomes a unit on the enemy side and explodes later; the Bombling

- Duelyst: Luminous Charge, joined 0/1 walls that deal damage to nearby enemies when destroyed (-> Ammunition + Powder:

- Faeria: Hammer of Destruction shuffles itself back into the deck after use; Rain of Fish lands units on random spaces

- No library source: the Fuse at round end against an enemy who must answer first (Claude's, from this game's round end).

NAME AND ICON
The Rootkin. Icon: an oak tree with a round crown and roots (new glyph "oak"). Colour: moss green and bark brown.

THEME: an ancient living forest of walking-tree legend (the sacred groves of the Celts and Germans; the oaks of Dodona).
1. Trees are rooted: once a tree stands, it never moves again.
2. Trees grow slowly but never stop: every season adds a ring.
3. Seeds fall and drift: saplings spring up wherever there is room.
4. Fallen leaves and dead wood feed the soil: when a tree falls, the trees beside it grow.
5. The roots run far under the ground and strike up beneath the enemy.
6. Great trees make a canopy: under it, smaller plants are sheltered.
7. Thorns and brambles hurt whoever pushes into them.
8. Sap closes wounds; bark grows back.
9. Druids tend the grove and speak for it; sacred groves are holy ground.
10. Vines and creepers climb and drag things toward them.
11. They fear fire and the axe; they lack speed and any way to change place.
12. Old groves hold their ground for centuries: time is on the forest's side.

IDENTITY (one line)
A rooted forest where nothing moves: every tree grows at each round end, seeds saplings into free cells, feeds its
neighbours when it falls and strikes up through its roots; it wins late, when its trees are bigger than anything the enemy has.

MECHANICS (each "I do X -> I benefit from Y")
1. Growth: my trees stand through a round end -> they grow (the keyword Perennial N: round end, I gain +N|+N). Cards grow
   faster while you hold the lane's base, or grow the trees around them. Library: permanent_growth (Faeria Blue and Green,
   Monster Train Wurmkin and Awoken).
2. Seeding: my trees drop seeds -> Saplings (1|1 tokens, Immobile, Perennial 1) appear in free cells, front row first, to
   hold dots and to be the soil for other cards (the keyword Seeds N: Death, N Saplings in my lane). Library: summon on
   death (Shadow Isles, Monster Train Umbra).
3. Leaf-fall: my trees and Saplings die -> the trees touching them grow. Library: other_death_trigger (Shadow Isles,
   Monster Train Melting Remnant).
4. Roots: my trees in any row hit the enemy facing them -> the keyword Roots N: round end, deals N damage to the enemy
   nearest the base in my column. This is the back rows' job: an oak in row 3 hits the enemy front row. Library: turn_end
   damage (Duelyst Vetruvian obelysks, Faeria).
5. The canopy: my big trees shelter the small ones -> allies in their row or column have more defence, and Saplings
   under the canopy grow faster. Library: aura (Demacia, Faeria Green).
Vines (a few cards, Kaptain's "drag the opponent to the first cell" idea): Pull the enemy into the front row, into the
roots and thorns. Not a mechanic of its own.

HOW THE MECHANICS FEED EACH OTHER
- Seeding makes small units; Growth makes them big over rounds; Leaf-fall turns their deaths into Growth for the others.
- Roots and the canopy need trees in place; Seeding fills cells; Growth makes Roots hit harder (cards with "deals damage
  equal to my strength" use the growth).
- The canopy keeps Saplings alive until they have grown.
MECHANIC Growth | makes: grown-tree | needs: tree-stands, base-held
MECHANIC Seeding | makes: sapling, tree-stands | needs: tree-dies
MECHANIC Leaf-fall | makes: grown-tree | needs: tree-dies, sapling
MECHANIC Roots | makes: enemy-damaged-roundend, base-held | needs: grown-tree, tree-stands
MECHANIC The canopy | makes: tree-stands, tree-dies | needs: sapling, grown-tree

KEYWORDS OF ITS OWN
- Perennial N (value 3 per point; growth that keeps coming): round end, I gain +N|+N.
- Seeds N (value N + 1): Death: places N Saplings in my lane (front row first).
- Roots N (value N + 1; it hits from any row): round end, deals N damage to the enemy nearest the base in my column.
Every Rootkin unit is Immobile (value -2, as for every Immobile unit; the Rootkin's Immobile bonus is part of each budget).
Shared keywords used: Bodyguard (old oaks), Ranged (one seed-spitter), Pull (vines, two cards).

GROUPS
1. The old grove: few big Perennial trees, the canopy over them, slow but huge. Champion: The Eldest Oak (2x2, Perennial 1,
   Roots 2; allies in its rows have +1 defence; links to roots).
2. The thicket: Seeds, Saplings and Leaf-fall: many small things that die and feed each other. Champion: Mother of
   Saplings (round end: a Sapling in a free cell of my lane; whenever a Sapling of yours here dies, I gain +1|+1; links
   to the old grove).
3. The root web: Roots trees in rows 2-3, thorns in the front, vines that pull enemies in. Champion: Deep Root (back
   row, Roots 3; links to the thicket because Saplings in its column hold the front).
Two more champions: Grove Druid (Place: draw 2 if you have 3 or more trees in this lane; links the thicket and the old
grove) and Wandering Seed-Wind (between lanes: round end, a Sapling in each other lane; links seeding and growth).

STRONG / ONLY A LITTLE / WEAK
- Strong: the late game, holding the dots with growing defence, round-end damage from any row.
- Only a little: removing enemies on its own turn (thorn spells, Pull into roots), drawing, supply (Mossbed: Supply +1).
- Weak: early rounds (small trees), moving (none can), answering Ranged and spell damage to its back rows, burst.
- No card lets a Rootkin unit move, or gives it Charge or Outrider.

INTERACTION WITH THE ENEMY: low. It grows and hits facing enemies; vines and thorns are its few touches.

SELF-SUFFICIENCY
- Holds dots: Saplings in the front row, grown trees, the canopy's defence.
- Removes: Roots, Brambles (round end: deals 1 damage to each enemy in my column(s)), thorn spells (Thornburst).
- Supply: Mossbed (Supply +1), Rich Loam (Supply +1; Death: Seeds 1).
- Draw: Grove Druid (Place), Whispering Leaves (spell: draw 1; draw 2 if you hold this lane's base), Acorn Cache (Death:
  draw 1).
- Lane control: Sacred Grove (as long as you hold this lane's base, other allies here have +1 strength), Old Growth
  (round end: gains +1|+1; +2|+2 instead if you don't hold this lane's base).
- Between lanes: Seed-Wind (round end: a Sapling in each other lane), Spreading Roots (spell, Everywhere: your units
  in every lane gain +1|+1; a very rare kind of card).
- Hooks for two-faction decks: Saplings and Perennial make any deck's front row grow; Leaf-fall gains from any ally's death.

ROWS 2-3: Roots trees hit from there; the canopy's big trees; seed-trees that drop Saplings into the front.
FREQUENCY: growth and damage once per round end (each unit); Saplings on death are bounded by deaths; draws once per card.

BENCHMARK REPORT OF THE SEVEN FACTIONS (4 Nov 2026, after the roster rework; node tools/cardstats.js <letter> 5)
Each faction played about 55 Benchmark games (depth 1): its two premade decks plus decks of 12 different cards x3 that
cover every card, against the premade decks of the other six (the old factions are gone). The cover decks are weak mixes,
so every faction stays under 50%; compare the factions with each other. 55 games is a small sample (about 10% either way).
"Card effect never happened" counts only a unit's own card effects (not keywords or position bonuses).

Changes made from the runs: Harden value 1 and sorties (Place: strike) on ten Hospice Knights; +1 defence on every unit of
the Horologers, Impi, Long Guns, Sunfeeders and Star Priests (their units died too easily to hold dots); the Rootkin lost
1 defence on every tree with 3 or more (they won 71%, then 33%); an Offering now pays the victim's supply into carry-over
(offerings cut the lane's income; the Sunfeeders went from 20-25% to 34-40%); victims' Death effects and the offering
effects are stronger; four Star Priest champions and top cards cost 1 less.

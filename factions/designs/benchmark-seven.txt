BENCHMARK REPORT OF THE SEVEN FACTIONS (4 Nov 2026; node tools/cardstats.js <letter> 4)
Each faction played 44 Benchmark games (depth 1): its two premade decks plus decks of 12 different cards x3 that together
cover every card, against the other premade decks of the game (the old five, the seven, both sides). The Benchmark plays
combos only by accident and 44 games is a small sample, so a card never played is a lead to look at, not a verdict.
"Card effect never happened" counts only a unit's own card effects (not keywords or position bonuses).
Change made from it: Harden's value went from 2 to 1 and every Harden unit got +1 defence (the Hospice Knights won 11%
of their games before, 30% after). The Rootkin win the most (68%); a test without the Saplings' growth did not change that.

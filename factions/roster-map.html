The roster: map

How to read it

Each dot is a concept. Two dots sit close when real decks hold both more often than chance. A dot takes the colour of the faction whose kernel holds it; grey dots are in no kernel. Tick factions to light their concepts: big dots are the kernel, middle dots the other concepts, small dots the extras; lines, in the faction's colour, are strong card links between its kernel and other concepts. Drag to move, wheel to zoom, hover a dot for its name.

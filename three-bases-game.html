How to play
  • Each base has 3 dots, one facing each column. A dot is yours if you have a unit in that column of your front row and the enemy doesn't. 2 dots take the base. Win by holding 2 or more bases at the end of a round that you also held when that round started. A base's colour shows who holds it now (green: you, red: the opponent); a frame around it, in the same colours, shows who held it when the round started. If a round ends with you having no carry-over and no units that generate supply, you lose (if both sides do, both lose). If three rounds in a row pass without either side taking any action, for whatever reason, both sides lose.
  • Players alternate single actions: deploy a unit, move one, attack, or play a tactic. Pass when you're done (Space). When both sides pass in a row, the round ends. Who acts first in round 1 is random; then the starting player alternates each round.
  • At round start every lane gives supply equal to the bread of your units there (plus bonuses). Spend it in that lane; your carry-over works anywhere. A unit's supply is also its cost, and the price of each move, attack or triggered ability.
  • Each unit can move once and attack once per round. Moves go to any free spot in your camp, in any orientation. Melee units strike the cells around them; from your front row that reaches across the base to the three enemy front-row cells facing them (a Pike also from your second row). Ranged units hit any enemy in the lane. Each point of strength (red) breaks one shield (blue); lost shields stay lost.
  • At round end, round-end abilities fire in reading order, then leftover supply fills your carry-over from your left lane to your right, up to 5. You start with 3. Each player draws 2 cards at round start; a hand holds at most 10 cards, and with 10 you draw nothing.
  • Drag or click to place or move. R or mouse wheel rotates. Right-click cancels.

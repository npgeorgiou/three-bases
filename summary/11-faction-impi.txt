11. IMPI (faction letter a; 107 cards: 78 units, 29 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: the Zulu regiments of the early 19th century.
Identity: Fast, cheap warriors that attack from the first round in waves: every attack and every kill pushes the next warrior on, the horns strike from the side columns, and the reserves run in to take each fallen warrior's cell; they win before the enemy is settled.
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Onrush (value 1): whenever another ally in my lane attacks, I gain +1 strength this round.
- Flanker (value 1): has +2 strength while in a side column of my front row.
- Reserve (value 1): when an ally in my lane dies while I am outside the front row, I move straight forward as far as I can (free; it is not a move action).
Champions: Induna of the Wave, Horn Captain, Old Bull of the Loins, Praise Singer, Spear of the King.
Design entry: factions/designs/impi.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-impi.js.

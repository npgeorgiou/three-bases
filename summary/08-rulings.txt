==================================================================
8. RULINGS
==================================================================
- Strike and damage (AGREED, October 2026): card texts write "strike" only for hits with the unit's strength (section 6) and "deals N damage" for fixed amounts; "damage" alone covers both (e.g. "survives damage").
- Kinds of stat change and their wording (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): a lasting change uses a plain verb with no time word ("gains +1 strength", "loses 2 strength"); it stays with the unit until it leaves the board. A change for this round only ends with "this round" ("gains +1|+1 this round"). A position bonus says "have" ("Other allies in my row have +1 strength"): it holds only while its condition holds. Lost shields are separate: they stay lost until restored. The word "permanently" is not used.
- Place (AGREED): a unit is placed when it enters the grid from the hand; its Place effects fire then.
- An effect with no free target cell does nothing.
- The discard pile is the graveyard: a unit that dies goes there.
- Losing a defence bonus (a position bonus ends, or a "this round" bonus expires): the unit keeps its current defence if that is at or below its defence without the bonus; otherwise its defence drops to that value. So a unit that lost shields while boosted loses nothing more when the boost ends (as in Legends of Runeterra).

==================================================================
8. RULINGS
==================================================================
- Strike and damage (AGREED, October 2026; replaces the earlier ruling): card texts write "strike" only for hits with the unit's strength (section 6) and "deals N damage" for fixed amounts from abilities and spells; "damage" alone covers both (e.g. "survives damage").
- Kinds of stat change and their wording (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): a lasting change uses a plain verb with no time word ("gains +1 strength", "loses 2 strength"); it stays with the unit until it leaves the board (and through Revenant returns and returns to the hand, below). A change for this round only ends with "this round" ("gains +1|+1 this round"; replaces "until round end"). A position bonus says "have" ("Other allies in my row have +1 strength"): it holds only while its condition holds (auras, Shield wall, Pack, Skirmisher). Lost shields are separate: they stay lost until restored. The word "permanently" is not used.
- Place (keyword and event) (AGREED): a unit is placed every time it enters the grid — when played from the hand, when it defects, when it is resurrected, when a Revenant returns, and when a token appears. Place effects (Charge, Mandator, Monomachos, Komes, Presbeutes, Crowd Pleaser, Cinder Imp, and the Voukinator/Warhorn bonuses for units placed later) fire on every placement, so a defected unit fires its Place effects for its new side. A Place choice can only be made by its owner on their own turn; a unit placed at any other time (during the opponent's turn or the round end) skips the choice (Cinder Imp then makes only its enemy strike).
- Follow-up choices: when a card needs a choice after it is played (a Place pick, Reform Ranks' moves, a defector's new spot, Monomachia's enemy, Synaspismos' partner), the same player keeps the turn until the choice is made; the turn then passes. Mandator and Reform Ranks may be finished early (Done); the other choices are required.
- An effect with no free target cell (push, pull, defection) does nothing.
- Armoured blocks the first damage each round from any source, spells and own strikes included. Damage blocked by armour still counts as surviving damage (Varangoi, Pain Eater).
- Charge (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): a unit placed in its own front row doesn't charge (it has no run-up), so a Warhorn unit placed there neither charges nor dies from Reckless. Charge strikes only if the unit reaches its front row, and then every enemy in the facing front-row cells of its columns (not the diagonal ones), each with its full strength (a 2-wide charger can strike two units); a charger stopped short strikes no one. A 0-strength charger that reaches enemies still counts as striking them for the charger only (Reckless fires, so e.g. a Warhorn-charged Powder Keg dies and explodes); the strike does nothing to the target, uses up no Armour and doesn't count as surviving damage. Logged, no animation.
- Ability damage (Akritai, Cinder Imp, Blood Drum, Blood Sorcerer, Powder Keg, Flesh Shield, Pyre Colossus) adds the unit's current strength bonuses, on every target (own units included). Every damage amount (abilities, charges, attacks) is fixed when the effect starts and stays the same for all its strikes, even if the unit gains or loses strength meanwhile (e.g. a Cinder Imp boosted to 3 that kills the Blood Drum still strikes the enemy with 3); for Death effects, the bonuses it had when it died. A unit's card (hover) shows these numbers with its bonus included, highlighted in a small dark chip; Blood Sorcerer's bonus reads "…1 for each ally struck, plus N".
- Losing a defence bonus (an aura such as Strategos or Shield wall ends, or a bonus "this round" such as Tourmarches' expires): the unit keeps its current defence if that is at or below its defence without the bonus; otherwise its defence drops to that value. So a unit that lost shields while boosted loses nothing more when the boost ends (as in Legends of Runeterra).
- Pull (Monomachos): the enemy moves straight forward in its column, toward its base, as far as the cells are free. Monomachos' Place pick (AGREED, October 2026) offers only enemies that can move at least one cell forward; with none, there is no pick.
- Defect: the unit is placed with the new side in a free spot of that side's camp in the same lane and can't act that round.
- Akritai react to any arrival into an enemy front-row cell facing them: placement, moves, charges, pulls, Onslaught, defection, returns. A placed unit's own effects fire first (its Place effects, after its Place choice once made, and a Revenant's return effect), then the Akritai react (a Plague Acolyte gives Plague before it is struck).
- Warhorn: units placed later gain Charge and Reckless; the charge strike counts as their attack, so they die after it; with nobody to strike, or placed in the front row, they survive.
- Cinder Imp: if it has no other unit in its lane, only the enemy strike happens.
- Rock Hurler's splash: the cell behind the target gets half its strength, the one after a quarter, rounded down.
- Thousand Cuts may also target your own units; Armoured blocks only the first of its damage.
- Riposte (AGREED) answers attacks and charge strikes, not strikes from spells such as Stampede.
- Monomachia offers only units with strength. Bodyguards also protect each other.
- Varangian Guard (AGREED, October 2026): it gains its +1 defence after taking the damage for the ally, if it survives.
- Resurrection (AGREED): the killed unit comes back on your side as the same card, with debuffs that halve its printed strength, defence and supply (rounded down, no minimum; a unit whose defence would halve to 0 can't come back and stays dead). A unit resurrected from the discard pile (Raise the Fallen, Grave Robber, Aurelius, Danse Macabre, Katabasis) comes back fresh: none of its earlier gains, losses or damage. It is placed (Place effects fire), then auras and other area effects apply to it again. With a Banner of the Returned in its lane, a returning Revenant keeps its printed strength; its defence and supply still halve (AGREED, October 2026).
- Revenants keep their lasting changes (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): a returning Revenant keeps its lasting stat gains and losses and its gained or lost keywords (e.g. Komes' +1 defence, Tomb Warden's +1|+1, Creeping Rot's -2 strength, a Revenant given by Embalmer); it doesn't keep lost shields or anything "this round". How many times it returns is still decided only by halving the printed stats. Example: a Knight of the Ninth Barrow (3|3) with Komes' +1 defence returns as 1|1 plus +0|+1, and Tomb Warden adds +1|+1: 2|3. On its next death its printed defence 1 would halve to 0, so it stays dead.
- Returning to the hand with changes (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): a unit that goes back to the hand (Undying Reaver) keeps its lasting stat changes and its gained or lost keywords; lost shields aren't kept. Its hand card shows the changed strength and defence in green (higher) or red (lower) and adds a line "Kept from the battlefield: …" listing them; played again, the unit comes back with them. Built as a general mechanism (any future card that returns a unit to the hand works the same way); in the discard pile the card is always the plain deck card.
- One return per death (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): a dead unit's card can go back only once per death, and the first effect that takes it from the discard pile wins. Revenant goes first, so an embalmed Undying Reaver returns to the board (halved) until its defence would halve to 0; then its card is still in the discard pile and its own Death effect takes it back to the hand (with Revenant kept). Tokens have no card in the discard pile, so an embalmed token still returns.
- The discard pile is the graveyard. Tokens vanish when they die (no card goes to the discard pile).
- Units you control in the enemy camp (AGREED): they count for your base dots, give you their supply, and you select and use them like any of your units. Positions are measured (AGREED) from the camp the unit stands in, so it moves within that camp, its "front row", "facing" and melee attacks point at the opposite camp, and it strikes or affects only enemies. Such a unit has a ring in its controller's colour (green you, red opponent). When it dies, its card goes to its controller's discard pile.
- Built choices (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): Shades have supply 1; Gorvan rises as soon as all four parts are done; Hungering Mass takes the eaten unit's cells; the Ogre Thrower's throw costs what an attack costs.

11. FACTIONS (continued)
------------------------------------------------------------------
EXANIMATI (death faction; formerly "The Grave Court")
------------------------------------------------------------------
Identity (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026): death and disease; own non-Greek theme. The faction profits from dying: its own deaths feed Shades, returns, card draw and growth. Its main weakening tool is Plague: diseased enemies lose a shield every round end and spread the disease when they die; a smaller set of cards also lowers enemy strength (another faction will mostly lower defence). Uses the discard pile as a graveyard (resurrection). It leaves Shades behind when it dies; remains (corpses) belong to the future Rotborn, not to the Exanimati. Baseline 1-cost unit: Bone Thrall 1 - 1|2.
Core archetypes: four, one per unit group below. Each unit sits in the group whose mechanic it mainly serves; spells are listed together.
Core keywords: Plague, Revenant, Afterlife, Fading, Sacrifice, Immobile, Drain. Spice: Incorporeal. A unit with two keywords is rare.
Name concepts: Children of the Plague (disease as divine revelation; plague masks, fever visions), Ossuary Kingdom (the dead as honoured workforce; bone architecture, soul contracts), the Returned (the dead return with memories; returned knights, ancestral spirits, dead kings), Pale Physicians (death as a disease to cure; plague doctors, surgeons, stitched flesh), the Mist (ghosts, Shades).
Card list status: built (October 2026 redesign; revised 10 Oct 2026). Stats follow section 9; the strength/defence split is Claude's unless marked AGREED. Names, keywords, costs and build choices that were proposed were approved on 10 Oct 2026.

- Requiem (3) — Return up to 2 units from your discard pile to your hand.
- Mass Grave (3) — Pick a lane: your Shades there fly through the enemy camp, in each column as many at the first enemy as it takes to kill it, then at the next one (AGREED, 10 Oct 2026; Shades only since 10 Oct). The strikes are planned per column in flying order, front flier first: each flier goes at the first enemy in its column not yet planned to die (by its defence and Armour left); each strikes with its strength, then dies. All the strikes count at once, when the spell ends, and the deaths then resolve as usual. One with no enemy left in its column flies off the board and leaves the game without dying.
- Ossuary Volley (3) — Pick a lane: deals 1 damage to each enemy in its front row for every 4 cards in your discard pile (up to 4).
- Contagion (4; AGREED, 10 Oct 2026, was 2 and hit a unit and its neighbours) — Pick a column of an enemy camp: every enemy in it catches Plague. Hovering an enemy camp cell highlights the column, as for Greek Fire.
- Raise the Fallen (4) — Resurrects the top unit of your discard pile (spells skipped) in a free spot of your choice in your camp; the card leaves the discard pile. (AGREED)
- Thinning of the Veil (4) — Everywhere. Until round end, each of your units on the whole board that dies (Shades included) returns as a Shade in its cell. Built: hovering a lane highlights your whole board.
- Danse Macabre (5; AGREED, 10 Oct 2026, replaces "Shades and Revenants strike") — Everywhere. Each of your units that died this round is resurrected in its own cells, if they are free (halved printed stats, placed, its card leaves the discard pile). They rise one by one in resolution order; tokens can't rise (they have no card), nor can units whose defence would halve to 0.
- Night of Shades (5) — Pick a lane: places one Shade in each free cell of your camp there, up to the number of units in your discard pile, and removes one unit card from the top of your discard pile per Shade placed. (AGREED)
- Katabasis (5; was "Soul Theft") — Kills any unit, yours or the enemy's, and resurrects it on your side in its own cells (even in the enemy camp) at full printed stats, without the resurrection debuffs. (AGREED)
- Execution (6) — Kill an enemy unit. (AGREED: moved from the Horde in October 2026; kills belong to the death faction.)
- The Long Night (6) — Pick a lane: every enemy there loses 2 strength.
- Debt of the Fallen (7; AGREED, 10 Oct 2026) — Costs 1 less for each of your units that died this round (never below 0). Kill an enemy unit. Its card in the hand shows the current cost ("now N"). The Mist deck's finisher.

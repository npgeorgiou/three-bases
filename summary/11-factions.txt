==================================================================
11. FACTIONS
==================================================================
The game has no factions. 20 new ones are designed by the wheel (factions/START_DESIGN-1-wheel.md); ideas for factions
are in factions/.

Notation: supply - strength|defence, then the size: no mark 1 cell, ▪▪ line of 2, ▪▪▪ line of 3, ▪▪▪▪ 2x2.

Leaders (5 per faction) have a golden border in the game, on the card and on the board; no other card has one.

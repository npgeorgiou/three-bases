==================================================================
11. FACTIONS
==================================================================
The game has seven factions (4 Nov 2026), one file each: identity, keywords with values, champions, units, spells, tokens.
Ideas for new factions and redesigns are in factions/; the removed factions (Tagma, Ashfang Horde, Exanimati) are in
factions/removed/.

Notation: supply - strength|defence, then the size: no mark 1 cell, ▪▪ line of 2, ▪▪▪ line of 3, ▪▪▪▪ 2x2.
Value = stat points the keyword takes from the supply budget (negative = a drawback that buys stats). AGREED.

General keywords (any faction may use them):
Keyword        | Value       | Description
---------------+-------------+------------
Ranged         | 3           | Can attack any enemy in its lane, from any row.
Supply N       | 3 per point | Adds N supply to its lane at each round start.
Armoured       | 2           | The first damage it takes each round is blocked.
Charge         | 2           | When placed outside the front row, moves straight forward; if it reaches the front row, strikes the enemies right ahead.
Riposte        | 2           | Strikes back an attacker when it survives.
Flurry         | 3           | Attacks twice per round, pays once.
Pike           | 1           | Can also attack the enemy front row from your second row.
Lightfooted    | 1           | Moves twice per round, pays for the first move only.
Shield wall    | 1           | Beside another Shield wall unit in its row: +1 defence.
Push           | 1           | A surviving front-row enemy it strikes moves one row back.
Outrider       | 1           | Can move to a free spot in another lane's camp.
Bodyguard      | 1           | Takes the damage aimed at adjacent allies.
Immobile       | -2          | Can't move.
Fading         | -2          | Dies at the end of the round in which it was placed.
Reckless       | -2          | Dies right after it attacks.

Factions (letter, file):
- Horologers (c): 11-faction-horologers.txt. Clockwork: machines act at round end, winding makes them act now, broken ones become Spare Parts.
- Sunfeeders (d): 11-faction-sunfeeders.txt. Death: every death of the round feeds them (Toll, Obsidian, Offering).
- Impi (a): 11-faction-impi.txt. Aggro: waves of cheap attackers, horns in the side columns, reserves fill fallen cells.
- Hospice Knights (b): 11-faction-hospice.txt. Defence: few tough knights that harden, strike back and are restored.
- Star Priests (s): 11-faction-starpriests.txt. Spells: units react to spells, Omens, back-row priests add spell damage.
- Rootkin (o): 11-faction-rootkin.txt. Every unit Immobile: trees grow each round end, seed Saplings, strike through roots.
- The Long Guns (l): 11-faction-longguns.txt. Artillery: guns in the back rows bombard at round end, observers mark targets.
Units whose text checks "you hold this lane's base" have a golden border in the game.

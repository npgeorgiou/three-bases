11. ROOTKIN (faction letter o; 102 cards: 71 units, 31 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: an ancient living forest of walking-tree legend (the sacred groves of the Celts and Germans; the oaks of Dodona).
Identity: A rooted forest where nothing moves: every tree grows at each round end, seeds saplings into free cells, feeds its neighbours when it falls and strikes up through its roots; it wins late, when its trees are bigger than anything the enemy has.
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Perennial N (value 3 per point; growth that keeps coming): round end, I gain +N|+N.
- Seeds N (value N + 1): Death: places N Saplings in my lane (front row first).
- Roots N (value N + 1; it hits from any row): round end, deals N damage to the enemy nearest the base in my column.
Champions: Grove Druid, Mother of Saplings, Deep Root, The Eldest Oak, Wandering Seed-Wind.
Design entry: factions/designs/rootkin.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-rootkin.js.

==================================================================
15. COMPUTER OPPONENTS
==================================================================
In the game (three-bases-game.html):
- The Benchmark (default): the AI lab's ifelse AI with the "ifelse AI gen 18" values (the earlier Benchmark is retired from the game). It sees the opponent's hand and searches the real position (alpha-beta, scored as the round would end). Follow-up choices are plies of the same player in its look-ahead (so after a Place pick it sees one ply less far). Thousand Cuts always spends all available supply, so it is one option per target. A unit's worth adds a rough, hand-set bonus for its ability (e.g. Ranged and unused Armoured 8, Pike 4, row auras 6 per point given; drawbacks such as Reckless or self-strikes subtract a few points); the Exanimati cards have no such values yet. Its threat estimate ignores a single strike on an unused Armoured unit and any strike on an Incorporeal unit. It considers Onslaught only in lanes where it would move at least one unit. Redraw: cards costing 4 or more, the most expensive first, at most 3 (Bribe and Thousand Cuts count as cost 0).
- Reworked (October 2026) on top of the gen 18 values: it never casts a spell that would change nothing (no board, hand, deck or supply change beyond its own card and cost; units are compared by card, not by id, so Last Rites on a Shade, which only swaps a Shade for a Shade, counts as nothing), never casts Reform Ranks or makes a free move (Reform Ranks, Mandator) unless one of the moves makes the round better than making none (October 2026; it used to move a unit there and back again), never places Blood-Letter when its Place pick would have no target, and doesn't consider Monomachia when neither unit has strength; at its root choice, an action that kills enemy units counts their worth extra (killPref) and passing right after the opponent passed counts 40 less (passPen 40); "two bases" counts only bases held at round start and now (the win rule; in its look-ahead the next round starts with the current bases). An evaluation rebuilt from scratch on the round-end forecast was tried and lost (3 of 12), so the tuned values stay.
- Kills over carry-over (October 2026): it valued each point of carry-over at about 61 (an enemy unit is worth about 20–40), so it passed to save supply instead of killing. Now carry 30 and killPref 1 (was 61 and 0.5): against the previous version it won 15 of 24 games (8 lost, 1 stopped) on mixed premade-deck pairings.
- Tournament check (10 Oct 2026): every premade deck against every premade deck, both seats (162 games per run), the reworked Benchmark against the version before the Reform Ranks and free-move fix. Played at search depth 1 for speed (2 cores; the real depth 3 takes about 1 s per move, a full run would take hours). Result over three seed sets: 250 wins to 228, i.e. even. An extra term valuing a unit's abilities by the design budget was tried and showed no gain, so it is not in the game; a weight-by-weight search (each value halved or doubled) found nothing that held up on fresh seeds. A real improvement needs tuning at the true depth over many hours, or new evaluation terms for the new rules (tombstones, deaths in chains, abilities).
- Known: it has no hand-set values for the new cards; retuning on the current cards belongs to the AI lab.
- The Random player: picks a kind of move at random (deploy, move, attack, spell or pass), then a move of that kind; redraws 0-3 random cards.
- Second player on the same screen.
- Not in the game yet: the Strategist, the tunable ifelse AI and the learning AIs (lab only).
- Speed: the look-ahead uses alpha-beta pruning (skips lines that cannot change the choice) and fast board-geometry helpers; moves are identical to the unpruned search (verified on seeded games).

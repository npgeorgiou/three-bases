==================================================================
12. DECKS AND START PAGE
==================================================================
- Decks hold 30–40 cards of one faction, at most 3 copies of a card. Unfinished decks can be saved but not picked for play. Decks are kept in this browser; cards no longer in the game are dropped from saved decks.
- Premade decks: none (14 Nov 2026, the factions were deleted; PREMADE_VERSION 8). The start page works with no decks.
- Start page: your picked deck (shown as a deck list in a grid of at most 2 columns; card names wrap onto two lines), Pick a deck, Edit deck (opens the picked deck in the deck builder; greyed out with no deck picked) and Create a deck under the deck box; opponent (Computer or second player on the same screen), computer player, the opponent's deck; Start battle. A battle can't start without a picked ready deck and an opponent deck. The opponent's deck list starts with "Random deck" (AGREED, October 2026: each battle, including Play again, picks a faction at random, then one of its ready decks), then offers every ready deck, the same faction included (a mirror battle). The computer-player choice is remembered. Test game option: any deck size and copies, unlimited supply; under it "In test games the opponent doesn't act" (on by default, greyed out unless test games are on): the computer opponent only passes.
- Decks page: faction buttons (the deck builder's) filter the tiles; every saved deck with its faction and card count (a deck that isn't ready shows just its card count in red; a ready one adds "Ready"); each tile has a thick faction-coloured ribbon on top; click a tile to pick it (ready decks, or any non-empty deck in test games); Edit, Delete, Create a deck.
- Deck builder: all cards on the right, grouped by faction, with keyword panels (and the cards they name) on hover; click a card to add a copy. Picking a card of one faction greys out the other factions' cards. Token cards (Shade, Abomination, Gorvan) exist as cards (shown beside a card that names them) but aren't listed in the deck builder, nor are hand cards carrying changes.
  Top bar: faction filter buttons, then Units / Spells filter buttons (none pressed = both), set apart from the search box; the search box takes comma-separated words or phrases and matches card names, card text, the card's keywords and their hover texts, and the name and text of any card it names (e.g. Husk matches "Abomination"); inside the box on the right, a large clear button (an X, shown only while the box has text) empties it. Right of the box, a toggle, green "Include" by default (shows the cards matching any of the words) or red "Exclude" (hides them). The "any of the words" reading is AGREED (10 Oct 2026).
  Left sidebar: deck name, status, and the picked cards as deck-list items (oblong: cost badge, name, symbol, the unit's shape, strength, defence; copies to the right), ordered by cost (Bribe and Thousand Cuts count as 0), then strength, defence, name. Clicking a deck-list item removes one copy; hovering it shows its card and keywords. Save (the saved deck becomes the picked deck; returns to the previous screen), Empty deck (removes every card; Back still drops the change unsaved), Back (asks before dropping unsaved changes), Delete.
- In battle, each side is named after its army, not the deck's name. In a mirror battle the names add who plays them: "(you)" / "(computer)", or "(player 1)" / "(player 2)".

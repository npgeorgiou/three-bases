==================================================================
11. STAR PRIESTS (faction letter s; 103 cards: 62 units, 41 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: the astrologer-priests of Babylon (the 7th-6th century BC temples and ziggurats).
Identity: Priests whose spells are their army: every spell cast makes their temple units act, spells laid on their own units fulfil those units' omens, and lesser tablets put greater ones into the deck; they win by burning the enemy's holders while their guards hold the dots.
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Omen (value: its effect): "Omen: X" means X happens whenever one of your spells targets me.
- Augur N (value 2 per point): your spells deal N more damage.
Champions: High Astrologer, Bull-Man of the Gate, Chief Scribe, King's Seer, The Star of the Seven.
Design entry: factions/designs/starpriests.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-starpriests.js.

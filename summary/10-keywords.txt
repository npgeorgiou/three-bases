==================================================================
10. KEYWORDS
==================================================================
Rule words: their texts are shown on hover in the game (src/game/js/cards/keywords.js). Ranged and melee reach are
described in section 6, supply and carry-over in section 5; their values in the supply budget are in section 9.

- Ranged: Can attack any enemy in its lane, from any row.
- Immobile: Can't move.
- Supply: What cards cost; each lane has its own. A unit with Supply N adds N to its lane at each round start.
- Carry-over: Supply you keep between rounds. At round end your unspent lane supply is added to it, up to 5 in total; you can spend it in any lane.
- Strike: A hit with a unit's strength: its attacks, and strikes with its strength from an ability or a spell. A melee unit strikes only what it could attack. Fixed amounts from abilities and spells are damage dealt, not strikes.

Keywords are shared by the factions, and every faction has at least 1 of its own (factions/roster/HANDOFF.md); they are made
new for the 20 factions.

ASHFANG HORDE (barbarians / orcs)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Identity: damage-heavy and self-destructive; units strike their own side, die to their own effects and grow from blood. Baseline 1-cost unit: 2|1 with no ability. Inspiration: Legends of Runeterra's Noxus and Faeria's red cards. Its spells deal damage; plain kill spells belong to the Exanimati (AGREED), though Cull and Decapitate stay with the Horde (AGREED). The Horde is not about fine-tuning (AGREED): its X effects spend everything available.
Main keywords: Reckless, Charge, Push, Splash, Overrun, Ranged, Pack, Frenzy, Sacrifice, Riposte, Flurry, Death, Round end self-strikes.

==================================================================
6. UNITS — GENERAL RULES
==================================================================
- Shapes: 1 cell, line of 2, line of 3, 2x2 square. Units can take any orientation.
- Stats: strength, defence, supply. Supply is both what the unit gives its lane each round and what it costs (to deploy and per action). It represents the resources the unit can muster.
- Supply ranges from 1 upward; costs above 6 are allowed (the supply-to-stats rule continues at 2 per supply). Size is independent of supply but eats into the stat budget (section 9).
- Minimum printed defence is 1 (0 defence means dead). Values never go negative.
- Units with 0 strength can't attack.
- Damage breaks shields; if the unit survives, it keeps fewer shields from then on.
- Reach (AGREED, October 2026): a melee unit reaches its adjacent area: every cell around its cells, diagonals included. For this, the base doesn't exist: the two front rows of a lane touch, so a unit in your front row also reaches the enemy front-row cells facing its cells and the ones diagonally next to them (a unit in your front-right cell reaches the enemy's middle and right front-row cells; a unit in the front-middle cell reaches all three). A Pike unit also reaches the enemy front-row cells two rows ahead of it (column ±1), so from your second row it reaches the enemy front row the same way. Ranged units reach every cell of their lane. Multi-cell units reach around every cell they cover. Attacks hit only enemies in reach.
- Strike and damage (AGREED, October 2026): a strike is a hit with the unit's strength (its attack). Strikes reach only what the unit could attack (reach above). A fixed amount of damage is damage dealt, not a strike.
- No active (triggered) abilities on units. No ranged units that attack only under conditions.
- Timing/build-up cards (e.g. "at 3 counters, do X") very sparingly (Kaptain, 16 Nov 2026). Round-end abilities are wanted, as are cards that play on positioning relative to other units (adjacency, rows, formations).

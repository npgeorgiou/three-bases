11. HOSPICE KNIGHTS (faction letter b; 104 cards: 75 units, 29 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: the military orders of hospital knights, at the great siege of an island fortress in the 16th century.
Identity: Few, tough knights who hold the dots by outlasting the enemy: every blow they survive hardens them or is struck back, the hospital restores them, and as long as they hold a base, relief keeps arriving.
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Harden (value 1; measured: at 2 the faction lost almost every Benchmark game): when I survive damage, I gain +1 defence.
- Retaliate N (value N): when I survive damage from an enemy unit, I deal N damage to it.
- Vigil N (value N): round end: restores N of my shields.
Champions: Castellan of the Bastion, Knight of the Sortie, Infirmarian Mother, The Grand Master, Relief Galley.
Design entry: factions/designs/hospice.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-hospice.js.

==================================================================
11. HOROLOGERS (faction letter c; 111 cards: 82 units, 29 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: the clockmakers' guilds of a 17th-18th century European clock city (the astronomical clocks of Prague and Strasbourg, the automata of the Jaquet-Droz workshop, the Nuremberg guilds).
Identity: Machines that act by themselves at round end; the Horologers wind them so they act again during the round, and turn every broken machine into spare parts that wind the next one.
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Geared (value 0): when I am wound, every Geared ally touching me is wound too (each machine once per wind).
- Wound (value 1): Place: my round-end effects happen now.
- Salvage (value 1): Death: a Spare Part into your hand.
Champions: Master Horologer, The Grand Orrery, Mistress of Salvage, The Great Clock Tower, Brass Colossus.
Design entry: factions/designs/horologers.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-horologers.js.

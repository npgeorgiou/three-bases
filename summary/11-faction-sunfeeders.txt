==================================================================
11. SUNFEEDERS (faction letter d; 106 cards: 76 units, 30 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: the Mexica (Aztec) world of the 15th century: the flower wars, the temple pyramids, the Day of the Dead.
Identity: Every death on the board feeds the Sunfeeders: they wound enemies with small cuts and finish the wounded, offer their own units for greater power, and grow with each life taken this round. (Not the Exanimati: no returns, no Shades, no Plague, no discard-pile play. The Sunfeeders profit from deaths happening, the Exanimati from coming back.)
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Toll (value 1 per point): I have +1 strength for each unit that died this round, on either side.
- Obsidian (value 1): when my strike leaves an enemy Bloodied, it dies.
- Offering (value 0; the effect is costed per card): Place: the ally directly behind me dies and its supply goes to your carry-over (Benchmark, 4 Nov 2026: offerings cut the lane's income, so the tribute pays it back). "If offered" effects need it.
Champions: Jaguar Knight, High Priestess of the Steps, Keeper of the Skull Rack, Lady of the Marigold Path, The Sun-Eater.
Design entry: factions/designs/sunfeeders.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-sunfeeders.js.

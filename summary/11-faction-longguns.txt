11. THE LONG GUNS (faction letter l; 103 cards: 72 units, 31 spells)
==================================================================
Theme: the artillery of the Western Front, 1914-1918.
Identity: Heavy guns in the back rows bombard the enemy's rear at every round end while dug-in infantry holds the front; observers mark targets so every strike on them hits twice as hard, and the infantry goes over the top once the barrage has broken the enemy.
Keywords (value = stat points from the supply budget):
- Barrage N (value N + 1; it reaches the back row from any row): round end, deals N damage to the enemy furthest from the base in my lane.
- Entrenched (value 1): while I am in my front row with an ally directly behind me, I am Armoured.
Champions: Chief Observer, Trench Major, Quartermaster General, The Great Howitzer, The Railway Gun.
Design entry: factions/designs/longguns.txt. Cards in the game: src/game/js/cards/new-longguns.js.

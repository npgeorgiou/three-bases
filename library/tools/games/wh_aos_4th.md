# Warhammer Age of Sigmar (4th edition)

One file per game: the game, its factions, the source summary, the mechanics (patterns) and the real-world cases.

## The game
*Claude's summary (Oct 2026), from general knowledge of the game and the rules and terms under Mechanics; not from the sources below.*
- Genre: tabletop miniatures wargame (Games Workshop, Age of Sigmar 4th edition 2024); armies built to a points limit from regiments.
- Battle: 5 battle rounds; after the first, a priority roll decides who goes first, which can give a double turn (in matched play this gives up that turn's battle tactic).
- Turn: start of turn, Hero, Movement, Shooting, Charge, Combat (players alternate fighting, strike-first and strike-last change the order), End of turn.
- Attacks: hit, wound, save, then ward saves for some units; then damage.
- Command points: spent on commands such as All-out Attack, Redeploy and Counter-charge.
- Army rules: battle traits, battle formations, heroic traits, artefacts, spell and prayer lores.
- Win: victory points from objectives and battle tactics.
- Grand Alliances: Order, Chaos, Death and Destruction.

## Factions

### Stormcast Eternals
*Claude:* Order: forged warriors of Sigmar who return after death and strike from the heavens.
Balanced, durable, few elite units [1]. Traits: units wait in the Celestial Realm and arrive anywhere; once per battle a
destroyed unit is replaced [st].
### Cities of Sigmar
*Claude:* Order: human, duardin and aelf city armies with combined arms.
Mortal hosts with diverse options [1]. Traits: orders that let officers boost nearby units [ci].
### Kharadron Overlords
*Claude:* Order: sky-ship duardin with guns.
Shooting from airborne gunboats; weak melee [1]. Traits: skyvessels carry units and wait in the clouds [kh].
### Sylvaneth
*Claude:* Order: forest spirits that teleport between woods.
Mobile hit-and-run, up to half the army in reserve [1]. Traits: overgrowth spreads from terrain; slain Dryads return; units
in the overgrowth heal or return models; enemies there pay an extra command point [sy].
### Daughters of Khaine
*Claude:* Order: aelf cultists who grow stronger each round.
Fast blood-focused melee, buffs, very vulnerable [1]. Traits: blood rites unlock blessings that buff units [dk].
### Fyreslayers
*Claude:* Order: duardin berserkers with runes.
Melee, runic ward saves, slow [1]. Traits: runes each round; Rune of Fury makes units strike first against chargers [fy].
### Idoneth Deepkin
*Claude:* Order: sea aelves who raid and strike first.
Fast elite army whose tide changes its abilities by round [1]. Traits: tides give cover, run-and-charge, Strike-First [id].
### Lumineth Realm-lords
*Claude:* Order: disciplined aelves with magic and elementals.
Strong magic and shooting [1]. Traits: one rune a round [lu].
### Seraphon
*Claude:* Order: lizardmen and astral magic, units summoned from the stars.
Versatile units, strong wizards, summoning through conjuration [1]. Traits: the Great Plan: choose an Asterism to prophesy [se].
### Slaves to Darkness
*Claude:* Chaos: mortal warriors marked by the Dark Gods.
Strong melee, Marks of Chaos [1]. Traits: pledge a unit to a god; Dark Apotheosis points for fighting or holding a far
objective, spent to heal or become a Daemon Prince [sd].
### Blades of Khorne
*Claude:* Chaos: blood and melee; no wizards, prayers instead.
Aggressive fast melee daemons; Blood Tithe rewards deaths [1]. Traits: a tithe point whenever any unit is destroyed, spent to
unlock abilities [bk].
### Disciples of Tzeentch
*Claude:* Chaos: daemons and sorcerers, magic and fate.
Magic-heavy, many spells [1]. Traits: illusions send units to reserve and swap them; fate points from setbacks, spent on
healing, casting and charges [tz].
### Skaven
*Claude:* Chaos: ratmen hordes, warpstone weapons.
Horde with poison and plague attacks, inventive weapons [1]. Traits: units in the tunnels below arrive from Gnawholes [sk].
### Hedonites of Slaanesh
*Claude:* Chaos: fast daemons of excess.
Speedy glass-cannon daemons, quick high-damage strikes [1]. Traits: Paragons; heroes force enemy charges to end near them [sl].
### Beasts of Chaos
*Claude:* Chaos: beastmen ambushes.
Fast, mobile beastmen; units die easily [1]. Traits: ambush reserve from the battlefield edge [bc].
### Maggotkin of Nurgle
*Claude:* Chaos: plague daemons, tough and slow.
Very tough, slow, mortal wounds [1]. Traits: disease spreads to enemies and deals mortal damage each turn [nu].
### Helsmiths of Hashut
*Claude:* Chaos: fire-daemon duardin with war machines.
Not in [1]. Traits: daemonic power points shared by desolation tokens on terrain and objectives [hh].
### Nighthaunt
*Claude:* Death: ghosts that fly, ignore terrain and frighten.
Spectral, flying, fast, weak saves [1]. Traits: ignore save penalties; redeploy a unit once a turn; enemies in combat lose
control score [nh].
### Flesh-eater Courts
*Claude:* Death: ghoul courts in a shared delusion.
Ghouls and monsters that summon units [1]. Traits: delusions chosen each turn; heroes heal; destroyed units replaced; slain
models returned [fe].
### Soulblight Gravelords
*Claude:* Death: vampires leading undead that rise again.
Vampire-led skeleton and zombie horde [1]. Traits: units rise from the grave; destroyed units replaced at half size; heroes
heal; vampires heal after fighting [sb].
### Ossiarch Bonereapers
*Claude:* Death: bone constructs, durable and disciplined.
Tanky, disciplined; its army rule replaces command points [1]. Traits: relentless discipline points spent on abilities [os].
### Ironjawz
*Claude:* Destruction: armoured orruks, fast melee.
Brutally simple, no ranged weapons [1]. Traits: Waaagh!: +1 charge and +1 Attacks for the turn [ij].
### Kruleboyz
*Claude:* Destruction: sneaky orruks with poison and tricks.
Cunning, devious [1]. Traits: Dirty Tricks; mortal damage on units that charged them; enemies strike last [kr].
### Bonesplitterz
*Claude:* Destruction: savage orruks hunting monsters.
Not covered on its own in [1]. Traits: bonuses against chargers and Monsters; a once-per-battle Waaagh! [bs].
### Gloomspite Gitz
*Claude:* Destruction: grots, squigs, spiders and troggoths, moon-powered.
Horde, magic or cavalry [1]. Traits: the Bad Moon's face changes each round and gives different bonuses [gg].
### Ogor Mawtribes
*Claude:* Destruction: ogors and beasts, heavy charges.
Tough ogors, heavy charges, healing [1]. Traits: Bull Charge pushes an enemy 3" and deals mortal damage; ambush reserve [og].
### Sons of Behemat
*Claude:* Destruction: armies of giants.
Giants, few units, simple [1]. Traits: mortal damage when they move, charge, fight; leave for reserve and return [sob].

# Concept tree v3 (Step 1 of the tag-fix plan, 13 Oct 2026; Claude's design). Built from the player's point of view, for a
# lane-control card battler: every effect answers first "does this help or hurt, and whom".
# One line per concept: id | name | facet | parents (comma-separated, empty for a root) | what counts | what doesn't count (and where it goes)
# Examples (two real cards that fit, one look-alike that doesn't) are in concept_examples.tsv. Read by concept_tree.py.

## Effects: the four branches
effect | Effects | effect |  | What a card does when it works; every effect sits under who it helps or hurts. | When it happens is a trigger, what it depends on is a condition, how the game is laid out is structure.
helps_me | Helps my side | effect | effect | The effect leaves the card owner's side stronger, safer, faster, or with more or better cards or resources. | Making the enemy weaker is hurts_enemy, even though it helps me too.
hurts_enemy | Hurts the enemy | effect | effect | The effect damages, removes, weakens, stops or disrupts the opponent's units, cards, resources or plans. | Losses on my own side are drawback.
drawback | Hurts my side (drawback) | effect | effect | The card costs its owner something on their own side beyond its normal cost: own units, cards, life, attacks, or something given to the opponent. | An optional extra payment for a bonus is kicker; paying a card's normal cost is not a drawback.
game_rules | Neutral game rules | effect | effect | The effect changes how the game, a lane or playing cards works for both players alike, or works the same whichever side it touches. | A rule that only binds the opponent is disruption.

## Helps my side
buff | Make a unit stronger (buff) | effect | helps_me | A unit gains stats or a combat ability it did not have: more power or health, first strike, flying, extra attacks, reach, piercing. | Improving a card still in hand is in_hand_buff; a unit printed with high stats is not buffed.
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | effect | buff | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.
temporary_buff | Temporary bonus (this turn only) | effect | buff,temporary_effect | A unit's bonus lasts only this turn, round or fight. | A bonus that stays is stat_gain or permanent_growth.
permanent_growth | Grows again and again | effect | buff | A unit gains stats repeatedly (each turn, each trigger) and keeps them. | A single lasting bonus is stat_gain.
stat_multiply | Double or multiply stats | effect | buff | A stat or a total is doubled, tripled or multiplied. | Adding a fixed amount is stat_gain.
first_strike | First strike (hits before the enemy) | combat | buff | The unit deals its combat damage before the enemy can strike back. | Attacking the turn it arrives is haste.
multi_attack | Attack more than once | combat | buff | The unit attacks or strikes more than once per turn or round. | One attack that hits several targets the player chose is multi_target_attack; readying a used unit is untap.
counterattack | Strike back when attacked (counterattack, thorns) | combat | buff | When the unit is attacked or hit, it deals damage back to the attacker. | Damage it deals when it attacks is on_attack.
piercing | Ignore armour or defence | combat | buff | The unit's damage or attacks ignore or lower the target's armour, saves or defence. | Damage that carries on to another target is excess_damage.
evasion | Hard to block (flying, unblockable) | combat | buff | The unit can't be blocked, can be blocked only by some units, or hits the player past blockers. | Can't be targeted is immunity; hidden until it acts is stealth.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | combat | buff,positional | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Damage from a spell is damage.
multi_target_attack | Damage to several chosen targets | combat | damage,scope_multi | The player picks every target of one damage effect: "3 to an enemy and 1 to another", "up to 3 chosen characters", damage divided as you choose. | Damage that also lands around the target, in a row or on all is aoe_damage; random targets are random_target; several separate attacks are multi_attack.
excess_damage | Excess damage carries on (trample) | effect | buff,damage | Damage beyond what kills the target goes on to the player or another unit. | Ignoring armour is piercing.
deathtouch | Any damage kills (deathtouch, poisonous) | combat | buff,destroy | Any damage this unit deals destroys the unit it hits. | Destroying by an effect without combat damage is destroy.
random_ability | Gain a random ability or keyword | effect | buff,random_effect | A unit gains a randomly chosen keyword or ability. | Other random results are random_effect.
spell_power | My spells and abilities get stronger | effect | helps_me | The owner's spells or abilities deal more damage or have bigger effects: spell damage, ability power, amplify. | A unit gaining stats is stat_gain.
protection | Keep my units alive (protection) | effect | helps_me | A unit on my side is guarded against damage, destruction or targeting. | Restoring health already lost is heal.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | effect | protection | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
block_armor | Block points that wear off | effect | damage_reduction | Points that absorb damage and run out or wear off, usually at the end of the turn. | A lasting reduction is damage_reduction.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | effect | protection | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
immunity | Immune / can't be targeted | effect | protection | A unit can't be targeted, or is immune to a damage type, effect or condition. | Hard to block is evasion; hidden until it acts is stealth.
cheat_death | Cheat death (survive lethal) | effect | protection | A unit that would die survives, or comes back in the same moment it dies. | Bringing back a unit that died earlier is revive.
bodyguard | Protect a specific ally (bodyguard) | effect | protection | The unit takes hits for, or turns attacks away from, a particular ally. | Forcing all enemy attacks onto itself is taunt.
taunt | Taunt (enemies must attack it) | effect | protection,targeting_rules | Enemies must attack or target this unit before other units. | Shielding one chosen ally is bodyguard.
stealth | Stealth / hidden until it acts | effect | protection,information | The unit can't be seen, targeted or attacked until it acts or a condition ends. | Untargetable for good is immunity.
heal | Restore health (heal) | effect | helps_me | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
lifesteal | Lifesteal / drain | effect | heal,damage | Damage dealt also heals its source or its owner. | Healing not tied to damage dealt is heal.
regeneration | Heal every turn (regeneration) | effect | heal | A unit heals again each turn or returns to full health each turn. | A single heal is heal.
card_advantage | Get more cards | effect | helps_me | The owner ends up with more cards: drawn, created or copied. | Choosing which card to get without getting more is selection.
draw | Draw cards | effect | card_advantage,card_flow | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
generate_cards | Create new cards | effect | card_advantage | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Units made on the board are summon.
card_quality | Better or chosen cards | effect | helps_me | The owner gets the cards they want, keeps them, reuses them or makes them better before playing them. | Simply getting more cards is card_advantage.
selection | Look at cards and pick (scry) | effect | card_quality,card_flow | The owner looks at several cards and chooses which to keep, take or put back. | Searching the deck for a stated kind of card is tutor.
tutor | Search the deck for a card (tutor) | effect | selection | The owner searches the deck for a card of a stated kind and takes it. | Drawing the top card is draw.
retain | Keep cards in hand across turns | effect | card_quality,card_flow | Cards that would leave the hand at the end of the turn stay in it. | Returning a played unit to hand is return_to_hand.
recycle | Reuse my cards (shuffle back) | effect | card_quality,card_flow | The owner's used or discarded cards go back into deck or hand to be used again. | An enemy unit sent to its deck is bounce_enemy.
upgrade | Upgrade / level up a card | effect | card_quality | A card is upgraded, levelled up or enhanced into a better version of itself. | A plain stat rise on the board is stat_gain.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | effect | card_quality | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
resource_advantage | More resources or cheaper plays | effect | helps_me | The owner gets more to spend, or pays less for their cards. | Making the opponent pay more is cost_increase.
resource_gain | Gain resources (ramp) | effect | resource_advantage,economy | The owner gains extra mana, energy, supply or another resource, now or for good. | Cards costing less is cost_reduction.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | effect | resource_advantage,economy | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.
cost_set | Set a cost to a fixed number | effect | resource_advantage,economy | A card's cost becomes a fixed number (costs 0, costs 3). | A reduction by an amount is cost_reduction.
free_play | Put into play for free | effect | resource_advantage,economy,tempo | A card enters play without its cost being paid, by an effect or automatically. | A dead unit coming back is revive.
summon | Summon units / tokens | effect | helps_me,board_presence | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
revive | Bring back dead units (revive) | effect | helps_me,death | A unit that died or was destroyed earlier comes back to play or to hand. | Surviving at the moment of death is cheat_death.
self_mill | Fill my own discard pile (self-mill) | effect | helps_me,graveyard,card_flow | The owner puts cards from their own deck straight into their discard pile or graveyard. | Doing it to the opponent is mill.
speed | Act sooner or more often | effect | helps_me | The owner's units or turns act earlier or more times than normal. | Several strikes in one attack step is multi_attack.
haste | Act the turn it arrives (haste, rush) | combat | speed,tempo | A unit can attack or act on the turn it is played. | An effect that makes it attack at once is attack_now.
attack_now | Attack right away | combat | speed,tempo | An effect makes a unit attack at once, outside the normal attack step. | Being allowed to attack the turn it arrives is haste.
extra_turn | Extra turns or actions | effect | speed,tempo | The owner takes an extra turn, round or action. | Readying a used unit is untap.
untap | Ready a used unit again (untap) | effect | speed | A used or tapped unit or card is readied so it can act again. | A whole extra turn is extra_turn.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | effect | helps_me,positional | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
swap | Swap positions | effect | movement | Two units trade places, or trade lanes or locations. | Trading stats is stat_gain or stat_loss; trading hands or decks is hand_deck_swap.
blink | Blink (leave and come back fresh) | effect | helps_me | A unit leaves play and returns at once, reset, repeating its on-enter effect. | Going back to hand is return_to_hand.
return_to_hand | Return my unit to hand | effect | helps_me | The owner returns their own unit to hand, to replay it or save it. | Sending an enemy unit back is bounce_enemy.
copy | Copy cards or units | effect | helps_me | A copy of a card, unit or effect is made for the owner. | Doing a trigger again is repeat_trigger; a copy given to the opponent is enemy_benefit.
repeat_trigger | Repeat or double triggers | effect | helps_me | A trigger or effect happens again, or twice. | Copying a whole card is copy.
transform | Transform my card into another | effect | helps_me | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.
flexibility | Choose what a card does | effect | helps_me | The owner picks between effects, modes or forms. | A random pick is random_effect.
modal_choice | Choose one of several effects | effect | flexibility | The card offers several effects and the owner picks one or more. | Switching a unit between lasting forms is stances.
stances | Switch stances or forms | effect | flexibility | The owner switches a unit or hero between lasting modes. | A one-time pick is modal_choice.
steal | Steal from the opponent | effect | helps_me,hurts_enemy | The owner takes the opponent's units, cards, resources or text for themselves. | Destroying them instead is removal.

## Hurts the enemy
damage | Deal damage | effect | hurts_enemy | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | effect | damage,scope_multi | Damage reaches several units without the player choosing each: around the target, a row, a lane, an area or all. Full or reduced damage both count. | If the player picks every target it is multi_target_attack; random targets are random_target.
damage_over_time | Damage over time (poison, burn) | effect | damage | Damage repeats over turns: poison, burn, bleed. | One-time damage is damage.
direct_damage | Damage to the player or base | effect | damage | Damage goes straight to the enemy player, hero or base. | Damage to units is damage.
ping | Small repeatable damage | effect | damage | A small amount of damage (1 or 2) that can be dealt again and again. | One big hit is damage.
collision_damage | Bump damage (pushed into something) | combat | forced_movement,damage | A unit takes damage from being pushed into a unit, wall or obstacle. | Damage from the push effect itself is damage.
removal | Remove an enemy unit | effect | hurts_enemy | An enemy unit leaves the board: destroyed, exiled, sent to hand or deck, or turned into something harmless. | Weakening it but leaving it there is debuff.
destroy | Destroy outright | effect | removal | A unit is destroyed or killed regardless of its health. | Killing by damage is damage; destroying my own unit for value is sacrifice.
banish | Banish / exile (gone for good) | effect | removal | A unit or card is removed from the game for good, beyond the discard pile. | Leaving and coming back is blink.
board_wipe | Board wipe | effect | removal,scope_multi | Most or all units on the board, or in a lane, are destroyed or removed. | Damage to several that may leave survivors is aoe_damage.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | effect | removal,tempo | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | effect | removal | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | My own card turning into another is transform.
debuff | Make a unit weaker (debuff) | effect | hurts_enemy | A unit loses stats or gets a combat penalty. | Stopping it from acting is suppress.
stat_loss | Stat loss (-power, -health) | effect | debuff | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes down, or is set or swapped to a lower value. | Not being able to attack is attack_lock or cant_attack.
attack_zero | Attack set to 0 | effect | no_damage_output,debuff | A unit's attack, power or strength becomes 0. | Lowered by an amount but above 0 is stat_loss.
mark_target | Mark a target for later hits | effect | hurts_enemy,condition | A target is marked so that later effects or attacks hit it harder. | A lasting stat loss is stat_loss.
suppress | Stop an enemy unit from acting | effect | hurts_enemy | An enemy unit can't attack, deal damage, block, move, use abilities, or act at all. | Lowering its stats is debuff.
no_damage_output | Deals no damage | effect | suppress | The unit deals no damage, however it is done: can't attack, attack set to 0, damage prevented. | Can't block is cant_block.
attack_lock | Can't attack (lock on an enemy) | effect | no_damage_output | An enemy unit can't attack, often for a turn or round. | My own unit that never attacks is cant_attack.
damage_prevention | Its damage is prevented | effect | no_damage_output | Damage an enemy unit would deal is prevented. | Damage I would take being reduced is damage_reduction.
cant_block | Can't block | effect | suppress | An enemy unit can't block or intercept. | Can't attack is attack_lock.
immobilize | Can't move (root) | effect | suppress | An enemy unit can't move: root, snare, pin. | Moved against its will is forced_movement.
silence | Silence (abilities removed) | effect | suppress | A unit's abilities or text are removed or switched off. | Lowering stats is stat_loss.
disable | Can't act at all (stun, freeze) | effect | suppress,no_damage_output,cant_block,immobilize | The unit can't act at all: stun, freeze, sleep, skip its turn. | Losing only its attack is attack_lock.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | effect | hurts_enemy,positional | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Moving my own units is movement.
forced_attack | Force enemy units to attack | effect | hurts_enemy | Enemy units are made to attack, often a chosen or bad target. | My own unit that must attack each turn is drawback.
disruption | Disrupt the opponent's plans | effect | hurts_enemy | The opponent's hand, deck, resources, plays or board space are interfered with. | Damaging or removing their units is damage or removal.
hand_disruption | Attack the opponent's hand | effect | disruption | Cards in the opponent's hand are discarded, taken, weakened, made dearer or looked at. | Discarding my own cards is discard.
counter_spell | Counter / cancel a play | effect | disruption | An opponent's card or effect is cancelled before it resolves. | Removing a unit after it is in play is removal.
cost_increase | Make the opponent's cards cost more | effect | disruption,economy | The opponent's cards cost more. | Making my cards cheaper is cost_reduction.
mill | Mill the opponent's deck | effect | disruption,card_flow | Cards are put from the opponent's deck into their discard pile or removed. | Doing it to my own deck is self_mill.
junk_cards | Junk cards into a deck or hand | effect | disruption,card_flow | Useless or harmful cards (curses, statuses, rocks) are put into a deck or hand to clog it. | Useless units on the board are board_clog.
board_clog | Clog the opponent's board | effect | disruption,board_limit | Weak, useless or harmful units are put onto the opponent's side to take their slots. | Units on my own side are summon.

## Hurts my side (drawback)
enemy_benefit | Helps the opponent | role | drawback | The card gives the opponent something: cards, power, resources, units or a copy. | Giving them harmful units is board_clog.
friendly_fire | Hits my own side too | role | drawback | An effect also damages, weakens or destroys the owner's own units or player. | Destroying my own unit on purpose for value is sacrifice.
self_destruct | Destroys itself / temporary unit | effect | drawback,death | The unit destroys itself or leaves after a time or after use. | Destroying another own unit for value is sacrifice.
sacrifice | Sacrifice my own units | cost | drawback,death | The owner destroys or gives up their own unit, as a cost or for value. | An enemy being made to sacrifice is destroy.
discard | Discard my own cards | effect | drawback,card_flow | The owner discards cards from their own hand, as a cost or to fuel effects. | Making the opponent discard is hand_disruption.
exhaust | One use (exhausted for the battle) | effect | drawback,card_flow | A card is removed for the rest of the battle after use, or other cards of mine are. | Removing a card from the game for good is banish.
cant_attack | Can't attack (my own unit) | combat | debuff,drawback | The unit itself can't attack: defender, wall. | A lock put on an enemy is attack_lock.
donate | Give my units or cards to the opponent | effect | drawback | The owner gives their own units or cards to the opponent. | Putting harmful units on their side is board_clog.

## Neutral game rules
rule_change | Change the game's rules | effect | game_rules,structure | An effect changes a rule of the game or of a zone for everyone. | A rule that binds only the opponent is disruption.
play_restriction | Limit what can be played | effect | game_rules | Where, what or how many cards can be played is limited: can't be played here, only N per turn. | Cancelling a play already made is counter_spell.
lane_closure | Close or ruin a lane | effect | play_restriction,rule_change,lanes | A lane, location or zone stops being usable: flooded, destroyed, locked. | A lane with an extra rule is lane_rule.
forced_placement | Force where cards go | effect | play_restriction,placement_rules | Where units are played or end up is dictated: all cards must be played here, played cards move here. | A unit moved after it was placed is movement or forced_movement.
hand_deck_swap | Swap or replace hands or decks | effect | game_rules,card_flow | Hands or decks are exchanged, replaced or reshuffled and redrawn. | Taking single cards from the opponent is steal.
rewind | Rewind / reset to an earlier state | effect | game_rules | A unit, card or the game goes back to an earlier state: reset to base stats, undo, redo. | A plain heal is heal.

## Combat rules
combat | Combat rules | combat |  | How attacks, blocks and targeting work. | What a single unit gains for combat is buff.
advantage | Advantage / disadvantage (roll twice) | combat | random_effect,combat | A roll is made twice and the better or worse result is kept. | A flat bonus to the roll is buff or debuff.
blocking | Blocking / interception rules | combat | targeting_rules,combat | Rules for who can block or intercept an attacker. | A unit that can't block is cant_block.
damage_types | Damage types, resistances and weaknesses | combat | combat | Damage has a type (fire, poison...) and units resist or are weak to types. | Damage over turns is damage_over_time.
saving_throws | Defender rolls to resist (saves) | combat | random_effect,combat | The target rolls to resist an effect or damage. | Armour that always reduces damage is damage_reduction.
targeting_rules | Targeting rules | combat | combat | Rules for what may be targeted: only the front row, only damaged units, the nearest. | Forcing attacks onto one unit is taunt.
target_choice | Choose attack targets | combat | targeting_rules | The owner chooses which unit an attack or effect hits. | A random target is random_effect.

## Conditions
condition | Conditions | condition |  | An effect works only if, unless or when something is true. | When it happens is a trigger.
adjacency | Neighbours matter (adjacency) | condition | positional | The effect uses the units or tiles beside it for any purpose: hits them, moves them, gives or needs a bonus, or the lane or location to the left or right. | Several lanes away is cross_lane.
assists_support | Help from nearby allies (assists) | condition | adjacency,synergy_payoff | Nearby allies add a bonus to an action: assists, support, flanking help. | Bonuses for any ally anywhere are tribal or aura.
combo_sequence | Order of my plays matters (combo) | condition | condition | The effect cares about the order of the owner's plays: the last card played, played another card first. | Order between the players is reveal_order.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | condition | condition | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
condition_life | Damaged unit matters | condition | condition | The effect cares that a unit is damaged or wounded: enrage, bloodied, below full health. | The player being at low life is low_life.
low_life | Low life matters (player behind) | condition | condition,comeback | The effect cares that the owner's player or hero is at low life. | A unit being damaged is condition_life.
cost_condition | Cost matters | condition | condition | The effect cares about a card's cost: 1-cost cards, odd or even cost, N or more. | Changing costs is cost_reduction or cost_set.
count_payoff | Grows with what happened this game | condition | condition_count | The effect grows with how many times something happened this game: cards played, units died, spells cast. | A count of what is on the board now is condition_count.
death_count | Counts deaths | condition | death,condition_count | The effect counts or scales with how many units died. | A single death trigger is death_trigger.
deck_condition | Deck contents matter | condition | condition | The effect cares about what is in the deck: deck size, cards in it, an empty deck. | The top card is deck_position.
deck_position | Top or bottom of the deck matters | condition | card_flow,condition | The top or bottom card of a deck matters: looked at, revealed or changed. | Searching the whole deck is tutor.
flanking | Facing / attack from behind | condition | positional | Facing, flanking or attacking from the side or behind matters. | Simple neighbours are adjacency.
hand_position | Position in hand matters | condition | condition,card_flow | Where a card sits in hand (leftmost, oldest) or how long it has been there matters. | Hand size is hand_size.
hand_size | Hand size matters | condition | condition,card_flow | How many cards are in hand matters: empty or full hand. | Position in hand is hand_position.
link_formation | Formations (pairs, chains) | condition | positional,condition | Units must stand in a pattern: pairs, chains, circuits. | Simple neighbours are adjacency.
lone_unit | Alone matters | condition | condition,adjacency | The effect rewards a unit that stands alone: the only unit in a lane, no neighbours. | Many units are board_presence.
quests | Quests / objectives with rewards | condition | condition_count | A goal with progress gives a reward when it is completed. | A goal that scores points is scoring_objectives.
race_objective | First to do it wins the reward | condition | quests | The reward goes to the first player to meet a condition. | A goal for one player only is quests.
scoring_objectives | Objectives that score points | condition | quests,points_scoring | Objective or secret-goal cards score points when their condition is met. | Rewards other than points are quests.
random_effect | Random results | condition | randomness | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | A random ability is random_ability.
random_target | Random target | condition | random_effect | The effect picks its target or targets at random among units, players or places that exist: "a random enemy", "two random minions". | A random card, a random summoned unit or a random amount is random_effect.
stacking_keyword | Stacking keywords / counters | condition | condition_count | A keyword or counter stacks and grows with each copy. | A one-time count is condition_count.
stat_condition | Stats matter (highest, lowest) | condition | condition | The effect cares about stats: the highest or lowest power, N or more attack. | Changing stats is buff or debuff.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | condition | condition,synergy_payoff | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Counting as another type is type_grant.
variable_stats | Stats equal to a count | condition | condition | A unit's stats are computed from the game state: power equal to the number of something. | A fixed stat rise is stat_gain.
variety_payoff | Variety matters (all different) | condition | condition_count | The effect rewards different costs, types or names, or no duplicates. | Many of the same tribe is tribal.

## Costs
cost_type | Alternative costs | cost | economy | A card can be paid with something other than the usual resource. | An optional extra payment for a bonus is kicker.
kicker | Optional extra cost for a bonus | cost | cost_type | Paying extra is optional and adds a bonus effect: kicker, enhance. | A required alternative cost is cost_type.
life_payment | Pay life as a cost | cost | cost_type | The owner pays life or health to play or use something. | Damage to my own units is friendly_fire.
overload_debt | Pay later (overload, debt) | cost | cost_type,delayed | Part of the cost is paid later: locked resources next turn, debt. | A cost paid now is cost_type.

## Resources
economy | Economy & resources | resource |  | How players get and spend resources. | Cards themselves are card_advantage.
coin_economy | Second currency for abilities | resource | economy,multiple_resources | A second currency (coins, crowns) is spent on abilities. | Shop gold between fights is shop_gold.
graveyard | Discard pile / graveyard as a resource | resource | death | Cards in the discard pile or graveyard are used: played, counted or eaten. | Filling it from my deck is self_mill.
high_cost | Expensive card | resource | economy | The card is at the top of its game's cost curve (top 10% of costs). | Cost conditions are cost_condition.
multiple_resources | Several resource types | resource | economy | The game uses several resource types. | One resource gained is resource_gain.
resource_banking | Save unspent resources | resource | economy | Unspent resources are kept for later, or reward the owner. | Gaining new resources is resource_gain.
resource_conversion | Convert one resource into another | resource | economy,multiple_resources | One resource is turned into another. | Gaining resources from nothing is resource_gain.
shop_gold | Shops and gold | resource | economy,run_structure | Gold is spent in shops to buy cards or items. | A currency spent in battle is coin_economy.
upkeep | Upkeep (ongoing resource drain) | resource | economy | Resources must be paid again and again to keep things: food, fuel, upkeep. | A one-time cost is cost_type.

## Roles
role | Design roles | role |  | The job a card does in a deck. | What the card does is an effect.
alt_wincon | Alternate win condition | role | win_condition,role | The card gives another way to win the game. | Scoring more points is points_scoring.
board_presence | Board presence / going wide | role | role | Having many units on the board. | One big unit is tank.
comeback | Comeback / catch-up | role | role | The card helps the player who is behind or losing. | A damaged unit is condition_life.
finisher | Finisher / closer | role | role | The card closes out the game. | Steady damage is damage.
fodder | Fodder / disposable bodies | role | role | Bodies meant to die or be used up. | Units meant to last are tank.

# Reading request: lorcana
Read the game block once, then every card below. Judge only against the concept definitions at the end of the game block; use
the game's rules, keywords and factions to understand what a card does. Write nothing but the answer lines.

## How to read a card
- Go clause by clause and write each concept the clause expresses, with the exact words that justify it, copied character for
  character from the card's name or text (a short phrase is best). A script rejects any tag whose words are not in the text.
- Use the most specific concept that fits; do not add its parent as well. Check sibling concepts against their "Not:" lines.
- A clause can express several concepts (a trigger, a condition and an effect). Triggers and conditions count too.
- A keyword or ability named on the card counts: tag that keyword's effects, quoting the keyword's name.
- Keyword or glossary entries: tag what the keyword itself does.
- A card with no mechanic (lore, a bare name, plain stats with nothing else) gets an empty answer.
- Do not guess beyond the text. If the text doesn't say it, it isn't there.

## Answer: one line per card line below, in the same order
`<ids>|<concept>:<role>:<exact words>;<concept>:<role>:<exact words>;...`   (nothing after `|` for a card with no mechanic)
<ids> as given on the card line. Roles: does (the card does it), grants (it gives it to another card), benefits (it is better
when this happens), counters (it answers this), cost (it is part of the cost). The exact words never contain `;` or `|`:
choose a shorter phrase.

# Game block: Disney Lorcana (sets 1-12 (12 incomplete) and Illumineer's Quest Q1-Q2)

## Rules
Two-player card game; ink (any card with the inkwell symbol can be put face down as ink, one per turn) pays for cards; characters quest for lore (first to 20 wins) or challenge exerted opposing characters; songs can be sung by exerting a character instead of paying; locations give lore each turn and characters move to them by paying their move cost.
Keyword definitions are the reminder text the cards print. Reprints and alternate printings left out.

## Keywords
- Alert: This character can challenge as if they had Evasive.
- Bodyguard: This character may enter play exerted. An opposing character who challenges one of your characters must choose one with Bodyguard if able.
- Boost: Once during your turn, you may pay N ink to put the top card of your deck facedown under this character.
- Challenger: While challenging, this character gets +N strength.
- Evasive: Only characters with Evasive can challenge this character.
- Puppy Shift: You may pay N ink to play this on top of one of your Puppy characters.
- Reckless: This character can't quest and must challenge each turn if able.
- Resist: Damage dealt to this character is reduced by N.
- Rush: This character can challenge the turn they're played.
- Shift: You may pay N ink to play this on top of one of your characters named <name>.
- Sing Together: Any number of your or your teammates' characters with total cost N or more may exert to sing this song for free.
- Singer: This character counts as cost N to sing songs.
- Support: Whenever this character quests, you may add their strength to another chosen character's strength this turn.
- Universal Shift: You may pay N ink to play this on top of any one of your characters.
- Vanish: When an opponent chooses this character for an action, banish them.
- Ward: Opponents can't choose this character except to challenge.

## Rules and terms
- Exert: a card turned on its side; an exerted character can't use abilities that need exerting until it is readied. Characters ready again at the start of their owner's next turn.
- Quest: questing gathers lore, which wins the game. Reckless characters can't quest.
- Challenge: characters challenge opposing characters, using their Strength.
- Ink pays card costs; a newly played card must wait for its ink to dry before it can exert or quest.
- Songs: a character can sing a song for free when the song's ink cost fits its own; Singer N lets it sing songs costing up to N.

## Mechanics the game's guides name

Read the game block once, then every card below. Judge only against the concept definitions at the end of the game block; use
the game's rules, keywords and factions to understand what a card does. Write nothing but the answer lines.

## How to read a card
- Go clause by clause and write each concept the clause expresses, with the exact words that justify it, copied character for
  character from the card's name or text (a short phrase is best). A script rejects any tag whose words are not in the text.
- Use the most specific concept that fits; do not add its parent as well. Check sibling concepts against their "Not:" lines.
- A clause can express several concepts (a trigger, a condition and an effect). Triggers and conditions count too.
- A keyword or ability named on the card counts: tag that keyword's effects, quoting the keyword's name.
- Keyword or glossary entries: tag what the keyword itself does.
- A card with no mechanic (lore, a bare name, plain stats with nothing else) gets an empty answer.
- Do not guess beyond the text. If the text doesn't say it, it isn't there.

## Answer: one line per card line below, in the same order
`<ids>|<concept>:<role>:<exact words>;<concept>:<role>:<exact words>;...`   (nothing after `|` for a card with no mechanic)
<ids> as given on the card line. Roles: does (the card does it), grants (it gives it to another card), benefits (it is better
when this happens), counters (it answers this), cost (it is part of the cost). The exact words never contain `;` or `|`:
choose a shorter phrase.

# Game block: Star Wars: Unlimited (sets SOR to ASH and Intro Battle: Hoth)

## Rules
Two-player card game. Each player has a leader and a base with health; units are played into one of two arenas, ground and space, and attack enemy units in the same arena or the enemy base; the first base destroyed loses. Players take actions in turn and pass; any card can be played face down as a resource. The leader can deploy as a unit.
Keyword and trait names translated from the French export (keywords checked against the English texts); keyword definitions are printed reminder text where cards have one, else Claude's summary.

## Keywords
- Ambush: When you play this unit, it may attack an enemy unit.
- Bounty: When it is defeated or captured, the opponent collects the bounty.
- Coordinate: It has this ability while you control 3 or more units.
- Exploit: While playing this card, defeat up to 2 units you control. This card costs 2 resources less for each unit defeated this way.
- Grit: This unit gets +1/+0 for each damage on it.
- Hidden: This unit can't be attacked if it was played this phase.
- Overwhelm: When attacking an enemy unit, deal excess damage to the opponent's base.
- Piloting: It may be played as an upgrade on a friendly Vehicle unit without a Pilot, for its Piloting cost.
- Plot: When you deploy a leader, you may play this card from your resources, paying its cost. Replace it with the top card of your deck.
- Raid: This unit gets +1/+0 while attacking.
- Restore: When this unit attacks, heal 1 damage from your base.
- Saboteur: When this unit attacks, ignore Sentinel and defeat the defender's Shields.
- Sentinel: Units in this arena can't attack your non-Sentinel units or your base.
- Shielded: When you play this unit, give a Shield token to it.
- Smuggle: It may be played from your resources for its Smuggle cost; replace it with the top card of your deck.
- Support: When you play this unit, you may attack with another unit. It gains this unit's other abilities for this attack.

## From the game's rules pages
# Source: https://rulespal.com/star-wars-unlimited/rulebook
- Zones: each player has a base and a leader in the base zone, a resource zone (face-down resources), deck, hand and discard pile; the ground arena and the space arena are shared, and a unit attacks an enemy unit in its own arena or the opposing base.
- Rounds: an action phase in which players alternate one action (play a card, attack with a unit, use an action ability, take the initiative, pass) until both pass in a row; then the regroup phase: each player draws 2, may put one card from hand into resources, and readies all exhausted cards.
- Ready and exhaust: non-leader units and resources enter play exhausted; paying costs exhausts resources; a ready unit exhausts to attack.
- Attacks: attacker and defender deal damage equal to their power to each other at the same time. A unit with damage equal to or over its HP is defeated (a leader unit returns exhausted to the base zone). A player whose base reaches 0 HP loses.
- Upgrades attach to friendly or enemy units and change power and HP; they are defeated when their unit leaves play. Experience tokens are upgrades giving +1 power and +1 HP.
- Initiative: its holder acts first each round; taking it is an action, after which that player passes for the rest of the phase.

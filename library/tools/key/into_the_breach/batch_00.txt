Each island has a set variety of Vek that can spawn, one of which is a Psion, a supporting unit that grants a passive bonus to others. They emerge from the ground on certain tiles, which can be intercepted at the cost of 1 health during the enemy turn. Before the player turn any active Vek will move to a certain tile and prepare to attack it, aiming for either a mech, building or objective. These attacks are executed during the enemy turn unless they are cancelled or the Vek is killed beforehand. Vek are divided into five main groups: *Common Vek are those that can spawn on any island and make up most of the spawns on a mission. All of them have a soft limit on spawns per mission that can be bypassed if the spawning algorithm needs to. * Rare Vek are those whose attacks are more unique than common ones and do not appear on early islands. *Psions, which provide passive bonuses to other Vek when spawned. Note that the Psion itself does not benefit from the passive bonus. *Minor Vek, which are spawned by other Vek, are typically very weak, and bypass the spawning algorithm. They are not worth any experience and are not affected by Psion bonuses. *Hive Leaders, bosses found on the final mission of each island, the Volcanic Hive and normal missions on the fourth island in Unfair difficulty.

Description: Passives confer a constant benefit to all Mechs in the squad, without the need of activating them during an encounter. Since an action does not need to be used to activate passives, their effects will persist even when the Mech equipped with a passive is submerged into Water, a Lava Tile, an A.C.I.D. Note that powering Upgrade 1 is never a prerequisite for powering Upgrade 2. Mechs with Passives in Custom Squads: When creating a Custom Squad, it is possible to choose a Mech which has a passive equipped multiple times.

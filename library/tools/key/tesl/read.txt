# Reading request: tesl
Read the game block once, then every card below. Judge only against the concept definitions at the end of the game block; use
the game's rules, keywords and factions to understand what a card does. Write nothing but the answer lines.

## How to read a card
- Go clause by clause and write each concept the clause expresses, with the exact words that justify it, copied character for
  character from the card's name or text (a short phrase is best). A script rejects any tag whose words are not in the text.
- Use the most specific concept that fits; do not add its parent as well. Check sibling concepts against their "Not:" lines.
- A clause can express several concepts (a trigger, a condition and an effect). Triggers and conditions count too.
- A keyword or ability named on the card counts: tag that keyword's effects, quoting the keyword's name.
- Keyword or glossary entries: tag what the keyword itself does.
- A card with no mechanic (lore, a bare name, plain stats with nothing else) gets an empty answer.
- Do not guess beyond the text. If the text doesn't say it, it isn't there.

## Answer: one line per card line below, in the same order
`<ids>|<concept>:<role>:<exact words>;<concept>:<role>:<exact words>;...`   (nothing after `|` for a card with no mechanic)
<ids> as given on the card line. Roles: does (the card does it), grants (it gives it to another card), benefits (it is better
when this happens), counters (it answers this), cost (it is part of the cost). The exact words never contain `;` or `|`:
choose a shorter phrase.

# Game block: The Elder Scrolls: Legends (sets up to Houses of Morrowind (2018))

## Rules
Two-player card game with two lanes: the field lane and the shadow lane, where a creature gets Cover (cannot be attacked) on the turn it arrives. Each player has 30 health with five runes at 25, 20, 15, 10 and 5; breaking a rune draws a card, which may be played for free if it has Prophecy. Magicka grows by 1 each turn. Creatures attack creatures in their own lane or the opponent.
Keyword field from the source; keyword definitions are Claude's summaries. Sets after Houses of Morrowind are missing.

## Keywords
- Activate: Triggers when you use the support (supports have a number of uses).
- Assemble: Choose one of two bonuses; all your Factotums get it.
- Battle: Two creatures deal damage to each other without attacking.
- Beast Form: 
- Betray: After you play it, you may sacrifice a creature to play it again.
- Breakthrough: Damage it deals beyond what kills a creature is dealt to the opponent.
- Charge: It can attack the turn it is played.
- Cover: It cannot be attacked (creatures in the shadow lane get Cover for a turn).
- Drain: On your turn, damage it deals heals you that much.
- Evolves: It transforms into a stronger creature when its condition is met.
- Exalt: Pay extra magicka when you play it to get a bonus.
- Guard: Enemy creatures in its lane must attack it before attacking anything else.
- Last Gasp: Triggers when the creature dies.
- Lethal: Any damage it deals to a creature destroys that creature.
- Ongoing: 
- Pilfer: Triggers when it deals damage to the opponent.
- Plot: Triggers if you played another card this turn.
- Prophecy: When it is drawn from a broken rune, you may play it at once for free.
- Rally: When it attacks, a random creature in your hand gets +1/+1.
- Regenerate: At the start of your turn it heals all its damage.
- Shackle: A shackled creature cannot attack or block on its next turn.
- Shout: Each time you play a shout, all your copies of it level up.
- Silence: Removes all text and effects from a creature.
- Slay: Triggers when it destroys an enemy creature on your turn.
- Summon: Triggers when the creature is played.
- Treasure Hunt: It collects treasure when you draw cards of the listed kinds and gets a bonus once all are found.
- Unsummon: Returns a creature to its owner's hand.
- Ward: The first damage or effect that would hit it is prevented and the Ward is removed.

# Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Legends:Guard (and its keyword navigation)
- Enemy Guard creatures in a lane must be destroyed before other creatures in that lane can damage the opponent's health.
- The shadow lane gives Cover; damage to a player breaks Runes; some effects gain max magicka.

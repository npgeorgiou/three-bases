740674 | One Half Bones | One Half Bones is a variable stats present in Kaycee's Mod of Inscryption which causes a cards Power to equal half the Bones the player carries. The Lammergeier is the only card to possess this trait. /  / This behaviour can be seen as similar to the Enlarge Active Sigil from Act II, which costs 2 Bones to use and increases the Power and Health of the card. 740690 | Sprinter | Rulebook: At the end of the owner's turn, a card bearing this sigil will move in the direction inscribed in the sigil. /  / Sprinter is a sigil which causes the card bearing it to move once in the direction indicated by the Sigil when the owner's turn ends. /  / Behaviour: / The Sigil can point to the left or to the right, defaulting to the right when it is first played. If the arrow points to an edge, or another card, the Sigil will change direction before the card moves. If the card is obstructed on both the left and right, it shakes and does not move.  /  / Cards bearing this sigil move after all damage has been dealt, with each card moving in order from left to right: for example, if two Sprinter cards exist in the third and fourth lanes and both sigils point left, then the creature in the third lane will move left into the second lane, followed by the creature in the fourth moving into the third lane. This can lead to some unorthodox effects: if two Sprinter cards bear sigils pointing left and exist in the first and second lanes, then the creature in the first lane will attempt to move and fail, with the creature in the second lane turning around and moving right into the third lane, thereby creating an open space in the second lane. /  / Sprinter has a unique interaction with the Hefty and Rampager Sigils when it is added to a card. Cards that naturally bear either of latter Sigils that gain Sprinter will have their effect doubled. This can be used to strategic effect, such as using Rampager-Sprinter to narrow in on two lanes.
740691 | Squirrel Shedder | Rulebook: At the end of the owner's turn, a card bearing this sigil will move in the direction inscribed in the sigil and drop a Squirrel in its old space. /  / Squirrel Shedder is a Sigil present solely in Act II of Inscryption, unique to the Squirrel Ball card. It functions similarly to the Sprinter Sigil, causing the card bearing it to move to an adjacent space in the direction depicted by the arrow on the sigil at the end of its turn, if the space is unnoccupied. Setting it apart from Sprinter however, when a card bearing this sigil moves, the place it previously occupied will be filled by a free Squirrel card. /  / If a card bearing this sigil runs into the edge of the game board or another card, it will attempt to turn around and move in the other direction, though this may become difficult due to the trail of squirrels left in its wake.  /  / Notes: / - Squirrel Shedder shares many similarities with the Skeleton Crew sigil.

740699 | Trifurcated Strike | Rulebook: A card bearing this sigil will strike each opposing space to the left and right of the spaces across from it as well as the space in front of it. /  / Trifurcated Strike is a sigil that bestows a card the ability to strike the spaces to the left and right of its opposing space as well as the opposing space itself, meaning that it attacks three times in a single turn.  /  / Behaviour: / When a card bearing this sigil attacks, it strikes from left to right. If any attack causes it to perish, then subsequent attacks will not happen. If this card is in the leftmost or rightmost lane, then it will not attack the left and right spaces respectively, meaning it will only attack twice. /  / If a card bearing this sigil possesses the Sniper sigil as well, then the player will be allowed to choose three spaces to attack instead of one, and may choose to attack the same space multiple times. If a space occupied by a Repulsive creature is attacked, then only the attack on that space will be negated. /  / This sigil and Bifurcated Strike stack, resulting in 2 strikes to the left, 1 strike in the middle, and 2 strikes to the right. Additionally if a card possesses Trifurcated strike and/or Bifurcated Strike as well as Double Strike from Kaycee's Mod, the bonuses will stack, with all 3 allowing for a maximum of 6 strikes, 2 in each space.

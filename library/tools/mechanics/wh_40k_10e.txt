# term: Oath of Moment: at the start of the Command phase pick one enemy unit; Space Marines re-roll Hit rolls against it (wahapedia).
# term: Reanimation Protocols: at the end of the Command phase a Necron unit reanimates D3 wounds or returns destroyed models (wahapedia).
# term: Waaagh!: once per battle, for one round, Orks can charge after Advancing, +1 Strength and Attacks in melee, a 5+ invulnerable save (wahapedia).
# term: Synapse: Tyranids within 6" of a Synapse model test Battle-shock on 3D6 and get +1 melee Strength (wahapedia).
# term: For the Greater Good: Observers mark enemies Spotted; other T'au shoot them at +1 Ballistic Skill (wahapedia).
# term: Acts of Faith: a Miracle die replaces one roll; dice come each round and when a Sororitas unit is destroyed (wahapedia).
# term: Cult Ambush: destroyed Genestealer Cults units can come back from reserve with Resurgence points (wahapedia).
# term: Battle Focus: Aeldari spend tokens for extra movement and to shoot or charge after moving; Strands of Fate: a pool of dice that replace rolls (wahapedia).

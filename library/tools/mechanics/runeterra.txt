# term: Barrier: the unit ignores the first instance of damage it takes in a round of combat (mobalytics regions overview).
# term: Frostbite: sets an enemy unit's Power to 0 for the round; Shatter deals 4 to a 0-Power unit (mobalytics; pcgamer).
# term: Elusive: can only be blocked by other Elusive units (pcgamer).
# term: Recall: returns a unit to its owner's hand (mobalytics); it can heal them (pcgamer).
# term: Stun: the unit can't attack or block (mobalytics).
# term: Ephemeral: dies after dealing combat damage or when the round ends (pcgamer).
# term: Enlightened: having 10 mana gems (pcgamer).
# term: Overwhelm: excess damage past a blocker goes to the enemy Nexus (pcgamer).
# term: Fearsome: can only be blocked by enemies with 3 or more Power (pcgamer).
# term: Tough: takes 1 less damage (pcgamer).
# term: Fleeting: discarded at the end of the round (pcgamer).
# term: Piltover & Zaun: unused mana refills up to 3 spell mana (mobalytics).

# Blood Bowl (Third Season): what the sources say

The library measures no factions for Blood Bowl (teams have 3 to 10 entries each and are not in faction_signature), and its
skills are keyword entries, not cards, so neither faction nor combo cases apply. The earlier two cases (from
https://www.gaming.net/blood-bowl-3-all-teams-ranked/) named teams the library does not measure and are dropped.

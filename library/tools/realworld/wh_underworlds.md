# Warhammer Underworlds: what the sources say

Sources:
[1] https://spentglory.com/2025/08/11/master-list-of-warbands-for-second-edition/ (40 warbands: playstyle and key mechanics;
    the page breaks off at The Dread Pageant)

Not factions: Universal, and the grand alliances Chaos, Death, Destruction, Order. Library warbands not in [1] get no cases.
Teleports are movement; pushes are forced_movement; resurrection is revive; token control and occupancy are objectives;
counterpunch and defensive play are fortress only where [1] says defensive.

## Warbands [1]
- Sons of Velmorn: counterpunch, midboard melee, token control; defensive profiles, flanking, resurrection
- Thorns of the Briar Queen: board control, delving, swarm aggression; teleports
- The Exiled Dead: token control, swarm aggression
- Mollog's Mob: big-model aggression; resurrection, leader regeneration
- Zarbag's Gitz: swarm aggression, delving, board control
- Hrothgorn's Mantrappers: defensive counterpunch; trap mechanic
- Rippa's Snarlfangs: invading aggression; multiple attacks
- Skittershank's Clawpack: invading aggression, assassination
- Gnarlspirit Pack: midboard melee, fast aggression, token play
- The Wurmspat: midboard melee, denial; durability, control tools
- Xandire's Truthseekers: invading aggression; benefits from fighter deaths
- The Starblood Stalkers: defensive token control; defensive rerolls
- The Shadeborn: fast aggression, assassination; teleports, built-in ping
- Elathain's Soulraid: counterpunch, token occupancy; push, resurrection
- Kainan's Reapers: swarm aggression, delving and board control
- The Grymwatch: delving, swarm aggression; resurrection
- The Crimson Court: midboard melee, invading aggression; fast, accurate fighters
- Ylthari's Guardians: fast aggression, counterpunch; teleport, built-in card draw
- Hexbane's Hunters: swarm aggression, chip damage, board control; ranged options
- Myari's Purifiers: flexible; rerolls, toolbox
- Thundrik's Profiteers: defensive board control; ranged attacks, token holding
- Morgok's Krushas: midboard melee, full aggression; counterspell
- Blackpowder's Buccaneers: big-model aggression; two-damage ranged option
- Grinkrak's Looncourt: swarm aggression, delving
- Da Kunnin' Krew: counterpunch, midboard melee; built-in push, range-2+ attacks
- Khagra's Ravagers: board control, midboard melee; token control

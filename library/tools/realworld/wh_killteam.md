# Warhammer 40,000: Kill Team: what the sources say

Sources:
[1] https://warhammerguild.com/guides/all-kill-team-warbands/ (2026: 30 current teams, playstyle and key mechanics)
[2] https://wahapedia.ru/kill-team3/kill-teams/exaction-squad/ (faction rules)
[3] https://wahapedia.ru/kill-team3/kill-teams/corsair-voidscarred/ (faction rules)

Not factions: the two rule books (2018, 2024), Non-player Operatives. Library teams without a source: Astra Militarum,
Asuryani, Chaos Daemons, Death Guard, Deathwatch, Drukhari, Genestealer Cults, Space Marines, T'au Empire, Tyranids (old
compendium factions), Chaos Cult, Farstalker Kinband, Imperial Navy Breacher, Inquisitorial Agents, Sanctifier, Veteran
Guardsmen, Void-Dancer Troupe. Psychic powers have no concept.

## Kasrkin
Ranged; order system, team-wide buffs, specialist weapons; weak in melee [1].
## Phobos Strike Team
Stealth, mid-board control; concealed deployment, deep strike denial, cover-ignoring carbines [1].
## Scout Squad
Flexible ranged; concealed positioning, sniper rifles [1].
## Novitiate
Horde; Faith points for healing and actions, high model count [1].
## Exaction Squad
Control, target priority; warrants on enemy operatives, objective control [1]; a chosen mark gives attackers Punishing [2].
## Hunter Clade
Adaptive; Doctrina imperatives each turning point [1].
## Nemesis Claw
Melee, stealth; Terror lowers enemy APL [1].
## Legionary
Flexible all-rounder; Mark-based builds [1].
## Blooded
Horde, melee; Blooded tokens earned through violent actions [1].
## Fellgor Ravager
Melee, objective play; despoil objectives, charges [1].
## Gellerpox Infected
Horde, melee, high variance [1].
## Warp Coven
Psychic, objective play [1].
## Pathfinder
Ranged; markerlights, drones [1].
## Mandrake
Stealth, movement, melee; shadow-stepping, teleporting strikes [1].
## Kommando
Stealth, melee; concealment, ambush, explosives [1].
## Corsair Voidscarred
Movement, versatile; speed, Fate Dice [1]; a free Dash each activation [3].
## Hernkyn Yaegir
Shooting; scanner targeting, durable [1].
## Wyrmblade
Stealth, assassination; ambush deployment [1].
## Hierotek Circle
Attrition; Reanimation, Cryptek buffs and debuffs [1].
## Hearthkyn Salvager
Shooting, objective play; Judgment tokens for focus fire [1].
## Brood Brother
Horde, shooting; high model count [1].
## Hand of the Archon, Elucidian Starstriders
Specialist roles and named operatives only [1]; no case.

# definitions
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
copy | Copy cards or units | A copy of a card, unit or effect is made for the owner. | Not: Doing a trigger again is repeat_trigger; a copy given to the opponent is enemy_benefit.
damage_over_time | Damage over time (poison, burn) | Damage repeats over turns: poison, burn, bleed. | Not: One-time damage is damage.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Not: Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
delayed | Delayed / countdown | The effect happens later: next turn, after N turns, countdowns. | Not: A set turn number is turn_number.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
modal_choice | Choose one of several effects | The card offers several effects and the owner picks one or more. | Not: Switching a unit between lasting forms is stances.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
selection | Look at cards and pick (scry) | The owner looks at several cards and chooses which to keep, take or put back. | Not: Searching the deck for a stated kind of card is tutor.
stat_multiply | Double or multiply stats | A stat or a total is doubled, tripled or multiplied. | Not: Adding a fixed amount is stat_gain.
temporary_effect | This turn / round only | It lasts only this turn or round: a bonus, a penalty or a lock. | Not: Lasting changes are the effect itself.
tutor | Search the deck for a card (tutor) | The owner searches the deck for a card of a stated kind and takes it. | Not: Drawing the top card is draw.

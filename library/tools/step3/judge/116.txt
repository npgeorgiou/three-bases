# definitions
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
once_per_game | Once per game | The effect can be used only once per game. | Not: Waiting turns between uses is cooldowns.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
piercing | Ignore armour or defence | The unit's damage or attacks ignore or lower the target's armour, saves or defence. | Not: Damage that carries on to another target is excess_damage.
placement_rules | Where units can be placed | Rules for where units can enter. | Not: Moving after placement is movement.

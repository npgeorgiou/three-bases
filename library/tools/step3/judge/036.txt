# definitions
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.

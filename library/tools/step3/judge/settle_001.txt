# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aura | Aura (ongoing effect on others) | While the source is in play, other units are continuously changed: anthem, lord. | Not: A one-time change is the effect itself.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
cant_attack | Can't attack (my own unit) | The unit itself can't attack: defender, wall. | Not: A lock put on an enemy is attack_lock.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | Not: A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
damage_over_time | Damage over time (poison, burn) | Damage repeats over turns: poison, burn, bleed. | Not: One-time damage is damage.
death_count | Counts deaths | The effect counts or scales with how many units died. | Not: A single death trigger is death_trigger.
exhaust | One use (exhausted for the battle) | A card is removed for the rest of the battle after use, or other cards of mine are. | Not: Removing a card from the game for good is banish.
free_play | Put into play for free | A card enters play without its cost being paid, by an effect or automatically. | Not: A dead unit coming back is revive.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
recycle | Reuse my cards (shuffle back) | The owner's used or discarded cards go back into deck or hand to be used again. | Not: An enemy unit sent to its deck is bounce_enemy.
return_to_hand | Return my unit to hand | The owner returns their own unit to hand, to replay it or save it. | Not: Sending an enemy unit back is bounce_enemy.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Not: Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
stat_loss | Stat loss (-power, -health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes down, or is set or swapped to a lower value. | Not: Not being able to attack is attack_lock or cant_attack.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
transform | Transform my card into another | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Not: Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Not: Counting as another type is type_grant.
upgrade | Upgrade / level up a card | A card is upgraded, levelled up or enhanced into a better version of itself. | Not: A plain stat rise on the board is stat_gain.
variable_stats | Stats equal to a count | A unit's stats are computed from the game state: power equal to the number of something. | Not: A fixed stat rise is stat_gain.

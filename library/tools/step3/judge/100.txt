# definitions
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
block_armor | Block points that wear off | Points that absorb damage and run out or wear off, usually at the end of the turn. | Not: A lasting reduction is damage_reduction.
blocking | Blocking / interception rules | Rules for who can block or intercept an attacker. | Not: A unit that can't block is cant_block.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
turn_start | Start of turn / round | Effects at the start of a turn or round. | Not: End of turn is turn_end.
tutor | Search the deck for a card (tutor) | The owner searches the deck for a card of a stated kind and takes it. | Not: Drawing the top card is draw.
upgrade | Upgrade / level up a card | A card is upgraded, levelled up or enhanced into a better version of itself. | Not: A plain stat rise on the board is stat_gain.

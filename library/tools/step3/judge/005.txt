# definitions
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
damage_over_time | Damage over time (poison, burn) | Damage repeats over turns: poison, burn, bleed. | Not: One-time damage is damage.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Not: Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
damage_types | Damage types, resistances and weaknesses | Damage has a type (fire, poison...) and units resist or are weak to types. | Not: Damage over turns is damage_over_time.

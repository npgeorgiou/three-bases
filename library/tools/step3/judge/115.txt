# definitions
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
hand_disruption | Attack the opponent's hand | Cards in the opponent's hand are discarded, taken, weakened, made dearer or looked at. | Not: Discarding my own cards is discard.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
merge_attach | Attach or merge onto a unit | A card attaches to or merges with a unit: equipment, enchant creature, a leader attached to a unit, magnetic. | Not: What the attached card gives is buff or debuff.
modal_choice | Choose one of several effects | The card offers several effects and the owner picks one or more. | Not: Switching a unit between lasting forms is stances.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.

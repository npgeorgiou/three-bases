# definitions
evasion | Hard to block (flying, unblockable) | The unit can't be blocked, can be blocked only by some units, or hits the player past blockers. | Not: Can't be targeted is immunity; hidden until it acts is stealth.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
haste | Act the turn it arrives (haste, rush) | A unit can attack or act on the turn it is played. | Not: An effect that makes it attack at once is attack_now.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
on_ally_play | When I play another card | Triggers when the owner plays or summons another card or unit. | Not: When this card is played is on_play.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
on_spell | When a spell is played | Triggers when a spell or action is played. | Not: When a unit is played is on_ally_play.

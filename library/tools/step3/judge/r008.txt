# definitions
copy | Copy cards or units | A copy of a card, unit or effect is made for the owner. | Not: Doing a trigger again is repeat_trigger; a copy given to the opponent is enemy_benefit.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | Not: A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.
counter_spell | Counter / cancel a play | An opponent's card or effect is cancelled before it resolves. | Not: Removing a unit after it is in play is removal.
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.

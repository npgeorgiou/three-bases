# definitions
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Not: Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.
stat_loss | Stat loss (-power, -health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes down, or is set or swapped to a lower value. | Not: Not being able to attack is attack_lock or cant_attack.
stealth | Stealth / hidden until it acts | The unit can't be seen, targeted or attacked until it acts or a condition ends. | Not: Untargetable for good is immunity.

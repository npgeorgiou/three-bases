# definitions
damage_over_time | Damage over time (poison, burn) | Damage repeats over turns: poison, burn, bleed. | Not: One-time damage is damage.
death_trigger | When it dies (deathrattle) | Effects when a unit dies or is destroyed. | Not: Counting deaths is death_count.
deck_position | Top or bottom of the deck matters | The top or bottom card of a deck matters: looked at, revealed or changed. | Not: Searching the whole deck is tutor.
delayed | Delayed / countdown | The effect happens later: next turn, after N turns, countdowns. | Not: A set turn number is turn_number.
destroy | Destroy outright | A unit is destroyed or killed regardless of its health. | Not: Killing by damage is damage; destroying my own unit for value is sacrifice.
direct_damage | Damage to the player or base | Damage goes straight to the enemy player, hero or base. | Not: Damage to units is damage.
disable | Can't act at all (stun, freeze) | The unit can't act at all: stun, freeze, sleep, skip its turn. | Not: Losing only its attack is attack_lock.
discard | Discard my own cards | The owner discards cards from their own hand, as a cost or to fuel effects. | Not: Making the opponent discard is hand_disruption.

# definitions
discard | Discard my own cards | The owner discards cards from their own hand, as a cost or to fuel effects. | Not: Making the opponent discard is hand_disruption.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
hand_disruption | Attack the opponent's hand | Cards in the opponent's hand are discarded, taken, weakened, made dearer or looked at. | Not: Discarding my own cards is discard.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.

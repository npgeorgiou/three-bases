# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
adjacency | Neighbours matter (adjacency) | The effect cares about units beside it, or the lane or location to the left or right. | Not: Several lanes away is cross_lane.
banish | Banish / exile (gone for good) | A unit or card is removed from the game for good, beyond the discard pile. | Not: Leaving and coming back is blink.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
cant_attack | Can't attack (my own unit) | The unit itself can't attack: defender, wall. | Not: A lock put on an enemy is attack_lock.
coin_economy | Second currency for abilities | A second currency (coins, crowns) is spent on abilities. | Not: Shop gold between fights is shop_gold.
condition | Conditions | An effect works only if, unless or when something is true. | Not: When it happens is a trigger.

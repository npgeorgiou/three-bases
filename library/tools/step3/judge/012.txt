# definitions
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
terrain | Terrain / board features | Terrain and features on the board. | Not: A lane's rule is lane_rule.
transform | Transform my card into another | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Not: Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Not: Counting as another type is type_grant.
upgrade | Upgrade / level up a card | A card is upgraded, levelled up or enhanced into a better version of itself. | Not: A plain stat rise on the board is stat_gain.

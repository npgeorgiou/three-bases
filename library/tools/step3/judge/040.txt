# definitions
ping | Small repeatable damage | A small amount of damage (1 or 2) that can be dealt again and again. | Not: One big hit is damage.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
recycle | Reuse my cards (shuffle back) | The owner's used or discarded cards go back into deck or hand to be used again. | Not: An enemy unit sent to its deck is bounce_enemy.

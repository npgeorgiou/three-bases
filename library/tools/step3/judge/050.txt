# definitions
modal_choice | Choose one of several effects | The card offers several effects and the owner picks one or more. | Not: Switching a unit between lasting forms is stances.
on_ally_play | When I play another card | Triggers when the owner plays or summons another card or unit. | Not: When this card is played is on_play.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
on_play | When played (battlecry, on reveal) | Triggers when the card is played or revealed: battlecry, on reveal, enters. | Not: When another card is played is on_ally_play.
on_spell | When a spell is played | Triggers when a spell or action is played. | Not: When a unit is played is on_ally_play.
once_per_game | Once per game | The effect can be used only once per game. | Not: Waiting turns between uses is cooldowns.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.

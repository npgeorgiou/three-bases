# definitions
disable | Can't act at all (stun, freeze) | The unit can't act at all: stun, freeze, sleep, skip its turn. | Not: Losing only its attack is attack_lock.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
exhaust | One use (exhausted for the battle) | A card is removed for the rest of the battle after use, or other cards of mine are. | Not: Removing a card from the game for good is banish.
extra_turn | Extra turns or actions | The owner takes an extra turn, round or action. | Not: Readying a used unit is untap.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
free_play | Put into play for free | A card enters play without its cost being paid, by an effect or automatically. | Not: A dead unit coming back is revive.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.

# definitions
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
reactions | Reactions on the opponent's turn | The effect answers the opponent's actions: interrupts, after your opponent plays. | Not: Hidden traps are trap_secret.
saving_throws | Defender rolls to resist (saves) | The target rolls to resist an effect or damage. | Not: Armour that always reduces damage is damage_reduction.

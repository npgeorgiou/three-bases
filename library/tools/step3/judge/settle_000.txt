# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Not: Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
delayed | Delayed / countdown | The effect happens later: next turn, after N turns, countdowns. | Not: A set turn number is turn_number.
destroy | Destroy outright | A unit is destroyed or killed regardless of its health. | Not: Killing by damage is damage; destroying my own unit for value is sacrifice.
direct_damage | Damage to the player or base | Damage goes straight to the enemy player, hero or base. | Not: Damage to units is damage.
discard | Discard my own cards | The owner discards cards from their own hand, as a cost or to fuel effects. | Not: Making the opponent discard is hand_disruption.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
permanent_growth | Grows again and again | A unit gains stats repeatedly (each turn, each trigger) and keeps them. | Not: A single lasting bonus is stat_gain.
ping | Small repeatable damage | A small amount of damage (1 or 2) that can be dealt again and again. | Not: One big hit is damage.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
scope_global | Whole board / all players | The effect touches the whole board or all players. | Not: Several but not all is scope_multi.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Not: Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Not: Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.
stat_loss | Stat loss (-power, -health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes down, or is set or swapped to a lower value. | Not: Not being able to attack is attack_lock or cant_attack.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
symmetric_effect | Both players alike | The effect touches both players the same way: each player draws. | Not: Only my side is the effect itself.
temporary_effect | This turn / round only | It lasts only this turn or round: a bonus, a penalty or a lock. | Not: Lasting changes are the effect itself.

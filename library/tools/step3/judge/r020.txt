# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
banish | Banish / exile (gone for good) | A unit or card is removed from the game for good, beyond the discard pile. | Not: Leaving and coming back is blink.
blocking | Blocking / interception rules | Rules for who can block or intercept an attacker. | Not: A unit that can't block is cant_block.
board_wipe | Board wipe | Most or all units on the board, or in a lane, are destroyed or removed. | Not: Damage to several that may leave survivors is aoe_damage.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
coin_economy | Second currency for abilities | A second currency (coins, crowns) is spent on abilities. | Not: Shop gold between fights is shop_gold.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
copy | Copy cards or units | A copy of a card, unit or effect is made for the owner. | Not: Doing a trigger again is repeat_trigger; a copy given to the opponent is enemy_benefit.
cost_condition | Cost matters | The effect cares about a card's cost: 1-cost cards, odd or even cost, N or more. | Not: Changing costs is cost_reduction or cost_set.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | Not: A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.
variable_stats | Stats equal to a count | A unit's stats are computed from the game state: power equal to the number of something. | Not: A fixed stat rise is stat_gain.

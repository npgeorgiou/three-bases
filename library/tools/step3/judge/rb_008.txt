# definitions
copy | Copy cards or units | A copy of a card, unit or effect is made for the owner. | Not: Doing a trigger again is repeat_trigger; a copy given to the opponent is enemy_benefit.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Not: Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
modal_choice | Choose one of several effects | The card offers several effects and the owner picks one or more. | Not: Switching a unit between lasting forms is stances.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
scope_global | Whole board / all players | The effect touches the whole board or all players. | Not: Several but not all is scope_multi.
selection | Look at cards and pick (scry) | The owner looks at several cards and chooses which to keep, take or put back. | Not: Searching the deck for a stated kind of card is tutor.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Not: Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
symmetric_effect | Both players alike | The effect touches both players the same way: each player draws. | Not: Only my side is the effect itself.
temporary_effect | This turn / round only | It lasts only this turn or round: a bonus, a penalty or a lock. | Not: Lasting changes are the effect itself.
transform | Transform my card into another | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Not: Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Not: Counting as another type is type_grant.

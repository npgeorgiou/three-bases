# definitions
reserve_bench | Bench / reserve | Units wait on a bench or in reserve. | Not: Cards in hand are card_flow.
resource_gain | Gain resources (ramp) | The owner gains extra mana, energy, supply or another resource, now or for good. | Not: Cards costing less is cost_reduction.
sacrifice | Sacrifice my own units | The owner destroys or gives up their own unit, as a cost or for value. | Not: An enemy being made to sacrifice is destroy.
saving_throws | Defender rolls to resist (saves) | The target rolls to resist an effect or damage. | Not: Armour that always reduces damage is damage_reduction.
scope_global | Whole board / all players | The effect touches the whole board or all players. | Not: Several but not all is scope_multi.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Not: Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
spell_speed | Speed / reaction timing | Spells and actions have speeds: instant, fast, slow, responses. | Not: Reacting to the opponent is reactions.
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Not: Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
symmetric_effect | Both players alike | The effect touches both players the same way: each player draws. | Not: Only my side is the effect itself.
temporary_effect | This turn / round only | It lasts only this turn or round: a bonus, a penalty or a lock. | Not: Lasting changes are the effect itself.
terrain | Terrain / board features | Terrain and features on the board. | Not: A lane's rule is lane_rule.

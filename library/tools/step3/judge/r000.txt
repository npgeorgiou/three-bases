# definitions
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
selection | Look at cards and pick (scry) | The owner looks at several cards and chooses which to keep, take or put back. | Not: Searching the deck for a stated kind of card is tutor.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Not: Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
spell_speed | Speed / reaction timing | Spells and actions have speeds: instant, fast, slow, responses. | Not: Reacting to the opponent is reactions.
stat_multiply | Double or multiply stats | A stat or a total is doubled, tripled or multiplied. | Not: Adding a fixed amount is stat_gain.
stealth | Stealth / hidden until it acts | The unit can't be seen, targeted or attacked until it acts or a condition ends. | Not: Untargetable for good is immunity.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
temporary_effect | This turn / round only | It lasts only this turn or round: a bonus, a penalty or a lock. | Not: Lasting changes are the effect itself.
terrain | Terrain / board features | Terrain and features on the board. | Not: A lane's rule is lane_rule.
transform | Transform my card into another | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Not: Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.

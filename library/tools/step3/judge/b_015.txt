# definitions
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
banish | Banish / exile (gone for good) | A unit or card is removed from the game for good, beyond the discard pile. | Not: Leaving and coming back is blink.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
death_trigger | When it dies (deathrattle) | Effects when a unit dies or is destroyed. | Not: Counting deaths is death_count.
deck_position | Top or bottom of the deck matters | The top or bottom card of a deck matters: looked at, revealed or changed. | Not: Searching the whole deck is tutor.
direct_damage | Damage to the player or base | Damage goes straight to the enemy player, hero or base. | Not: Damage to units is damage.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
type_grant | Counts as another type | A card counts as a type, tribe or faction it is not, or as every type. | Not: Caring about a type is tribal.

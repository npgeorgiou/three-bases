# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
copy | Copy cards or units | A copy of a card, unit or effect is made for the owner. | Not: Doing a trigger again is repeat_trigger; a copy given to the opponent is enemy_benefit.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | Not: A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Not: Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
shield | Absorb one hit (shield, ward) | The next hit or damage instance is fully absorbed or cancelled. | Not: Damage lowered by an amount is damage_reduction.
spell_speed | Speed / reaction timing | Spells and actions have speeds: instant, fast, slow, responses. | Not: Reacting to the opponent is reactions.
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Not: Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.
temporary_buff | Temporary bonus (this turn only) | A unit's bonus lasts only this turn, round or fight. | Not: A bonus that stays is stat_gain or permanent_growth.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Not: Counting as another type is type_grant.

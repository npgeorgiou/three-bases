# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
cant_attack | Can't attack (my own unit) | The unit itself can't attack: defender, wall. | Not: A lock put on an enemy is attack_lock.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
deck_position | Top or bottom of the deck matters | The top or bottom card of a deck matters: looked at, revealed or changed. | Not: Searching the whole deck is tutor.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
on_play | When played (battlecry, on reveal) | Triggers when the card is played or revealed: battlecry, on reveal, enters. | Not: When another card is played is on_ally_play.
ping | Small repeatable damage | A small amount of damage (1 or 2) that can be dealt again and again. | Not: One big hit is damage.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
transform | Transform my card into another | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Not: Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Not: Counting as another type is type_grant.

# definitions
evasion | Hard to block (flying, unblockable) | The unit can't be blocked, can be blocked only by some units, or hits the player past blockers. | Not: Can't be targeted is immunity; hidden until it acts is stealth.
excess_damage | Excess damage carries on (trample) | Damage beyond what kills the target goes on to the player or another unit. | Not: Ignoring armour is piercing.
exhaust | One use (exhausted for the battle) | A card is removed for the rest of the battle after use, or other cards of mine are. | Not: Removing a card from the game for good is banish.
extra_turn | Extra turns or actions | The owner takes an extra turn, round or action. | Not: Readying a used unit is untap.
first_strike | First strike (hits before the enemy) | The unit deals its combat damage before the enemy can strike back. | Not: Attacking the turn it arrives is haste.

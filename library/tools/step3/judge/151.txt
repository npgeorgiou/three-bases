# definitions
reactions | Reactions on the opponent's turn | The effect answers the opponent's actions: interrupts, after your opponent plays. | Not: Hidden traps are trap_secret.
return_to_hand | Return my unit to hand | The owner returns their own unit to hand, to replay it or save it. | Not: Sending an enemy unit back is bounce_enemy.
scope_global | Whole board / all players | The effect touches the whole board or all players. | Not: Several but not all is scope_multi.
scoring_objectives | Objectives that score points | Objective or secret-goal cards score points when their condition is met. | Not: Rewards other than points are quests.
selection | Look at cards and pick (scry) | The owner looks at several cards and chooses which to keep, take or put back. | Not: Searching the deck for a stated kind of card is tutor.
stacking_keyword | Stacking keywords / counters | A keyword or counter stacks and grows with each copy. | Not: A one-time count is condition_count.
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Not: Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.

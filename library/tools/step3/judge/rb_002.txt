# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
aura | Aura (ongoing effect on others) | While the source is in play, other units are continuously changed: anthem, lord. | Not: A one-time change is the effect itself.
blocking | Blocking / interception rules | Rules for who can block or intercept an attacker. | Not: A unit that can't block is cant_block.
destroy | Destroy outright | A unit is destroyed or killed regardless of its health. | Not: Killing by damage is damage; destroying my own unit for value is sacrifice.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
transform | Transform my card into another | The owner's own card or unit turns into a different one: evolve, flip, become. | Not: Turning an enemy into something weaker is polymorph.
upgrade | Upgrade / level up a card | A card is upgraded, levelled up or enhanced into a better version of itself. | Not: A plain stat rise on the board is stat_gain.

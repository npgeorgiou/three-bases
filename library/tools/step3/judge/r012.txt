# definitions
extra_turn | Extra turns or actions | The owner takes an extra turn, round or action. | Not: Readying a used unit is untap.
first_strike | First strike (hits before the enemy) | The unit deals its combat damage before the enemy can strike back. | Not: Attacking the turn it arrives is haste.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
free_play | Put into play for free | A card enters play without its cost being paid, by an effect or automatically. | Not: A dead unit coming back is revive.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
haste | Act the turn it arrives (haste, rush) | A unit can attack or act on the turn it is played. | Not: An effect that makes it attack at once is attack_now.
immunity | Immune / can't be targeted | A unit can't be targeted, or is immune to a damage type, effect or condition. | Not: Hard to block is evasion; hidden until it acts is stealth.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.

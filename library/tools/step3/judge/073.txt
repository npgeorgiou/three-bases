# definitions
resource_gain | Gain resources (ramp) | The owner gains extra mana, energy, supply or another resource, now or for good. | Not: Cards costing less is cost_reduction.
return_to_hand | Return my unit to hand | The owner returns their own unit to hand, to replay it or save it. | Not: Sending an enemy unit back is bounce_enemy.
revive | Bring back dead units (revive) | A unit that died or was destroyed earlier comes back to play or to hand. | Not: Surviving at the moment of death is cheat_death.
sacrifice | Sacrifice my own units | The owner destroys or gives up their own unit, as a cost or for value. | Not: An enemy being made to sacrifice is destroy.

# definitions
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.
kicker | Optional extra cost for a bonus | Paying extra is optional and adds a bonus effect: kicker, enhance. | Not: A required alternative cost is cost_type.
multiple_resources | Several resource types | The game uses several resource types. | Not: One resource gained is resource_gain.
on_ally_play | When I play another card | Triggers when the owner plays or summons another card or unit. | Not: When this card is played is on_play.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
on_play | When played (battlecry, on reveal) | Triggers when the card is played or revealed: battlecry, on reveal, enters. | Not: When another card is played is on_ally_play.

# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
banish | Banish / exile (gone for good) | A unit or card is removed from the game for good, beyond the discard pile. | Not: Leaving and coming back is blink.
board_wipe | Board wipe | Most or all units on the board, or in a lane, are destroyed or removed. | Not: Damage to several that may leave survivors is aoe_damage.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.

# definitions
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
deathtouch | Any damage kills (deathtouch, poisonous) | Any damage this unit deals destroys the unit it hits. | Not: Destroying by an effect without combat damage is destroy.
destroy | Destroy outright | A unit is destroyed or killed regardless of its health. | Not: Killing by damage is damage; destroying my own unit for value is sacrifice.
direct_damage | Damage to the player or base | Damage goes straight to the enemy player, hero or base. | Not: Damage to units is damage.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
evasion | Hard to block (flying, unblockable) | The unit can't be blocked, can be blocked only by some units, or hits the player past blockers. | Not: Can't be targeted is immunity; hidden until it acts is stealth.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
haste | Act the turn it arrives (haste, rush) | A unit can attack or act on the turn it is played. | Not: An effect that makes it attack at once is attack_now.

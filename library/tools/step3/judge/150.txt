# definitions
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
ping | Small repeatable damage | A small amount of damage (1 or 2) that can be dealt again and again. | Not: One big hit is damage.
points_scoring | Points decide the winner | Points or score decide the winner. | Not: Destroying the enemy base is life_track.
quests | Quests / objectives with rewards | A goal with progress gives a reward when it is completed. | Not: A goal that scores points is scoring_objectives.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
reactions | Reactions on the opponent's turn | The effect answers the opponent's actions: interrupts, after your opponent plays. | Not: Hidden traps are trap_secret.

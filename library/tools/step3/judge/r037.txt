# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
aura | Aura (ongoing effect on others) | While the source is in play, other units are continuously changed: anthem, lord. | Not: A one-time change is the effect itself.
stat_loss | Stat loss (-power, -health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes down, or is set or swapped to a lower value. | Not: Not being able to attack is attack_lock or cant_attack.
stat_multiply | Double or multiply stats | A stat or a total is doubled, tripled or multiplied. | Not: Adding a fixed amount is stat_gain.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
temporary_effect | This turn / round only | It lasts only this turn or round: a bonus, a penalty or a lock. | Not: Lasting changes are the effect itself.
terrain | Terrain / board features | Terrain and features on the board. | Not: A lane's rule is lane_rule.
tribal | Tribe / type matters | The effect cares about a tribe, type, team or family of cards. | Not: Counting as another type is type_grant.
turn_end | End of turn / round | Effects at the end of a turn or round. | Not: Start of turn is turn_start.
turn_start | Start of turn / round | Effects at the start of a turn or round. | Not: End of turn is turn_end.
variable_stats | Stats equal to a count | A unit's stats are computed from the game state: power equal to the number of something. | Not: A fixed stat rise is stat_gain.

# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
adjacency | Neighbours matter (adjacency) | The effect cares about units beside it, or the lane or location to the left or right. | Not: Several lanes away is cross_lane.
aoe_damage | Damage to many at once | Damage hits several units, a row, a lane or an area at once. | Not: One unit's attack hitting several is multi_target_attack.
aura | Aura (ongoing effect on others) | While the source is in play, other units are continuously changed: anthem, lord. | Not: A one-time change is the effect itself.
board_wipe | Board wipe | Most or all units on the board, or in a lane, are destroyed or removed. | Not: Damage to several that may leave survivors is aoe_damage.
bounce_enemy | Send an enemy unit back (bounce) | An enemy unit is returned to its owner's hand or deck. | Not: Returning my own unit is return_to_hand.
cant_attack | Can't attack (my own unit) | The unit itself can't attack: defender, wall. | Not: A lock put on an enemy is attack_lock.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
sacrifice | Sacrifice my own units | The owner destroys or gives up their own unit, as a cost or for value. | Not: An enemy being made to sacrifice is destroy.
scope_global | Whole board / all players | The effect touches the whole board or all players. | Not: Several but not all is scope_multi.
turn_end | End of turn / round | Effects at the end of a turn or round. | Not: Start of turn is turn_start.
turn_start | Start of turn / round | Effects at the start of a turn or round. | Not: End of turn is turn_end.
tutor | Search the deck for a card (tutor) | The owner searches the deck for a card of a stated kind and takes it. | Not: Drawing the top card is draw.
variable_stats | Stats equal to a count | A unit's stats are computed from the game state: power equal to the number of something. | Not: A fixed stat rise is stat_gain.

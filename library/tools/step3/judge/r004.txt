# definitions
cost_condition | Cost matters | The effect cares about a card's cost: 1-cost cards, odd or even cost, N or more. | Not: Changing costs is cost_reduction or cost_set.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | Not: A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.
on_ally_play | When I play another card | Triggers when the owner plays or summons another card or unit. | Not: When this card is played is on_play.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
on_spell | When a spell is played | Triggers when a spell or action is played. | Not: When a unit is played is on_ally_play.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
ranged | Ranged attack / long reach | The unit attacks from a distance, from the back row or over other units. | Not: Damage from a spell is damage.
recycle | Reuse my cards (shuffle back) | The owner's used or discarded cards go back into deck or hand to be used again. | Not: An enemy unit sent to its deck is bounce_enemy.

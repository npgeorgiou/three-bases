# definitions
damage | Deal damage | Damage is dealt to a unit, player or base. | Not: Killing regardless of health is destroy; damage to my own side is friendly_fire.
damage_over_time | Damage over time (poison, burn) | Damage repeats over turns: poison, burn, bleed. | Not: One-time damage is damage.
damage_reduction | Take less damage (armour) | Damage a unit or player takes is reduced by an amount or a share. | Not: Absorbing one whole hit is shield; points that wear off are block_armor.
damage_types | Damage types, resistances and weaknesses | Damage has a type (fire, poison...) and units resist or are weak to types. | Not: Damage over turns is damage_over_time.
death_trigger | When it dies (deathrattle) | Effects when a unit dies or is destroyed. | Not: Counting deaths is death_count.
disable | Can't act at all (stun, freeze) | The unit can't act at all: stun, freeze, sleep, skip its turn. | Not: Losing only its attack is attack_lock.
draw | Draw cards | Cards are drawn from the owner's deck into hand. | Not: Searching the deck for a chosen kind of card is tutor; making a new card is generate_cards.
evasion | Hard to block (flying, unblockable) | The unit can't be blocked, can be blocked only by some units, or hits the player past blockers. | Not: Can't be targeted is immunity; hidden until it acts is stealth.
excess_damage | Excess damage carries on (trample) | Damage beyond what kills the target goes on to the player or another unit. | Not: Ignoring armour is piercing.
forced_movement | Move enemy units (push, pull) | Enemy units are moved against their will: push, pull, knockback, sent to another lane. | Not: Moving my own units is movement.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
on_attack | When it attacks | Triggers when the unit attacks or strikes. | Not: When it is attacked is on_damaged or counterattack.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
piercing | Ignore armour or defence | The unit's damage or attacks ignore or lower the target's armour, saves or defence. | Not: Damage that carries on to another target is excess_damage.
placement_rules | Where units can be placed | Rules for where units can enter. | Not: Moving after placement is movement.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.

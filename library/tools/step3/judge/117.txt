placement_rules | Where units can be placed | Rules for where units can enter. | Not: Moving after placement is movement.
quests | Quests / objectives with rewards | A goal with progress gives a reward when it is completed. | Not: A goal that scores points is scoring_objectives.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.

# definitions
on_spell | When a spell is played | Triggers when a spell or action is played. | Not: When a unit is played is on_ally_play.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.
resource_gain | Gain resources (ramp) | The owner gains extra mana, energy, supply or another resource, now or for good. | Not: Cards costing less is cost_reduction.
return_to_hand | Return my unit to hand | The owner returns their own unit to hand, to replay it or save it. | Not: Sending an enemy unit back is bounce_enemy.
stat_gain | Stat gain (+power, +health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes up, or is set or swapped to a higher value. | Not: Gaining only a keyword is that keyword's concept (evasion, first_strike...); a lasting rise each turn is permanent_growth.
stat_loss | Stat loss (-power, -health) | A unit's power, attack, health or other stat goes down, or is set or swapped to a lower value. | Not: Not being able to attack is attack_lock or cant_attack.
summon | Summon units / tokens | New units are put onto the owner's side of the board. | Not: Units put on the opponent's side are board_clog; units back from death are revive.
temporary_buff | Temporary bonus (this turn only) | A unit's bonus lasts only this turn, round or fight. | Not: A bonus that stays is stat_gain or permanent_growth.

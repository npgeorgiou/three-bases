# definitions
on_spell | When a spell is played | Triggers when a spell or action is played. | Not: When a unit is played is on_ally_play.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.
permanent_growth | Grows again and again | A unit gains stats repeatedly (each turn, each trigger) and keeps them. | Not: A single lasting bonus is stat_gain.
piercing | Ignore armour or defence | The unit's damage or attacks ignore or lower the target's armour, saves or defence. | Not: Damage that carries on to another target is excess_damage.
ping | Small repeatable damage | A small amount of damage (1 or 2) that can be dealt again and again. | Not: One big hit is damage.
polymorph | Turn an enemy into something weaker | An enemy unit is turned into a weaker or harmless card or unit. | Not: My own card turning into another is transform.
random_effect | Random results | The outcome is random: random target, random card, chance, dice. | Not: A random ability is random_ability.

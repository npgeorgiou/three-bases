# definitions
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.
life_payment | Pay life as a cost | The owner pays life or health to play or use something. | Not: Damage to my own units is friendly_fire.
lifesteal | Lifesteal / drain | Damage dealt also heals its source or its owner. | Not: Healing not tied to damage dealt is heal.
merge_attach | Attach or merge onto a unit | A card attaches to or merges with a unit: equipment, enchant creature, a leader attached to a unit, magnetic. | Not: What the attached card gives is buff or debuff.
mill | Mill the opponent's deck | Cards are put from the opponent's deck into their discard pile or removed. | Not: Doing it to my own deck is self_mill.

# definitions
generate_cards | Create new cards | New cards that were not in the deck are put into hand or deck. | Not: Units made on the board are summon.
hand_disruption | Attack the opponent's hand | Cards in the opponent's hand are discarded, taken, weakened, made dearer or looked at. | Not: Discarding my own cards is discard.
haste | Act the turn it arrives (haste, rush) | A unit can attack or act on the turn it is played. | Not: An effect that makes it attack at once is attack_now.
heal | Restore health (heal) | Lost health of a unit, hero or player is restored. | Not: Preventing damage before it happens is protection.
in_hand_buff | Improve cards in hand or deck | Cards still in hand or deck get more power, a lower cost or better text before they are played. | Not: A discount on whatever is played this turn is cost_reduction.
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.

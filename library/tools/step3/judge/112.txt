# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
advantage | Advantage / disadvantage (roll twice) | A roll is made twice and the better or worse result is kept. | Not: A flat bonus to the roll is buff or debuff.
aura | Aura (ongoing effect on others) | While the source is in play, other units are continuously changed: anthem, lord. | Not: A one-time change is the effect itself.
buff | Make a unit stronger (buff) | A unit gains stats or a combat ability it did not have: more power or health, first strike, flying, extra attacks, reach, piercing. | Not: Improving a card still in hand is in_hand_buff; a unit printed with high stats is not buffed.
cant_attack | Can't attack (my own unit) | The unit itself can't attack: defender, wall. | Not: A lock put on an enemy is attack_lock.
condition_count | Counts / thresholds | The effect needs or scales with a number: for each, N or more. | Not: Counts of past events this game are count_payoff.
cooldowns | Ability cooldowns | An ability must wait some turns before it can be used again. | Not: Once per game is once_per_game.
cost_reduction | Cards cost less | The owner's cards cost less by an amount. | Not: A play that costs nothing at all is free_play; a cost set to a number is cost_set.

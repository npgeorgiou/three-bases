# definitions
activated | Activated ability (pay to use) | The player chooses when to use an ability: activate, tap, pay to use. | Not: Abilities that fire on their own are triggers.
turn_start | Start of turn / round | Effects at the start of a turn or round. | Not: End of turn is turn_end.
tutor | Search the deck for a card (tutor) | The owner searches the deck for a card of a stated kind and takes it. | Not: Drawing the top card is draw.
type_grant | Counts as another type | A card counts as a type, tribe or faction it is not, or as every type. | Not: Caring about a type is tribal.
untap | Ready a used unit again (untap) | A used or tapped unit or card is readied so it can act again. | Not: A whole extra turn is extra_turn.
variable_stats | Stats equal to a count | A unit's stats are computed from the game state: power equal to the number of something. | Not: A fixed stat rise is stat_gain.

# definitions
information | Hidden and revealed information | Hidden and revealed information: face-down cards, looking at the opponent's hand. | Not: Hidden units are stealth.
items_consumables | Items & consumables | Items and consumables outside the deck. | Not: Cards in the deck are card_flow.
modal_choice | Choose one of several effects | The card offers several effects and the owner picks one or more. | Not: Switching a unit between lasting forms is stances.
movement | Move my units (reposition) | The owner moves their own unit to another space, row, lane or location. | Not: Moving enemy units is forced_movement.
once_per_game | Once per game | The effect can be used only once per game. | Not: Waiting turns between uses is cooldowns.
passive | Passive / always on | The effect is always on while the card is in play. | Not: Effects that fire on an event are triggers.

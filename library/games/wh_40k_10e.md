# Warhammer 40,000 (10th edition)

One file per game: the game, its factions, the source summary, the mechanics (patterns) and the real-world cases.

## The game
*Claude's summary (Oct 2026), from general knowledge of the game and the rules and terms under Mechanics; not from the sources below.*
- Genre: tabletop miniatures wargame (Games Workshop, 10th edition 2023); armies built to a points limit.
- Battle: 5 battle rounds; each player's turn has phases: Command (both gain Command points, battle-shock tests), Movement, Shooting, Charge, Fight.
- Attacks: roll to hit, to wound (Strength against Toughness), the defender saves (armour or invulnerable), then damage.
- Units: datasheets with Move, Toughness, Save, Wounds, Leadership and Objective Control (OC).
- Command points: spent on stratagems.
- Army rules: each faction has an army rule and detachments with their own rules and stratagems.
- Win: victory points from primary and secondary missions, mostly by holding objectives.

## Factions

### Imperium - Adeptus Astartes - Space Marines
*Claude:* Elite generalists; army rule Oath of Moment.
Known for: flexible, simple rules, many units [1]; Oath of Moment: pick one enemy, re-roll hits against it [2a].

### Imperium - Adeptus Astartes - Black Templars
*Claude:* Crusading Space Marines, focused on melee.
Known for: ultra-aggressive melee; four Templar Vows boost the army [1].

### Imperium - Adeptus Astartes - Blood Angels
*Claude:* Space Marine assault army: jump packs and the Death Company.
Known for: melee, jump pack assaults [1].

### Imperium - Adeptus Astartes - Space Wolves
*Claude:* Space Marines with wolves, characters and close combat.
Known for: melee; Sagas: completing them empowers the Wolves [1].

### Imperium - Grey Knights
*Claude:* Daemon-hunting psykers who teleport across the board.
Known for: a small elite force of psykers [1]; Gate of Infinity: units go into Strategic Reserves and come back [2q].

### Imperium - Adepta Sororitas
*Claude:* Battle Sisters: Acts of Faith with Miracle dice, flamers and melta.
Known for: Miracle dice stored and used later [1]; Acts of Faith: a Miracle die replaces a roll; dice gained each round and
when a friendly unit is destroyed [2n]; fast hard-hitting melee, fragile glass cannons [1].

### Imperium - Adeptus Custodes
*Claude:* A few very elite, very tough models.
Known for: few, tough, deadly melee units, top defensive wargear [1]; Martial Ka'tah: one of two fighting stances [2o].

### Imperium - Adeptus Mechanicus
*Claude:* Tech-priests and Skitarii: shooting with doctrinas.
Known for: reconfigurable, stackable buffs [1]; Doctrina Imperatives: choose Protector or Conqueror each round [2p].

### Chaos - Chaos Space Marines
*Claude:* Traitor marines: Dark Pacts for extra power.
Known for: Dark Pacts that grant power or backfire [1][2i]: a failed test costs mortal wounds.

### Chaos - Death Guard
*Claude:* Nurgle marines: very tough, contagion around them.
Known for: slow, hardy, a plague aura that weakens enemies [1]; Nurgle's Gift: enemies in range take a chosen Plague penalty [2j].

### Chaos - Thousand Sons
*Claude:* Tzeentch marines: sorcery and psychic shooting.
Known for: psychic and sorcerous [1]; Cabal of Sorcerers: rituals, mortal wounds on themselves if pushed [2k].

### Chaos - World Eaters
*Claude:* Khorne marines: pure melee with the Blessings of Khorne.
Known for: fast, ultra-violent melee [1]; Blessings of Khorne: dice each round activate army-wide combat bonuses [2l].

### Chaos - Emperor's Children
*Claude:* Slaanesh marines: speed and excess.
Known for: fast aggressive melee and flexible firepower [1]; Thrill Seekers: shoot and charge after Advancing or Falling Back [2m].

### Aeldari
*Claude:* Fast, fragile elves: Strands of Fate re-rolls and mobility.
Known for: force multipliers, lightly armoured [1]; Battle Focus: tokens spent for extra movement; Strands of Fate: a pool of
dice that replace rolls [2f].

### Xenos - Necrons
*Claude:* Robot legions: Reanimation Protocols bring models back.
Known for: slow but resilient, Reanimation Protocols [1]; reanimate wounds and destroyed models each Command phase [2d].

### Xenos - Orks
*Claude:* Hordes: the Waaagh! turn of extra speed and melee.
Known for: an overwhelming green tide, devastating in numbers [1]; Waaagh!: once per battle, one round of charge after
Advance, +1 Strength and Attacks, invulnerable save [2b].

### Xenos - T'au Empire
*Claude:* Shooting: For the Greater Good guided fire.
Known for: dynamic, manoeuvrable, mobile battlesuits, superior weapons [1]; For the Greater Good: Observers mark Spotted
enemies for better shooting [2e].

### Xenos - Tyranids
*Claude:* Swarm: Synapse and Shadow in the Warp.
Known for: huge swarms or a few big monsters, Synapse; weak away from Synapse [1]; Synapse range gives units bonuses; Shadow
in the Warp forces Battle-shock tests [2c].

### Xenos - Genestealer Cults
*Claude:* Insurgents: ambush and units that come back.
Known for: a relentless horde whose units return to play [1]; Cult Ambush: destroyed units come back from reserve [2g].

### Xenos - Leagues of Votann
*Claude:* Dwarves: judgement tokens and durable shooting.
Known for: tech-heavy flexible infantry and vehicles [1]; Prioritised Efficiency: bonuses near and on objectives [2h].

## Combos
- Judiciar + Bladeguard Veteran Squad: Bladeguard decent in melee with a Judiciar [3]
- Impulsor + Sternguard Veteran Squad / Hellblaster Squad: Firing Deck lets them shoot from it [3]
- Storm Speeder Thunderstrike + Eradicator Squad: +1 to wound against vehicles and monsters it hit [3]
- Incursor Squad + Whirlwind: +1 to hit helps indirect fire [3]
- Drop Pod + Devastator Squad: delivers them into shooting position [3]
- Captain + Company Heroes: they can't deploy without a Captain [3]
- Ghazghkull Thraka + Meganobz: Ghaz can lead Meganobz [4]

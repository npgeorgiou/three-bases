# Legends of Runeterra

One file per game: the game, its factions, the source summary, the mechanics (patterns) and the real-world cases.

## The game
*Claude's summary (Oct 2026), from general knowledge of the game and the rules and terms under Mechanics; not from the sources below.*
- Genre: digital collectible card game (Riot, Legends of Runeterra, 2020); the Nexus has 20 health, 40-card decks of 2 regions, up to 6 champions.
- Mana: one gem per round up to 10; up to 3 unspent mana is kept as spell mana.
- Round: the attack token alternates; players take turns acting one at a time; attackers are blocked lane by lane.
- Spells: speeds Burst, Focus, Fast and Slow decide when they can be cast and answered.
- Board: up to 6 units. Champions level up when they meet a condition.
- Keywords: Elusive, Overwhelm, Quick Attack, Barrier, Challenger, Frostbite, Last Breath, Fleeting, Ephemeral, Lifesteal, Regeneration, Tough, Lurk, Plunder, Predict.
- Win: reduce the enemy Nexus to 0.

## The game's rules (from sources)
*Checked 29 Oct 2026 against Riot's support page https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/legends-of-runeterra/gameplay/round-faq.*
- Attack token: the player on attack acts first in a round; roles swap in the next round (round FAQ).
- Attacking: units are moved onto the battlefield and fight the defenders' blockers; an attacker that is not blocked strikes the
  opponent's Nexus (round FAQ). Win by bringing the enemy Nexus to 0.

## Factions

### Demacia
*Claude:* Knights: Rally, Barrier and strong followers.
Known for: strength in numbers, buffing allies, Barrier, Challenger, resilient units [1]; Barrier, Tough [2]; Rally, stat
efficiency, tempo [3][4].
Limited: direct damage and single-card removal [2].
Archetypes: board tempo and midrange [3].

### Freljord
*Claude:* Ice: Frostbite, Overwhelm and mana ramp.
Known for: Frostbite (only Freljord), Shatter, mana ramp, the biggest units, board control [1]; the only region with extra
mana gems, control [2]; Freeze, area board clears, mana acceleration [3].
Archetypes: control and combo [3].

### Ionia
*Claude:* Spirit and tricks: Elusive, recall and Nightfall.
Known for: Recall, Elusive, Stun, outmanoeuvring [1][2]; Deny, Lifesteal [3].
Archetypes: Barrier tempo and midrange [3].

### Noxus
*Claude:* Aggression: Overwhelm, Nexus damage and Quick Attack.
Known for: high-power units, aggression, many ways to deal damage, Overwhelm, damaging its own units for benefit, spell
damage [1]; Overwhelm, fast spells [2]; Fearsome, Stun [4]; burn finishers [3].
Weak at: defence [1]; card draw [3].
Archetypes: aggro, tempo, control [1][3].

### Piltover & Zaun
*Claude:* Invention: card generation, discard and Fleeting cards.
Known for: spells that make units, units that gain from spells, copying [1]; rewards for spells [2]; card generation, draw,
discounts, damage removal, burn [3].
Archetypes: spellslinger [1]; combo and fast-draw decks [3].

### Shadow Isles
*Claude:* Death: Last Breath, sacrifice, Ephemeral and Lifesteal.
Known for: Last Breath (the most), sacrifice, resurrection, Ephemeral [1][2]; Lifesteal, Drain, Fearsome [2]; removal,
Ruination [3].
Archetypes: sacrifice for benefit [1]; early tempo, control, heal-and-removal [3]; swarming the board [4].

### Bilgewater
*Claude:* Pirates: Plunder, the Deep and Lurk.
Known for: Toss and Deep [4]; Plunder, Vulnerable, Elusive, card draw, small removal [3].
Archetypes: Pirate Aggro, Scouts, Lurk [3].

### Targon
*Claude:* Celestial: Daybreak, Nightfall and big buffs.
Known for: Invoke, card generation, Hush, healing later [3].
Archetypes: decks that never run out of cards, Hush decks [3].

### Shurima
*Claude:* Desert: Predict, Reputation and Ascend.
Known for: Predict, Spellshield, counterspell, protection spells [3].
Archetypes: Sun Disk, explosive tempo [3].

### Bandle City
*Claude:* Yordles: Impact and tricks.
Known for: swarm, Attach, token generation, cards that replace themselves [3].
Archetypes: go-wide Yordle/Fae swarm, value-tempo [3].

## Combos
- Ashe + Rimetusk Shaman: Frostbite the strongest enemy each round [4]

- Towering Pairofant + Curious Changelings: surprise open-attack lethal [5]

- Ezreal + Timewinder / Bouncing Bomb: two Ezreal triggers [8]
- Vi + Ambush / Sumpworks Map: a sneaky strike to the nexus [8]
